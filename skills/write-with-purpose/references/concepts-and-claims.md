# Concepts and claims

Use this reference to resolve a particular concept, assertion state or claim-evidence relationship. Precision applies in accessible prose as well as formal documents; register can change while concept identity stays stable.

Select evidence for the requested use before deciding how much to say. Preserve the meaning of selected facts and enough context to avoid a consequential false inference. A summary may omit details; an omission becomes misleading when it changes the conclusion or the apparent scope of its support. A complete transcription, safety record or specified handoff can have different coverage requirements.

## Select concepts before substitutions

Establish what the passage refers to before simplifying its vocabulary. For a disputed or repeatedly used term, a compact record can identify the concept, definition, designation, easily confused concepts, relevant relations and source. Ordinary prose often supplies enough context.

Use the relevant project or disciplinary definition and its version. A glossary can support meaning without determining it universally. A source's authority concerns its actual scope. Preserve disagreements or unresolved mappings rather than forcing different concepts into one familiar word. A synonym in general usage can fail in a technical context.

## Engineering precision

Keep a requirement separate from its rationale, implementation proposal and verification evidence. Distinguish a measured result from a design target and a verified component from a validated overall use. For a project's modal convention, follow the adopted meanings of requirement, recommendation and prediction rather than substituting modals stylistically.

Bind each value to its entity, property, unit, time, comparator and operating condition where needed. Preserve thresholds and inclusive/exclusive boundaries. Scope a result to the tested range when it affects the conclusion. A model's simplifying assumptions and the real system it approximates remain distinct. When describing a proposed change, name the event or rule that would change and its intended consequence. Its prospective wording can establish that it is still a proposal.

## Clinical and research context

Preserve who a finding concerns, whether it is present, absent, suspected or unknown, when it applies, and who reported it. A current condition, a past condition and a family history are different assertions. A test ordered, collected, pending or resulted describes different states. Missing documentation does not establish that an action did not occur.

Use an appropriate diagnostic status or bind a value to its measurement conditions to carry the qualification directly. Supply an explanation when the receiving reader needs an inference beyond those facts. Select absent-measurement statements according to the handoff's purpose.

Use the supplied clinical or study definitions. Distinguish an observation from a diagnosis and an association from an established cause. Retain population, denominator, follow-up period, comparison and uncertainty needed to interpret a research result. Lack of a detected effect is not automatically evidence of equivalence. Draft within the authorized task; a language edit does not supply missing clinical evidence or treatment authority.

## Source fidelity and factual correctness

A faithful revision can preserve an incorrect source claim. Evaluate preservation separately from factual validation, and identify conflicts that matter to the requested result. When research is authorized, use sources appropriate to the claim. When the task is limited to supplied evidence, preserve that boundary.

Keep source facts, interpretations, hypotheses, proposals and illustrative scenarios distinguishable through their wording. Concrete details such as an actor, cause, date, sample size or success criterion need support. Clearly marked hypothetical material serves a different task from reporting observed facts. A proposed remedy should address the defect the evidence establishes. Introducing another defect changes the argument and needs support and a connection to the requested result.

## Check the relationships

Compare source and revision for actor/action/object, negation, modality, time, condition and entity/value bindings. Count checks can expose a difference without settling its meaning. A deterministic comparison of extracted records is only as valid as their extraction and declared coverage. Preserve unresolved and unavailable states when evidence cannot decide the issue.
