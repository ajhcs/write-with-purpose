# Structure, development and improvement

Use this reference for substantive planning, thought development or structural/expressive improvement. A narrow edit needs no outline, taxonomy or planning record. Infer settled choices from the brief and supplied material; ask only for information that materially blocks the work.

## Match depth to the request

Correction fixes the specified errors. Polish addresses the named clarity, flow or concision problem while preserving effective choices. Improvement actively considers a stronger thought, arrangement or expression, including in an adequate draft. New writing develops a contribution from the supplied ideas and evidence. These modes can overlap; the author's constraints govern particular passages. An explicit fixed order or quotation stays fixed, while a recommendation the author asks you to reconsider can change.

Examine the relevant whole before choosing an intervention. Seek a consequential opportunity rather than changing every sentence. A broader revision can be the first useful candidate; it need not follow a failed local repair. Conversely, an improvement request does not demand a rewrite when no alternative earns its tradeoff. Complete the selected revision and reread it as a whole; further work needs a particular expected benefit or unresolved issue.

## Develop a contribution without taking it over

Recover what the author observed, thinks, proposes or leaves open. A settled contribution needs development, not a questionnaire. With unsettled notes, consider genuinely different questions only when they help reveal the contribution; use the best-grounded route when intent is clear.

Distinguish three operations:

- Express an author-stated conclusion, correcting unsupported force when the task permits.
- Supply a supported connection needed to make that conclusion intelligible.
- Propose a new interpretation, marked as a hypothesis or question rather than attributed as the author's settled belief.

For requested stronger reasoning, test a consequential claim against a relevant alternative explanation, boundary case or objection. Identify what would change in the claim and what evidence could distinguish the readings. Model hypotheses are not exhaustive, equally plausible or necessarily what the author meant; neither may fit. Do not generate competing warrants merely because two sentences occur together. Seek the author's actual reasons first and continue independent editing while a material premise remains unresolved.

For example, increased backlog and a recommendation to hire two people do not alone establish the added capacity or headcount needed. If the brief permits reconsidering that recommendation, use supplied arrivals, closures and unknown capacity to develop a bounded decision; do not preserve an unsupported number as though it were an author fact. The lesson is to inspect the inference, not always to weaken recommendations or add caveats. Supported positive emphasis can remain.

## Make structural choices visible

For substantial planning or structural work, show **Family → Form → Section** at the useful level. Family groups shared conventions; form identifies the more specific reader expectation; section is a functional part and need not have a heading. Names and memberships may be provisional or mixed. Paragraphs can perform multiple functions. A platform label such as “blog” does not by itself determine the structure.

Choose a route for this contribution, reader and context. A decision memo can lead with a settled recommendation and its conditions; a reflective essay can develop a discovery without resolving it. An empirical report's Methods section can explain a research choice without becoming a reader procedure. A tutorial may need prerequisites and an observable result; a reference entry may need direct lookup rather than a scene.

Use functional blanks such as `[choice] / [reason] / [condition that changes the choice]` to draw out ideas. Do not import a source's findings, stock sentence stems or fixed sequence. A required template controls its actual required parts; otherwise preserve useful blended, recursive and unheaded structures.

The nine descriptive feature groups remain document length, section structure, section proportions, paragraph shape, sentence shape, word length, examples/evidence, presentation and communicative functions. Their observations are not quality scores. Keep **observed ranges**, **working targets** and **declared minimum/maximum limits** separate, each with source, scope and unit. A corpus extreme or median does not become the author's limit or target. Counts, clicks, citations, awards or perceived ease cannot substitute for the task's actual purpose.

## Compare the consequential change

An alternative structure must change an information dependency, the placement of a material qualification, or the intended experience. Renamed headings alone do not qualify. A sketch can locate the difference; write the decisive transition or representative passage to judge it, then carry a selected route into the requested writing. Two complete drafts are needed only when the tradeoff cannot otherwise be assessed or the author requests them.

Compare what the actual versions make recoverable, what they delay and what they might lose. Read a passage with its surroundings: what makes the next claim matter, or prepares the next event or turn? Moving or dividing material can change endorsement, timing or rhetorical force without changing the underlying facts. An unresolved reflection can become an apparent thesis; coordination can become an asserted sequence. Preserve supported relationships while pursuing a stronger arrangement. Choose independently when the brief settles the tradeoff; expose consequential unresolved intent rather than silently deciding a new belief.

Expression can improve through a new rhythm, a clearer connection or a useful image. Retain, transform or remove imagery according to its contextual effect and actual preservation requirements. Strangeness alone is not proof of voice; shorter sentences or a smaller diff do not prove improvement. Deliberate delay, repetition, humor and sound can earn their place. Allow “effect unclear”; do not fabricate a gain/loss rationale for every aesthetic choice.

## Finish the writing and teach only as wanted

Read the revised result as a standalone whole. Can the reader recover the intended distinction, reasoning or action, or follow the intended experience? Check sources separately for scope, attribution and certainty. Model comparison supplies provisional editorial judgment; author response and observed reader performance are different evidence.

If explanation or a learning workflow is requested, connect a real choice to the actual wording and its consequence. A countercase can clarify when the same move would be unhelpful. Follow the requested learning sequence. When writing or revision is requested, deliver it before optional practice. When attempt-first practice is requested, supply necessary raw facts and constraints in a form that leaves the chosen writing move to the author. Check that the setup itself has not completed that move, even if labelled as facts. For example, when practising replacement of vague praise with a specific result, “correct classifications: 80; evaluated cases: 100; comparator: none supplied” leaves the sentence to compose; “The model correctly classified 80 of 100 cases” already supplies it. Choose a useful presentation for the task; labels or bullets are not required. Let the author attempt before showing a solution. Deliver a requested worked solution directly. Neither accepting an edit nor repeating a phrase proves learning or transfer.
