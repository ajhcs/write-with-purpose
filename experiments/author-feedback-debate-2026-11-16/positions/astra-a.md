## A01 — Cole's enumeration preference, scoped and actionable

**Status:** Explicit author preference in R02, reinforced by R03; proposed implementation is a hypothesis. **Target:** Both `references/cole-lyons-style.md` files; replace the existing “Let a useful example develop the argument” bullet.

> **Let examples develop the argument.** Cole objects to prose lists of more than three items and to repeated lists within a paragraph. Treat three as a ceiling for ordinary illustrative series, not a target: even a shorter list can be filler. Select the example that advances the point, or explain the relationship the inventory was standing in for; do not merely spread the same inventory across sentences. Preserve required coverage and materially distinct reasons, reorganizing them when necessary rather than silently dropping them. This preference belongs to Cole's prose, not every author's voice.

**Boundary:** Lists required by a task remain complete. Distinct reasons may need development rather than deletion. No universal numeric checker or checklist log. Do not rewrite Doctorow's monetary functions into one representative example. Do not remove examples merely because the paragraph is long.

**Falsification:** On a fresh Cole paragraph with repeated illustrative series, a revision following this instruction should reduce felt filler without losing a needed reason; if it merely hides the inventory or Cole prefers its argument less, this implementation fails. A required-coverage countercase must keep all material distinctions.

## A02 — compare progression, not only local wording

**Status:** R11 supplies explicit overall-story evidence; R03 supplies explicit praise of a connecting reason. Proposed mechanism and generalization are hypotheses. **Target:** Both `references/structure-and-purpose.md` files, under “Compare the consequential change”; replace the paragraph beginning “Compare what the actual versions make recoverable”.

> Compare what the actual versions make recoverable, what they delay and what they might lose. Follow the developing thought: what makes the next claim matter, and what prepares the next event or turn? A locally smoother sentence can weaken that progression; a fuller connection can strengthen it. Read a revised passage with its unchanged surroundings. Moving or dividing the same material can change endorsement, timing or rhetorical force: an unresolved closing reflection can become an apparent opening thesis, or coordinated events can become an asserted sequence. Preserve those relationships while pursuing a stronger arrangement. When the original progresses better, retain its effective movement and repair the actual defects; this does not require retaining every sentence boundary or unusual phrase. Choose independently when the brief settles the tradeoff; expose consequential unresolved intent rather than silently deciding a new belief.

**Boundary:** No mandatory story arc, causal connective, two full drafts, or conservation of sentence lengths. R09 and R10 contradict those restrictions. “Enshittification kills” remains an author's claim; improving its cadence does not externally establish its causal truth. R03's explanatory speed relation can be made explicit without inventing new experience. Current permission for substantial restructuring must remain.

**Falsification:** Compare a fresh passage revision with its original and a defect-only repair, all embedded in identical surroundings. If a progression-directed revision invents chronology or makes the argument less compelling, reject it even when smoother. Include an explanatory technical passage to expose inappropriate narrative imposition.

## A03 — first-person claims and author-disputed qualifications

**Status:** R01 explicitly disputes an inherited disclaimer. R05 and R02 contain model-audited unsupported additions; I agree from the supplied text. No external biography was verified. **Target:** Both `references/structure-and-purpose.md` files, under “Develop a contribution without taking it over,” after the three-operation list.

> In first-person writing, small connecting phrases can add personal history: “first encountered,” “because of that,” or “as I worked on those systems” may assert an origin, cause or setting absent from the source. Make the supported connection without inventing that history. Treat a disclaimer as a claim too. If the author disputes an inherited disclaimer, follow the correction at its supported scope rather than preserving the disclaimer mechanically; do not infer a more specific credential or experience from the objection. Keep any independent material qualification that still applies. Ask only if the unresolved fact blocks the requested writing.

**Boundary:** This is a concrete application of existing source-fidelity instructions, not a demand for exhaustive autobiographical verification or defensive caveats. R06 shows that a stronger connection can be grounded in the full source. The task at generation time required preserving qualifications: R01 does not establish the generators should have silently removed the source disclaimer before the author's correction. “No surviving artifacts” and “not enrolled” are different assertions; deleting the latter need not erase the former.

**Falsification:** Supply a first-person passage where a report influenced the author but no first encounter is established; the revision must develop the connection without adding “first.” Pair it with a case in which an earlier qualification remains material after a separate author correction. Dropping that qualification or inventing a credential fails.

## What should not change

Keep the developmental ambition, selective whole-context reading, meaningful technical terms and freedom to transform imagery already present in the skills. Do not derive universal form routing from this trial. Do not reinterpret blank/Unsure responses as success. Keep source fidelity separate from preference. The checker already correctly treats style counts as advisory; do not add automatic list deletion or a maximum-three contract. Synchronizing shared editor references would be a consistency change, not demonstrated editor improvement.
