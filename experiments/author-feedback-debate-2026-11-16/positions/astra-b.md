## B01 — replace the Cole example/list bullet (recommend)

**Evidence:** explicit R02 feedback; R03 limits a naive threshold interpretation. Existing Cole guidance already says to remove inventories, but does not state the new author's threshold or repeated-list preference. General references already distinguish coverage from examples.

**Target:** both `skills/{write-with-purpose,edit-with-purpose}/references/cole-lyons-style.md`, replace the bullet headed “Let a useful example develop the argument.”

**Proposed text:**

> **Let a useful example develop the argument.** Cole explicitly dislikes prose lists of more than three items and multiple lists in a paragraph. For his prose, replace illustrative inventories with the relationship they support or develop the most useful example; a shorter list can still be filler. Preserve distinctions and coverage the task requires, explaining them in connected prose when useful. The wheelchair request shows how a warning can lose its meaning without inventorying every role or repeating the mechanism.

**Boundary:** this is Cole's prose preference, not a universal author rule, automatic comma counter, ban on necessary classifications, or permission to omit evidence. Do not turn four items into two adjacent two-item lists as compliance theater. User-required enumerations still govern.

**Falsification:** on fresh Cole passages, if the replacement merely hides inventories or removes distinct mechanisms, it fails even if counts fall. If Cole repeatedly prefers required connected enumerations over the attempted prose rewrite, narrow the operational guidance rather than forcing it.
