# Independent source and arithmetic audit

This is a bounded check of two retained source files in each of four formats, plus all uploaded-file hashes and medical passage hashes. It is not a full validation of the corpus, article identity, extraction completeness, or the appropriateness of a writing model. The machine-readable [snapshot](independent-audit.json) gives paths, hashes, and numerical checks. The numerical snapshot was refreshed after the XML and documentation owners regenerated their outputs; later reruns can change individual records.

All eight sampled source hashes matched their records. In each sampled record the sum of paragraph word counts and the sum of section words equaled the reported prose words. This checks internal arithmetic, not that the prose is the right text. Across the 17 uploaded files, both each original and its workspace copy matched the manifest SHA-256. All 22 selected passage hashes across the 11 medical reviews reproduced from the original PDFs using the stated page text and offsets. These checks establish byte and span identity, not the validity of the assigned paragraph functions.

Other limitations apply across the data: body and reference boundaries depend on source markup or heuristics; exact administrative heading variants can escape filters; titles and source publication types do not prove a form's suitability; and proximity, citations, and extracted text do not prove communicative function or evidence quality. The medical source reviewer identifies neighboring-article text on the first page of three uploads; generic PDF measurements cannot be treated as target-article-only until those boundaries are applied. Source-level verification of each record is still required before using its distributions as observed ranges or proposing targets.
