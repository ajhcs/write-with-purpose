# Independent aggregate audit

This audit independently recounts the parser receipts and spot-checks the resulting CSV and JSON. It does not validate all individual source extractions or certify candidate writing models.

The 4,375 inventory rows reconcile to 3,242 XML, 494 documentation, 559 HTML, and 80 PDF records. Of these, 4,104 have positive extracted prose words: 3,228 XML, 494 documentation, 303 HTML, and 79 PDF. This is a count of positive-prose source records, not distinct complete works or eligible profiles. Independent filesystem checks find 4,218 distinct extant source-path strings. This is path/copy grain, not a count of unique works or content hashes; unavailable and held records remain in the inventory. All profile eligibility values are false.

The prior screened-frame membership reconciles to 200 empirical/RCT, 200 MethodsX, and 300 systematic-review positive-prose XML records. Their output era cells sum respectively to 200, 200, and 300; an independent recomputation of each reported cell's sample size and median from the inventory found no discrepancy. These are selected discovery frames and exploratory metrics, not automatically verified forms, targets, or population estimates.

For XML, the script selects the earliest year among the XML-reported year and retained discovery-manifest year fields, preserves the set in `reported_years`, and flags disagreement. An independent comparison across all 3,242 XML rows found no discrepancy in the retained year sets or selection; the CSV records 165 disagreement flags; the selected year is not an independently established first-publication date. The reported era is derived from that selected year. Reviewed upload years use their review's publication-year claim; remaining metadata has different and sometimes unknown provenance.

**Identity finding and repair:** An earlier output incorrectly joined eight held HTML candidates with different essay titles because they shared the same `culture.org` response bytes. Shared response bytes establish the same retrieved content, not that eight named essays are one work. The aggregate owner corrected the link rule and regenerated the outputs. The eight candidates now have eight distinct linked IDs and appear in `response-collisions.json` as one response collision. The two remaining multi-record groups share, respectively, a repeated XML identifier/DOI and a repeated held HTML URL and title. All of these records are unmeasured, so 4,104 positive-prose records and the selected-cohort counts are unchanged. Links remain candidate identities; absence of a link does not prove distinct works.

The build does not use generic PDF `observed_first_two_pages_dois` for DOI identity. PDF DOI values are drawn from explicit upload reviews or a source record's `doi`, where supplied; reviewed DOIs themselves retain their stated confidence and limitations. The book *How Will You Measure Your Life?* is marked `excluded_book`; *Review of Particle Physics* is marked `split_composite_before_profile`. Neither is eligible for a profile. Generic PDF reading-order and article-boundary problems remain unresolved.
