# Medical uploads: source review

Eleven supplied PDFs were inspected as article sources: six NEJM Perspectives, one NEJM review article, three systematic reviews with meta-analysis, and one broader systematic review. The structured record contains each source hash, article identity, proposed family and form, print/PDF boundaries, outline, visual-material observations, and two selected passage annotations. The type shown in the source is distinguished from the proposed family and form. DOI for Hughes et al. is inferred from the filename PII and needs independent confirmation; the Greenhalgh DOI appears in the publisher download footer. The download dates are not publication or reprint dates; no distinct reprint year was established.

Three NEJM PDFs begin with the closing portion of a different article: **Dead Man Walking**, **Resisting the Suppression of Science**, and **The Other End of the Stethoscope**. The next article begins on that same page; a page-only filter would include the wrong source. The complete **Tragedy, Perseverance, and Chance** also shares its final printed page with the start of **The Other End of the Stethoscope** in the latter file. Preserve article-level boundaries and prevent duplicated or neighboring text from entering body metrics. Figure/table pages, acknowledgments, references, publisher notices, and external appendices also require separate treatment. Listed body page ranges are inclusive approximations; they do not imply every character on those pages belongs to the body. A scan of extracted page text and printed page sequences is not a complete visual or supplement audit.

The sampled Perspectives perform different work even with the same printed label. Stillman and Tailor move from cases to evidence and professional obligations; Obama argues from policy consequences; Rosenbaum uses an opening encounter to develop an argument; Joiner narrates family experience and reflects; Byrd and colleagues organize an account around a recall. The second Rosenbaum article integrates a patient's story, historical development and caveats. None should be automatically treated as an empirical research report. Camaschella's source explicitly says **Review Article**, defines related terms and proceeds thematically, rather than using the same structure as the systematic reviews.

The three meta-analyses each display a structured summary and methods, results and discussion, but their summaries and internal presentation differ. Greenhalgh and colleagues use a much longer review, definitions, a review-method phase sequence, research traditions and an explicit conceptual model. These are observed structures in individual sources, not mandatory templates or numerical writing norms. A methods description can **report** completed work; it does not automatically **instruct** the reader. A passage can combine functions, as the model description both reports its origin and qualifies its use.

For instructional blanks, ask for the contribution a writer intends: the event and its significance; a claim and its reason; the precise term and distinction; the review's scope and method; or a model's use and limits. These are optional questions, not prescribed role sequences. The annotations are two purposive selections per document, not estimates of document-wide role frequency or writing quality. They are a single reviewer's provisional interpretations; no second-reader agreement is claimed.

Mechanically checkable: PDF hashes, cached page text, page numbers, end-exclusive Unicode character offsets, exact span hashes, and declared section references. Editorial judgment remains necessary for meaningful body boundaries, paragraph boundaries, function assignments and their relationships, article completeness, source entailment, and whether a structure fits a new writer's task. Extracted PDF text can contain column-order artifacts, hyphenation, ligatures and headers; inspect visually before using it for fine-grained measurements. Exact sampled text resides only in the private workspace cache, not in this repository.

The DOI attribution was checked at each article's printed ending, not from its filename. In **NEJMp1312793.pdf**, page 1 ends “Expediting Drug Development” (DOI `10.1056/NEJMp1311439`), while page 2 ends the target “Dead Man Walking” (`10.1056/NEJMp1312793`). In **NEJMp1702362.pdf**, page 1 ends “Preventive Medicine for the Planet and Its Peoples” (`10.1056/NEJMp1702378`), while page 3 ends the target “Resisting the Suppression of Science” (`10.1056/NEJMp1702362`) and then begins “Beyond Genes and Molecules”; its DOI is not visible in this PDF. In **NEJMp1707514.pdf**, page 1 ends “Tragedy, Perseverance, and Chance” (`10.1056/NEJMp1711886`), while page 3 ends the target “The Other End of the Stethoscope” (`10.1056/NEJMp1707514`). The independently supplied **NEJMp1711886.pdf** prints the former target's DOI on page 3 before beginning the latter article. The JSON records the page and character positions of these identity markers. Passage functions now use the catalogue IDs; the earlier descriptive labels are retained alongside them.
