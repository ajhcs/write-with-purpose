## Preliminary counterpattern

In *Managing Oneself*, the visible heading sequence includes “What Are My Strengths?” (PDF p. 3), “How Do I Perform?” (p. 5), “What Are My Values?” (p. 6), “Where Do I Belong?” and “What Should I Contribute?” (p. 8), “Responsibility for Relationships” (p. 9), and “The Second Half of Your Life” (p. 10). Several sections develop questions using examples, explanation, and advice, but they are not a repeated set of named component definitions. The opening on p. 3 moves from changing career responsibility to the need for self-management; no separate research-basis heading appears before the questions. The ending on p. 12 makes a broad social-change claim rather than an explicit qualification of the central recommendation. These are preliminary structural observations, not results of completed all-paragraph annotation, nor evidence that other HBR essays follow this structure.

The original extraction interleaves columns when globally sorted by vertical position. In particular, the last paragraph of *Customers as Innovators* is in the right column of p. 11; reading the left column last falsely makes the article appear truncated. Private, page-indexed PyMuPDF blocks with bounding boxes, raw text and per-block hashes are stored at `/workspace/repos/builderbob/.writing-corpus-cache/pattern-test/hbr/`. These are an inventory aid, not normalized, validated paragraphs: individual paragraphs cross columns/pages, blocks merge headings or dialogue, and sidebars, summaries, exhibits, headers and footers remain. No word, section, paragraph or sentence metrics are reported until source-ordered clean bodies are validated. No full texts are included in this report.
