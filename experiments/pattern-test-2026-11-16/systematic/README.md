# Systematic-review scaffold test

Eight selected articles were read and coded throughout (287 prose paragraphs). All eight were judged eligible as systematic evidence reviews after reading their body; none was identified as a protocol or solely narrative review. No records were replaced. Interpretations are by one analyst and await independent review; the measurements describe this bounded sample and are not targets, limits, or an agreed genre profile.

## Boundaries and coding

`extract.py` records body paragraph order, section parent and printed heading, source XML element path, normalized private text offsets (zero-based Unicode code points, end-exclusive), XML and normalized-cache SHA-256 hashes, and individual normalized span hashes. It excludes title, abstract and back matter, administrative sections, table/figure/box/supplement content, nested lists and list paragraphs. Body prose remains in order, including unheaded prose. This is a normalized text representation: inline markup is flattened and whitespace collapsed; it is not byte offsets into raw XML. XML text in the private cache is copied from the previously acquired local sources with matching hashes. No network acquisition was performed. Tables, captions and nested lists are outside these prose counts; where a paper's substantive evidence is in tables or boxes, prose-only findings necessarily exclude it. The boundary logic and role assignments remain subject to independent review.

The eight `*-coding.json` files preserve all extracted main-body prose paragraphs, with the source's section ID and heading distinct from the analyst's role, a short reason for function labels and a single-coder status. Multiple catalog functions may coexist. The corresponding `*-metrics.json` files use a shared corpus-analysis `metrics.py` `word_tokens` rule; counts may include citation artifacts. The role totals sum over paragraphs; function counts overlap and cannot be added into a word budget. Paragraph-length extrema are observed descriptions, never minimum or maximum requirements. `aggregate.json` reports per-document direct-prose section counts, analyst-role totals, and overlapping function-conditioned paragraph lengths.

## Provisional observations from the eight read examples

- Both report actual search and selection actions as `report`, not `instruct`; neither provides an executable instruction to the reader merely by describing its methods.
- `xml-02282` combines its context, contested directionality, evidence gap and review purpose in one introduction paragraph. Its Results explicitly reports heterogeneous estimates and uncertainty, and its Discussion includes comparison, limitations and implications.
- `xml-02359` reports a one-database search and snowballing, selection and classification, but its body does not clearly specify extraction personnel or detailed extraction procedure. It acknowledges that formal quality assessment was not performed. Study count first appears in Methods; results and limitations are not confined to the expected heading sequence. Its conclusion explicitly limits causal inference from associations. These absences and placements should remain visible in the scaffold test, not be filled in by assumption.
- `xml-02334` reports that overlapping outcome domains precluded mutually exclusive categories in Methods and ends with an unheaded concluding discussion rather than a separate Conclusion section. It reports eligibility criteria but no distinct formal risk-of-bias tool in the body.
- `xml-02298` examines quality of reporting of validations; this should not be confused with a distinct formal risk-of-bias tool for the review. Its conclusion includes future opportunities and an unresolved issue.
- `xml-02415` has an unheaded introduction and makes risk of bias explicit in Results. It refers readers to a protocol for actual search sources and full construct; these details are not in the body. A planned meta-analysis was infeasible and another post hoc pooled analysis was characterized as experimental.
- `xml-02317` reports no formal quality rating and leaves its closing synthesis unheaded. Its assertion that all studies in one subgroup were prognostic is in tension with the immediately preceding description of an adjusted null result; retain the source contradiction.
- `xml-02382` explains in Methods why meta-analysis was not performed. Two paragraphs introduce lists excluded from this prose-only scope; their listed content is outside the measured body.
- `xml-02448` reports a change in children’s inclusion criteria in Results and revisits it in Discussion; a decision and its implications can therefore be distributed across sections.

## Observed measurements and scaffold assessment

The scoped, normalized body is 2,681–7,203 shared-tokenizer words per review (median 4,078; n=8); paragraphs per review 22–50 (median 35.5). Across 287 paragraphs pooled, paragraph length is 13–453 words (median 108). Source section IDs with direct prose, counting the unheaded introduction as one group, range 6–25 per review (median 11); nested source sections are not rolled up into their parents. Role-level word totals per review, including zero where no standalone analyst-role section was assigned, are Introduction 192–1,261, Methods 305–1,465, Results 286–3,601, Discussion 659–1,958 and Conclusion 0–279. In two articles the conclusion is an unheaded final Discussion paragraph, so zero is a structural coding choice, not an absence of closing meaning. Counts are sensitive to normalized XML, omitted lists/tables, citations and section-role judgments.

`aggregate.json` gives all 12 catalog functions, document/section counts and paragraph length distributions. For example, the 218 paragraphs marked report span 13–453 words; 116 marked qualify span 17–406. These subsets overlap because paragraphs can have several functions: their counts and lengths are not additive. No paragraph was marked instruct or narrate; that is a coding result for this sample, not proof either function is impossible. Exact counts remain subject to a second coder.

The proposed scaffold is useful as a set of questions, not a required sequence. All eight state a review purpose, identify evidence through search and selection, describe synthesis, report findings, discuss limitations and offer closing implications. Extraction detail and formal appraisal vary: `xml-02359` and `xml-02317` explicitly lack a formal quality assessment or rating and do not describe extraction personnel/procedure in the body; `xml-02415` sends readers to a protocol for search sources and terms. `xml-02334` does not identify a separate formal bias tool in the body. Thus missing details and actual choices must remain visible, not supplied from the template. In `xml-02359`, the initial count is in Methods; `xml-02334` explains overlapping outcome categories in Methods; `xml-02415` locates substantial uncertainty in Results; and `xml-02448` reports revised child selection criteria in Results and Discussion. `xml-02334` and `xml-02317` conclude inside Discussion without a separate conclusion heading; `xml-02415` opens without an introduction heading.
