WRITING BLANKS AFTER THE FIRST HOLDOUT PILOT

Status: candidate patterns, not adopted profiles.
Each block is a contribution, not a required paragraph or printed heading.
Observed ranges are in results/statistics.json and need their cohort context.
Working targets and declared limits: unset.

ARGUMENT / PERSPECTIVE

TITLE: [The question or tension I want the reader to notice]
OPENING — CHOOSE A ROUTE:
  [My position and the situation that makes it matter]
  OR [A problem the reader needs to recognize]
  OR [An encounter that motivates the argument]
DEVELOPMENT: [My reasons and relevant evidence]
CONNECTION: [How that material supports what I think]
QUALIFICATIONS, WHERE THEY APPLY: [Which claim changes under which conditions]
IMPLICATION: [What follows; a recommendation only if that is the purpose]
CLOSING: [The consequence, unresolved question or return that the argument earns]

PRACTITIONER ARGUMENT

TITLE: [The professional judgment I want to help someone make]
OPENING: [The practical problem and my position]
ORGANIZATION — CHOOSE A ROUTE:
  [Questions the reader needs to answer]
  OR [Components or processes that organize my proposed approach]
  OR [A case whose development reveals the approach]
FOR EACH LOCAL SECTION, CHOOSE WHAT THE READER NEEDS:
  [A distinction or definition]
  [An observed, sourced or explicitly hypothetical example]
  [The explanation connecting that example to my point]
  [What the reader could recognize or do]
  [The condition or exception relevant here]
Place research or experiential basis where it supports the point.
CLOSING: [The judgment, implication or remaining uncertainty]

SYSTEMATIC REVIEW

TITLE AND ABSTRACT: [Accurate question, scope, design and completed-report summary]
INTRODUCTION: [Need, gap and exact review question]
METHODS: [Actual search, eligibility, selection, extraction, appraisal,
          synthesis and relevant assumptions; identify external details]
RESULTS: [Selection, evidence characteristics, findings, uncertainty and differences]
DISCUSSION: [Interpretation, comparison, strengths, limits and implications]
CONCLUDING ANSWER: [What the findings justify; separate heading if appropriate]

Report missing or unperformed work accurately. A common omission in reference
papers does not remove a research or reporting requirement for the new review.
