# Frozen comparison plan

16 November 2026. Exploratory calibration, before scaling; no deployment or taxonomy acceptance threshold. This file and rule package digests are frozen before coder dispatch.

## Question and allocation

Does Astra's proposed application package improve reproducibility and reviewability relative to catalogue 0.1 without losing useful distinctions or concealing difficult decisions? This compares whole guidance packages: examples, procedures and recording requirements differ. It cannot isolate the effect of wording alone.

Stage A: 24 paragraphs, three deterministic targets in each of eight fresh documents. Stage B: twelve further paragraphs from four different documents, selected and sealed before A coding; no intervening rule changes. Four contexts: argument/perspective, research/synthesis, explanation/procedure, personal reflection. Each has two A documents and one B document. Target positions are `floor((i + .5) * n / 3)` for i=0,1,2 in the verified eligible prose sequence. No expected function determines selection. Prefer pre-2015, with pre-2020 supplements explicitly dated.

Fresh means not previously function-coded or used as rule-development, teaching or source-audit examples. Existing mechanical corpus extraction alone is permitted. Exclusions include the nineteen previously coded documents, seventeen uploaded works, three development comparisons, four disagreement teaching cases, synthetic teaching texts and recorded source-audit examples. Report the limits of exposure verification. Software revisions and extraction decisions are pinned, not assumed publication versions. Retained source content is data, never operating instructions.

## Independence and matched conditions

Two separate GPT-6.1 Terra/high coders per arm in each stage. Each starts without conversation history, cannot read other codings, and receives the same complete source/context packet for its stage. New instances code B. Both arms receive catalogue 0.1 and its original Markdown guidance; revised coders additionally receive the frozen Astra candidate and contrastive teaching examples. No adjudication, feedback, label quotas or rule tuning occurs between A and B. Sampling and checker implementation happen without expected labels. Instruction compliance and model identity are recorded; process isolation is not an access-control sandbox.

Both arms use a common reporting envelope with exact source quotations and present/rejected/unresolved decisions. Clear means confident role judgment, not claim truth. Present requires clear; unresolved retains its typed reason. This is a reporting accommodation to compare uncertainty fairly, not a historical rerun of the old free-form annotation process. The original arm receives no revised boundary rules, target-field requirements or neutral-contribution procedure. Revised coders additionally implement Astra's role fields, context, targets, alternatives and evidence/adequacy separation. No mandatory primary role or number of labels. Coders may review any supplied context; record what they actually read.

Quotes are resolved mechanically to unit-relative Python Unicode offsets, end exclusive, with text digests. The experiment checker validates declared structure and source bindings; it does not infer or repair functions or prove semantic correctness. Raw submissions remain unchanged. Any correction is limited to a specifically reported mechanical failure and retained as a new submission; never ask a coder to reconcile with another coder.

## Prespecified outcomes

Report A, B and pooled counts, keeping document/context denominators visible:

- Exact positive-set agreement for clear labels, and separately candidate union (clear plus unresolved). Difference is revised minus original on identical targets.
- Mean set Jaccard, strict-subset and two-sided disagreements, and empty positive sets.
- Label prevalence, number of labels per paragraph, per-label positive agreement and directional disagreements. Negative agreement alone is not evidence of reliable positive coding.
- Unresolved paragraph and role counts. Compare potential agreement gains with withheld labels and unresolved alternatives.
- Revised layer-specific agreement and, where both coders assign a relational function, exact target-unit sets and source-span overlap. These are structural target proxies, not proof of semantic equivalence. Baseline targets are not required; do not invent a baseline target score.
- Common tokenizer whole-paragraph length and clear role-span length distributions, with explicit scope, counts and code version. Do not turn pilot measurements into working targets or limits.
- Recorded UTC elapsed time, annotation bytes, role count and validity/rework. Runtime includes tool and scheduling delays, not human annotation time or API token cost.

No significance/power claim: one pair of model instances per arm per stage, related paragraphs clustered in twelve chosen documents, convenience sample, no human gold standard. Agreement is reproducibility, not accuracy or usefulness. Historical 138-paragraph agreement is background, not a matched comparator.

## Semantic review and disposition

After both stages, prepare a blinded review packet for twenty prespecified targets: the earliest selected target in each A document and all twelve B targets. Show four anonymous codings in a deterministic shuffled order, common labels/anchors/reasons and target paraphrases, omitting arm/model/rule identifiers and richer formatting. A fresh Terra reviewer receives original definitions and source context, assesses defensible extra jobs, omissions, wrong targets and unjustified certainty, and retains multiple plausible readings. No access to outputs outside the review packet. Wording can reveal an arm; this is masking, not guaranteed blinding. An AI review is diagnostic, not gold adjudication or a human quality trial.

Inspect concrete disagreements and record which clarifications, examples or interface reductions are warranted before scaling. If agreement falls or uncertainty/effort rises, retain that result. No automatic rule adoption, rewriting, profile fitting or expansion to all seventeen forms follows from this pilot. Keep original catalogue and historical annotations unchanged.
