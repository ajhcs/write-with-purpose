# Masked semantic diagnostic review

This independent AI review is diagnostic, not a gold standard. The four records were anonymous and shuffled separately for each target; differences in wording and detail could still reveal aspects of the guidance. The review did not identify arms or inspect agreement results. It assesses the work the supplied prose plausibly performs, not the truth or quality of its underlying evidence.

- **LF-d23d38c43ea7-p005:** The transition to the thesis and the position itself are distinct. An orientation target should identify the immediate shift accurately even when later analysis is relevant.
- **LF-80bd6f30b443-p006:** Reflection and a concrete behavior can coexist. The statement that something was never learned does not, by itself, establish an event developing through time; retain the irony of the reported reaction.
- **PMC2867979-p011:** A specific review result can illustrate and support an association at once, and a reported comparison has its own dimension. The paper's exclusion of design bias limits its scope, not the reported association.

- **LF-03f718ef8cc6-p021:** A quoted instruction to the narrator, a general second-person explanation, and a reader-directed instruction are different plausible targets. The unresolved instruction reading is reasonable in this narrative context.
- **PMC4566873-p028:** Screening procedure, operational categories and the rationale for the endpoint perform different jobs. A single category anchor does not substantiate a reason claiming coverage of all four definitions.
- **curl-p04:** A concise account of what a response code communicates can reasonably function as a partial definition or an explanation; a full technical definition should not be required.

Across cases, examples should teach how to identify a distinct contribution, its target, and supporting span without a quota or mandatory primary label. Preserve reported speakers, conditional wording and the scope of claims. A concrete example may also support a claim; an analogy may illuminate a relationship without establishing it empirically. Neighboring context can clarify a function without converting that neighboring text into part of the target paragraph.
