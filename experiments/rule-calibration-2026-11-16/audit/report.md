# Independent calibration audit

16 November 2026. This is a diagnostic pilot audit, not a quality certification or a test of label accuracy. The checks in `checks.json` and `check.py` are independent of the experiment's normalizer and metrics functions. They are tied to the comparison digest recorded there.

## Results and arithmetic

The selected raw roles independently reproduce the stage-level clear and candidate-union exact, strict-subset, two-sided and empty-set counts, and mean Jaccard. Clear exact counts are A original 13/24 and revised 12/24; B original 8/12 and revised 6/12. Pooling those independent counts gives 21/36 and 18/36, matching the result. Candidate-union exact counts are A 12/24 versus 12/24 and B 7/12 versus 6/12, pooled 19/36 versus 18/36. These comparisons concern agreement between the two instances in each arm; they do not establish accuracy, usefulness, or whether either interpretation is correct. The two empty sets conventionally have Jaccard 1; none occurs in these data. The three disagreement categories and exact sets partition each comparison. Independently expanding target quotations into Unicode character-position sets reproduces all nine reported target unit-set matches and span IoUs. They are structural overlap proxies, not target correctness.

The per-label positive-agreement formula `2*both_positive/(2*both_positive+coder1_only+coder2_only)` excludes shared negatives and is appropriate for its stated positive agreement. The metric review does not independently verify every secondary table or semantic interpretation. The reported role-span lengths are lengths of within-paragraph *evidence anchors*, which can be short diagnostic quotes; they are not established complete function-realization boundaries or valid function-length ranges. Estimating the latter would require distinct, consistently collected realization spans and evidence anchors.

## Sample and provenance

All 12 documents, 36 targets, eight A and four B documents, and two A/one B document per context match the plan. All 36 sampled IDs match the deterministic positions in their registered eligible sequences; every registered paragraph digest and packet paragraph binding matches its body slice. Current receipt, source, body, packet, selected and retained raw-submission hashes match their recorded digests. The two arms in each stage are assigned the same sealed packet in the dispatch record. These hash checks establish byte consistency, not authenticity or independent publication provenance. Source dates for essays and reviews derive from the existing inventory and are explicitly provisional; software names and commit IDs are recorded, but the source bytes and claimed commits were not independently downloaded or authenticated for this audit.

I directly inspected a target in each stratum against the retained underlying markup or software source, including its immediate context: `LF-d23d38c43ea7-p005` (argument) and `LF-fca9142fd337-p006` (personal reflection) match individual HTML `p` elements; `PMC2867979-p011` (research) matches one JATS body `p`, including the preceding section heading and adjacent paragraphs; `git-p02` (procedure) matches the blank-line-delimited source paragraph after joining wrapped lines, with preceding and following paragraphs in order. Normalized text, not identical raw byte layout, is the comparison. The personal reflection's next HTML paragraph contains an empty intervening `p`; the extraction includes the next substantive paragraph, with a visible `H e` drop-cap artifact. These four checks do not verify every source boundary or all extraction choices. The packet supplies the full reconstructed body plus nearby units; it does not necessarily preserve the source's complete publication, visual typography, figures or citations in original layout.

No selected source hash matches the 19 listed previously coded works or 17 attachment hashes, and no selected title matches the prior-coded titles; selected IDs do not exactly match the named explicit exposures. This is bounded evidence: PDF and HTML representations of one work have different hashes, the attachment registry offers filenames rather than uniformly normalized work titles, and the three development examples and some exclusions are described generically. Exact matching cannot prove no prior exposure or cross-format duplicate. The selection is convenience sampling, not a random or representative sample.

## Checker and process limits

The frozen structural checker resolves exact quotations and validates source bindings and designated fields, but does not validate the meaning, truth, completeness or correctness of a role. It does not require or validate revised role `layer`; all selected revised roles nonetheless supplied the expected layer in this sample. Its rule that a given paragraph/function may have only one decision type can reject legitimate mixed decisions for distinct instances; no selected record contains mixed decisions, so no affected selected record was identified. Neither absence of recorded violations nor a checker pass rules out cases the interface suppressed before submission.

A-revised-2's preserved `raw.json` and selected `raw-v2.json` differ only in `reviewed_units` and `context_reason`, across 24 annotations; roles and anchors are unchanged. The existing repair log describes these as context bookkeeping. Both retained files pass the current frozen checker, so those files alone do not establish a checker failure that required the repair. The submitted start and finish times remain identical across both versions and exclude subsequent repair. All elapsed values are coder-recorded drafting intervals, not independently timed end-to-end workloads; tool/scheduling effects are uncertain. Bytes and compact characters measure recorded artifact size, not tokens, cognitive burden, cost, or time.

The sample was sealed at 16:23:28 UTC and interface receipt at 16:23:39, before reported coder starts. Reported B starts follow reported A finishes; these are self-recorded times rather than independent dispatch evidence. The dispatch file was recorded at 16:28:01, after reported starts, so it is retrospective allocation metadata. The parent reports separate fresh-context dispatches in its tool transcript; shared filesystem instructions are not a technical access-control sandbox and cannot prove coders did not see other material. The blind-review builder masks identifiers and shuffles four records per target but distinctive target detail and wording may reveal an arm. An AI semantic review is diagnostic and is neither a gold standard nor a human quality trial.
