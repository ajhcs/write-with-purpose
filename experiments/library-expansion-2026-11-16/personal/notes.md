# Personal and public writing: retrieval selections

Twelve individual works, all originally published by 2014, supply three provisional targets in each of four applicable forms. Each is selected by an identified editorial list or award; these signals do not establish readership or prose quality. We have not downloaded article bodies or established completeness. Form assignments are retrieval hypotheses awaiting full-text review. Science stories combine explanatory passages with narrative reporting; their fit as conceptual explainers is provisional, particularly Hughes's case-centered story. They should not be treated as an established pure explainer cohort.

Other overlap remains possible; deduplicate against the complete workspace inventory before acquisition. An ASME portfolio citation describes its portfolio; it does not independently award each constituent piece.

Tutorial, procedure, and reference are conditional analogues for service journalism, not automatically personal essays. Institutional proposals and decision memos can be public but were not established as comparable to this literary stratum. Formal study reports, methods, theory, case reports and reviews belong to disciplinary research contexts; neither a personal narrative nor research about expressive writing should be substituted for those forms. A public reported feature may share explanatory functions without meeting the conceptual-explainer definition. These boundaries are scope decisions, not claims that other examples can never exist.
