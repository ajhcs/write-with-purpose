# Medicine and health: library-retrieval candidates

The 17-cell file distinguishes research and literary forms and retains conditional analogues. This is a targeted discovery exercise, not an exhaustive or systematic search, and is intended to provide texts for writing analysis, not clinical guidance. Citation counts are Europe PMC cited-by counts at retrieval, not normalized for field, age, or article type; they do not measure literary quality. The JAMA physician narratives were included for source genre and venue; none has established individual prominence, and they are tagged verified_relevant_no_prominence_evidence. They do not independently satisfy the highly cited, widely read, or notable criterion.

Searches and structured responses are retained in `search-receipts.json` and `review-protocol-receipt.json`; `pubmed-efetch.xml` and `pubmed-receipt.json` retain the PubMed batch response and request URL/time. For the original 49 candidates PubMed bibliographic titles and DOI identifiers were compared to Europe PMC; two additional reviews were checked against Europe PMC and the publisher because no PubMed PMID was present; publication year and citation count are from Europe PMC. PubMed and Europe PMC agreement is metadata corroboration, not independent Crossref DOI resolution. Crossref was not queried. Dates of online and print publication may differ. Names are the PubMed author-list names, including collective authors. No full-text copies were downloaded. `copy_status` compares DOI against the provided document inventory; it cannot establish whether an uncatalogued library copy exists.

The three publisher-classified case reports are [Okuma et al.](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1752-1947-5-111), [Reeves et al.](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1752-1947-2-139), and [Exley and Vickers](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1752-1947-8-41). Each publisher page says Case report and provides a case-presentation abstract. They should not be treated as proof of causal claims. JAMA identifies [the physician narratives](https://jamanetwork.com/collections/5766/narrative-medicine?fl_Categories=Narrative+Medicine&fl_ContentType=Article&page=59) as its A Piece of My Mind essays; the individual pages are recorded in candidate sources. The [FASEB Journal work review](https://faseb.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1096/fj.14-0901ufm) is one review of a specific book, not the book itself. Its Europe PMC cited-by number does not necessarily indicate readers or the quality of its prose.

The completed systematic reviews disclose search and selection methods in their abstracts. The scoping papers describe mapping broad literature, and the narrative papers synthesize topics, including one whose abstract explicitly says narrative review and synthesis. The protocol cell contains two study designs and one review design: its final entry is the latter. For individual cases and all other candidates, inspect the actual whole article before formally coding it. Historical guidelines and results may be outdated.

Reference-entry candidates are standalone clinical topic reviews offered as consultation-oriented analogues, not verified encyclopedia entries. Proposal candidates are proposals for classifications or definitions, not funding or project proposals. The recommendation statements are public evidence-to-recommendation analogues, not internal decision memos. These distinctions are carried in `coverage.json` and the individual form status.

Two further reviews complete the specific-work review cell: [Webster on Toxoplasmosis of Animals and Humans](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1756-3305-3-112) and [Walker on Ticks: Biology, Disease and Control](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1756-3305-2-1). The publisher explicitly labels both Book review and assigns each DOI to the review. Publisher pages displayed 28k and 9,246 accesses respectively when inspected; the first is rounded, neither is a count of unique readers, and the figures do not establish quality. Live Europe PMC metadata and request receipts are in `additional-review-receipts.json`. Both were absent from the supplied document inventory by DOI, although prior derived critical-theory records exist and should be deduplicated separately. Formal prose coding and assessment of the complete text are pending.

The three conditional analogue cells remain conditional; they are not counted as three direct examples of reference entries, project proposals, or internal decision memos. The publisher abstracts and metadata establish provisional form, not suitability as a writing exemplar.
