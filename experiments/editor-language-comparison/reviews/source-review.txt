Scope: exactly 30 supplied input files, five named files in each of cases 01-06. No protocol, mapping, editor notes, other experiment files, skill instructions, memory or web sources were read. No delegation. No sample edits.
Both anonymous samples were assessed against the actual brief and source ledger. No editor/condition/original identity was inferred, no usefulness ranking was made, and length was not treated as quality.

METHOD AND SHARED QUALIFICATIONS
Independently computed SHA-256 over the exact raw bytes using .NET SHA256. Independently counted maximal non-whitespace runs using regex \S+. Parsed the supplied mechanical.json only to compare its stated words and hashes with these independent values.
Whitespace-delimited token counts are descriptive and include punctuation/citation tokens as written; they are not a lexical word-count rubric or quality score.
Brief fit and source correctness are reported separately. Direct contradictions, unsupported claims, plausible inferences, optional wording and justified selection are distinguished.
fictional provenance: All six source ledgers explicitly describe fictional material. This audit treats every person, scene, study and specification as fictional and makes no claim about the user.
fiction label in samples: A.txt and B.txt omit the editorial fictional wrapper. That omission is not by itself evidence that a sample asserts real events or facts about the user; clean fictional prose can rely on its enclosing context. The fictional provenance should accompany any standalone reuse.
change record: Every brief requests clean edited prose plus a short change record. None of the twelve supplied A/B text files contains a change record. This is a shared as-supplied deliverable omission; separate editor records, if any, are outside the authorized inputs and were not inspected.
original preservation and voice: The permitted source.txt files are fact ledgers, not identified original draft files. The masked inputs do not establish which sample, if any, is the original. Original-file preservation, edit size relative to that draft and exact preservation of its authorial wording cannot be verified. Requested register and scene voice can still be assessed.
selection rule: A source fact need not be restated when the brief does not require it and its omission does not distort the selected account. Word count is descriptive only; no reduction target or usefulness ranking is imposed.
Severity: material = central contradiction/operating-condition error; moderate = substantive unsupported claim or evidence attribution; minor = local scope, precision, added-detail or brief-fit issue; contextual = delivery/provenance qualification.
