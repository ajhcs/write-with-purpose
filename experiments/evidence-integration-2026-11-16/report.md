# What the eighteen studies change for our writing harness

The strongest opportunity is to help the author develop an original thought, choose a useful structure, and make a small number of well-grounded revisions for a particular reader. The studies support conditional guidance tied to purpose, form, reader and evidence. Our own experiments argue for keeping detailed annotation available without making it a prerequisite for routine writing.

Seven Terra reviewers read all eighteen works, with an independent Terra audit and the requested Astra strategy review. The supplied PDFs total 247 physical pages. One partly corrupt attachment was supplemented with a complete official author copy; originals, extraction paths and hashes are retained. [All eighteen papers](paper-index.md), [structured evidence](evidence-map.json), [twelve integrated proposals](rule-decisions.md), [Astra's strategy](reviews/astra-advantage.md), and the [independent audit](reviews/independent-audit.md) are saved separately.

The batch contains different kinds of evidence: a headline meta-analysis, a systematic methodological review, randomized comparisons, observational/predictive studies, descriptive genre research and an editorial with an empirical analysis. It is not eighteen independent meta-analyses. Several studies contain multiple designs. We did not pool them into a universal writing effect.

## The most useful findings

### Reader knowledge matters, and a readability score cannot settle it

Shulman et al. (P01) randomized 650 participants to jargon and optional-definition conditions across three short science topics. Perceived processing fluency was lower with jargon, 4.57 versus 5.27. Offering optional mouseover definitions did not produce a significant fluency effect in that experiment. This does not show that inline explanations are ineffective or that necessary technical terms should disappear. The unadjusted engagement outcomes were not significantly different; modeled indirect pathways should not be restated as a demonstrated total effect on engagement. [Audience review](reviews/audience-readability.md), P01 PDF pp. 7–10, 14–15.

Pancer et al. (P05) and Ryba et al. (P09) add evidence about processing ease, engagement and understanding, with different tasks and populations. P09 tested bundled writing changes, so its result cannot establish that each ingredient—such as reducing hedges—caused the benefit. Its raw understanding results are not monotonic across style categories, and its modeled effect has a different basis. The audit checked the relevant table visually. We should separate perceived ease, confidence, correct answers and engagement rather than collapse them into one score.

**Consequence:** make assumed knowledge, necessary vocabulary and the point of explanation visible. Borrow glossary and first-use checks, then ask whether this reader can recover the relationship. Preserve exact terminology when it carries the needed distinction. This refines implementation of our existing skills rather than replacing their language principles.

### Common style maxims are weak candidates for automatic enforcement

Weinberger et al. (P14), *Ten Simple (Empirical) Rules for Writing Science*, is an editorial with observational citation models. Its now-retrieved supporting methods describe 1,065,139 retained Scopus category records, overlapping journal categories, selection filters and separate one-feature models of log(citations + 1). Citation associations differ from tests of reader understanding and from experiments that change a writing feature. Its contrary-to-common-advice findings do not justify deliberately making writing longer, rarer or more promotional. [Rules review](reviews/agreement-writing-rules.md), including the five-page supplement.

The grant studies also qualify simplistic deletion rules. Qiu et al. (P10) found adjusted promotional-word associations with funding in two application populations. Van den Besselaar and Mom (P11) report section-, stage- and model-dependent relationships, including nonlinear terms. Neither establishes that adding particular words causes funding success. Tentative-word counts also do not measure whether a qualification is scientifically warranted. [Funding review](reviews/funding-persuasion.md).

**Consequence:** preserve supported positive emphasis, useful modifiers, technical terms and uncertainty. Flag a change in meaning or claim strength for review; do not punish a word merely because it belongs to a stylistic category. Enforce an actual word ceiling when the author or destination sets one. Keep other measurements descriptive.

### Attention and influence are real outcomes with limited transfer

P04, P07 and P13 analyze the historical Upworthy archive. They are related analyses of overlapping source material, not three independent corpus replications. Headline packages were randomized; their individual linguistic features were not separately randomized in every reported analysis. P07's held-out winner-prediction accuracy was 54.42% on its selected paired task. P04's concreteness findings are conditional and nonlinear, while P13 studies negative language and clicks. None establishes a universal best title for a personal essay, manual or research article. [Headline review](reviews/headlines-attention.md).

P06 studies sharing, P08 studies narrative/citation associations, P12 combines predictive article analyses with one-sentence experiments, and P02 predicts reported view change in selected online conversations. These are useful comparisons, but sharing intentions, actual sharing, citations, perceived impact and a delta award are different outcomes. P02's 65.1% versus 59.6% result is matched-pair prediction accuracy, not a persuasion success rate or an effect of rewriting a sentence. [Narrative/style review](reviews/narrative-style-impact.md), [interactive-persuasion review](reviews/interactive-persuasion.md).

**Consequence:** choose the intended outcome before retrieving advice. Offer truthful title and framing alternatives with their tradeoffs. Use a relevant author-supplied example or experience when it serves the contribution; preserve non-narrative routes and unresolved endings. Popularity measures cannot stand in for the author's purpose, factual fidelity or reader comprehension.

### Form has useful conventions without one mandatory outline

The full papers make this especially concrete. Tessuto (P15) documents empirical-law sections, blends and recursive moves. Yeung (P16) analyzes 22 authentic business reports with recurring relationships among findings, interpretation, warrants and recommendations. Cotos et al. (P17) describe 900 Methods sections across 30 fields through three moves and sixteen steps, with overlapping realizations and disciplinary variation. [Genre review and inventories](reviews/genre-moves.md).

These sources support our Family → Form → Section direction. They also show why a printed heading, a broad publication family or an average proportion cannot determine the whole document. A Methods-section model is not a template for a methods paper. A business report's recommendation cycle is not a compulsory paragraph order.

**Consequence:** build functional blanks and structural alternatives. For a recommendation, make the choice, observations, criterion connecting them, alternatives and conditions available as questions. For Methods, ask about context, what was done, and the reasons needed to judge or repeat it. Select only what the actual piece needs. Use source inventories to expose choices, not to import wording or quotas.

### Annotation can help research without becoming a writing bottleneck

Kim, Qiu and Wang (P18) reviewed 65 genre studies and compared agreement measures after training on nine introductions and evaluating 26 introductions comprising 908 sentence units. The source's full 35-text set, evaluation subset, sentence units and parent 600-introduction corpus are different denominators. Multi-valued agreement can distinguish shared secondary functions from strict-set disagreement, but it does not establish independent realization boundaries, correct targets or semantic accuracy. Its sample-specific discussion of roughly 750 units is not a minimum or success threshold for us. [Agreement review](reviews/agreement-writing-rules.md).

Our original guidance produced exact-set agreement of 21/36, while the richer Astra candidate produced 18/36. The richer records were useful for examining disagreements, but showed no agreement improvement. Their approximately threefold JSON volume is artifact size, not measured cost or effort. Evidence quotations were not necessarily complete realization spans, so we still cannot derive valid function-length profiles from them. [Local calibration](../rule-calibration-2026-11-16/report.md).

**Consequence:** retain the twelve IDs for research compatibility. Use a small number of function questions when they clarify a passage. Reserve exhaustive coding, alternative targets and fine boundary records for actual ambiguities and offline evaluation. Measure labels, spans, targets and reader benefit separately.

## Compare this with the rules we already have

The current drafting and editing skills already protect voice, necessary terms, supported qualifications, reader purpose and local editing. They explicitly reject generic goals of fewer words, uniformly short sentences, or lower readability scores. They distinguish conformance, source fidelity and usefulness. The new evidence largely reaffirms these choices; it does not justify a larger universal blacklist or an automatic rewrite loop.

The missing part is operational. The model can forget why a term matters, repeat advice already rejected, substitute a generic contribution for the author's thought, or apply a broad instruction in the wrong form. A durable project record, relevant retrieval, visible choices and inspectable diffs can make existing commitments survive the work. That is an implementation hypothesis to test, not an effect established by the papers.

The [rule decision ledger](rule-decision-ledger.json) contains twelve scoped proposals with source pages, current rule files, triggers, counterexamples, reusable parts and limits on code judgment. The [32 reviewer proposals](reviewer-candidate-register.json) remain separately preserved. None has silently changed a canonical skill or the accepted catalogue.

## Astra's answer: where the advantage could be

Astra's thesis is that the strongest opportunity lies in a persistent, author-controlled working process: retain the author's thought, make structural alternatives visible, apply only relevant guidance, and show minimal source-bound edits. I agree with that direction. A taxonomy, long prompt, larger corpus or panel of critics alone has not demonstrated an advantage here.

The model should do bounded jobs it is good at: recover a tentative contribution from notes, ask one consequential missing question, propose structural alternatives, develop a selected section, and diagnose a concrete reader problem. Code should maintain versions, retrieve scoped guidance and check declared constraints, references, spans and diffs. The author supplies experience, priorities, meaning and voice decisions. The model's inferences remain identified; silence does not become approval, and a local preference does not become a universal writing rule.

The proposed advantage is cumulative: over time the harness retains which choices the author made, why, for which form and reader, and how a particular revision helped. That material can reduce repeated correction and support learning. It is a plausible personal advantage over an otherwise similar stateless session; market uniqueness and performance superiority remain untested.

The [runtime proposal](runtime-proposal.md) specifies the first implementation and an example interaction. Astra's [full strategy](reviews/astra-advantage.md) contains five contrasting form examples and stop rules. Both preserve broad form flexibility while avoiding a claim that every form has a validated numeric profile.

## How to demonstrate that it works

Compare the same model using our current skills with the same model using the harness on fresh author tasks. Hold purpose, sources and actual constraints comparable, counterbalance task order, and preserve versions. Assess author repair work and repeated corrections, independent meaning/source fidelity, actual reader task performance, and voice acceptance separately. A later unaided piece can test whether a writing choice was learned rather than merely accepted.

Do not make model agreement, lower word count, acceptance rate or clicks the sole success measure. Select practical success criteria with the author before testing; a feasibility pilot does not establish general superiority. Our current work supplies an evidence-based design and evaluation plan, not an installed plugin or a measured advantage.

## Verification and remaining limits

All eighteen primary works have a dedicated full-text review and source-page locators; strategy and audit citations are not counted as additional papers. The independent audit inspected selected numeric and methodological claims, including rendered tables. It corrected an early reviewer interpretation that treated two Kickstarter error measures as one contradictory number. Other apparent reporting tensions are retained with their scope and unresolved causes, outside the premises of the product design.

The user's attachment originals are preserved. P02's replacement source is explicitly identified; no text was fabricated for missing pages. P14 supplementary methods and P08 materials were retrieved from official publisher endpoints; the downloaded R script was read as text only. Raw PDFs, extracted full text and supplemental participant data remain in the private workspace cache, with contributor identifiers excluded from derived outputs. No statistical analyses were replicated or effects pooled, and the existing skills, frozen coding packets and accepted foundations remain unchanged.
