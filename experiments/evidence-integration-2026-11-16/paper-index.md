# All eighteen papers: identity, design and useful contribution

Seven thematic reviewers read the full supplied papers. The independent audit checked selected decisive passages; Astra supplied strategy, not a second independent study. Physical PDF page locators, denominators and limitations are in the linked structured reviews and [combined evidence map](evidence-map.json). The batch includes a headline meta-analysis, a systematic methodological review, randomized comparisons, observational/predictive analyses, descriptive genre studies and an editorial. These are different evidence types. The three Upworthy papers share an archive, and several papers contain multiple designs or nonindependent annotations. No pooled universal style effect is estimated.
