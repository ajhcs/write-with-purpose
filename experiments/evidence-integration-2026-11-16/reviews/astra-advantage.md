# A plausible advantage for our writing harness

16 November 2026. Strategy proposal for the author's own plugin; not an installed capability or a demonstrated advantage. This review assesses the recorded Astra revision without claiming personal memory or authorship of every earlier rule. It reads the current skills, accepted foundations, calibration, dataset-adoption work and available specialist reviews. Selected decisive paper passages were checked against the page-marked source files; the specialist reviews own full-paper extraction.

**Our strongest opportunity is to help the author develop and express a thought they recognize as theirs, while making the consequential writing choices visible and learnable.** The useful product is a persistent working relationship among the author's ideas, a reader's task, structural alternatives, evidence and actual edits. A new taxonomy, a longer prompt, more model reviewers, or a larger dashboard is easy to reproduce and has not earned an advantage here.

The existing skills already say much of the right thing: recover the contribution, preserve voice and technical terms, expose missing connections, edit locally, and separate fidelity from usefulness. The proposed advantage is therefore operational: make those commitments survive long sessions and changing drafts, select only the relevant assistance, and demonstrate better author and reader outcomes than the same model using those skills alone. Calling this unique in the market would require a broader comparison we have not done.

All seven specialist syntheses reinforce this distinction: attention, sharing, citations, awards, perceived ease and correctness are different outcomes. Their rule proposals mostly refine existing skill guidance; they do not license a universal writing score. The three headline papers draw on the same Upworthy archive; overlapping historical tests are not three independent replications. Random assignment of complete headline variants does not isolate each word's effect. No function-length profiles follow from our evidence quotations: those anchors are not complete realization spans. The bounded independent audit was also read; reported paper inconsistencies are not premises of this design.

The full-text genre and agreement reviews upgrade earlier evidence access, including P15–P18, rather than establish a new independent replication of the local experiments. Findings about tentative words concern measured language features; they do not authorize changing the factual uncertainty of a claim. The current skills already preserve that distinction. Most additions proposed here belong in executable state, retrieval, provenance and review behavior, not in a new list of universal prose rules.

## Borrow the common parts; build the author workflow

Reuse our existing `check_text.py`, `edit_locally.py` and bounded annotation checker before replacing their working behavior. Use the AcaWriter schema/condition/message separation as an implementation pattern, subject to component and dependency review. Reuse Research Writing Tutor's progression from goals to annotated examples to draft feedback as a precedent, without assuming its application or full corpus is open source. Import GUM relations, README section roles, DITA task fields and scientific move definitions through small adapters that preserve their original units and namespaces. The acquired packages already reduce the need to label raw text from scratch.

Build the layer those ingredients do not supply for this author: durable idea provenance, reversible decisions about voice and structure, sparse feedback tied to the current reader task, and a short cycle in which the author makes a meaningful choice and sees its consequence. This is a design hypothesis; AcaWriter and RWT already cover substantial teaching and feedback territory.

Keep imported labels as lenses. “Methods,” “support,” “according to this source,” and “the reader should now run this command” belong to different layers. Do not force them through one universal classifier. Preserve the twelve candidate IDs for research compatibility, but let an ordinary author see “this paragraph explains why you chose the method,” without needing to adjudicate every possible label.

The nine accepted feature groups remain the measurement framework. The new session and decision records are workflow state, not additional writing-quality features. Family → Form → Section remains visible, with provisional family names and mixed or unresolved forms allowed. It should select useful questions, not become an obligatory taxonomy quiz or force an essay to acquire printed headings.

## Runtime contract: model, code and author

| Job | Model | Code | Author |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recover intent | Infer a tentative contribution from supplied notes; identify a consequential gap | Store original notes and their locators; separate author material, evidence, approved examples and rejected output | Supply or correct the thought, purpose and lived experience |
| Choose structure | Propose a small number of genuinely different routes and explain the tradeoff | Display Family → Form → Section and preserve versions, provenance and selected route | Choose, combine or replace the route; keep purposeful exceptions |
| Develop a section | Ask one question that unlocks missing substance; draft only within authorized scope | Retrieve only the applicable form/section guidance and approved materials | Contribute reasons, observations, priorities and judgments |
| Review | Identify a concrete reader problem; show the exact span, consequence and minimal remedy | Check spans, links, declared terms, explicit limits, counts and diff integrity | Judge voice and intended meaning; accept or reject substantive suggestions |
| Learn and finish | Explain the selected change in functional terms when teaching is wanted | Retain the decision and its scope; invalidate receipts when text changes | Decide whether the piece serves the purpose; decide which preferences should persist |

Do not make literal protection an automatic veto on paraphrase. An exact name can be mechanically protected; preserving a concept or a qualification needs contextual review. Do not convert “sounds like me” into a model-generated percentage. The model can identify possible meaning changes, but a matching number or term set cannot certify fidelity.

Use one conversational model by default. For a consequential ambiguity, a separate reading may help if authorized, but a panel is not a routine requirement. Keep source verification and a reader pass distinct even when one model performs both. The model's reader simulation is provisional editorial evidence, not an audience study.

A compact session needs: original notes/draft; reader and purpose; required terms and preservation obligations; selected form/section hypotheses; source records; decisions with their reasons and scope; current candidate and its diff; unresolved questions. Save the author's original words before paraphrasing them. A model inference remains marked as inferred until the author supplies or accepts it; silence is not approval. A local exception must not silently become a global voice preference.

An applicable feedback rule should carry `scope`, `trigger`, `source`, `evidence_kind`, `suggestion`, `counterexample`, `check_kind`, and `status`. The runtime can check whether known metadata match scope, but interpreting an ambiguous trigger remains a model judgment. Separate observed ranges, working targets, and declared minimum/maximum limits in both storage and display. Missing observations stay missing. Source scope follows the rule into the feedback card rather than disappearing after retrieval.

## Concrete pilot interactions

These are synthetic interaction designs, not transcripts or claims about Cole's actual experience. The family labels are illustrative and provisional. Prompts are functional blanks; source wording is not offered as a writing stem.

**Personal → Reflective essay → A moment that changed the author's understanding.** Start from the author's notes and preserve their striking wording. Ask: “Which moment made you reconsider the idea, and what did you believe before it?” If the answer supplies an episode, offer two outline routes: encounter → prior belief → changed understanding; or present judgment → remembered encounter → unresolved tension. The author can retain uncertainty and decline a moral. A blank reads `[what happened] / [what you noticed] / [what remains unresolved]`; it does not invent the experience or prescribe a dramatic opening. Teach the distinction between recounting an event and drawing meaning from it through the actual choice, not a lecture about narration.

**Explanatory → Conceptual explainer → The difficult relationship.** For a passage using “identifiability,” keep that term and ask which distinction the reader must understand. Offer a definition-led route or an author-supplied example followed by the term. Show the local difference: the example may make the relationship concrete but does not establish a general empirical claim. If the intended readers already know the term, no definition is needed. P01 motivates checking the reader's encounter with explanations; it does not prove either proposed route is superior.

**Research → Empirical report → Methods.** Ask: “What choice would another researcher need to understand to judge or repeat this work?” The blank is `[what was done] / [conditions] / [why this choice was appropriate]`, with “not applicable” permitted. Offer procedure-first and rationale-first arrangements only when both fit the research. Preserve whether the work is planned or completed. P17 pp. 15–16 supports functional inquiry and variation; its Methods model does not supply a whole methods-paper template or require every step in every discipline.

**Technical → README/how-to → First successful use.** Recover the user's intended end state, prerequisites and actual command from supplied project evidence. Show `[starting state] → [action] → [observable result]`, with an exception only when known. If an expected result is absent, say which field or source is missing; do not invent output or claim a command is executable because the fields are filled. Borrow DITA/OpenPI structure and README role comparisons while keeping procedure structure distinct from prose functions. A code check can resolve a link or literal; actual task success requires execution or a reader walkthrough.

**Professional → Decision memo/proposal → Recommendation.** Ask: “What decision can this reader make, and which uncertainty could change your recommendation?” Offer recommendation-first or problem-and-options-first arrangements. The blank is `[choice] / [reason] / [alternative] / [condition that changes the choice]`. Preserve the author's priorities and tradeoffs instead of fabricating a more persuasive justification. Downloaded email and legal labels do not establish professional memo norms; show that coverage gap and use the actual brief. A proposal additionally needs the commitments and feasibility evidence its recipient requires, not an automatic funding-success phrase list.

Each interaction can end with one useful explanation: “Moving this condition next to the recommendation makes clear when your advice applies.” If the user only requests a copy edit, perform the edit. Teaching is available in the writing work, not imposed as a course or a mandatory question at every turn.

## What to build first

Build a thin local plugin prototype around the existing skills and scripts, with two complete paths: developing an idea into a short explainer, and making a targeted revision while preserving meaning. Include one author-supplied reflective piece as a stress case for voice and unresolved endings. Keep the other forms visible as provisional choices, with scoped questions and candid evidence gaps; do not announce seventeen validated form profiles.

The first implementation should contain only the session record, scoped rule retrieval, a visible section map, original/candidate/diff views, sparse feedback cards, and deterministic checks for declared constraints. A feedback card needs a source span, reader consequence, a possible action, and “keep as written.” An initial display budget of up to three material issues is a configurable usability hypothesis, not a validated numerical optimum; consequential fidelity problems must not be hidden by that budget. Explain more on request. An issue earns attention by changing the reader's task or a stated author requirement, not by increasing a score.

Do not begin with a full annotation workbench, a bespoke base model, a multi-agent coding service, automatic corpus-derived length targets, or installation of a whole predecessor application. Keep full realization-span collection in the research path. It is necessary for future function-length observations, not for every useful edit. The earlier Astra revision overinvested in recording machinery before demonstrating that it improved the central judgment. Preserve its inspection benefits and test a much lighter author interface.

## Stop, abstain and preserve disagreement

- If meaning is adequate and the requested constraints pass, leave the passage alone. Another edit needs a named benefit.
- If a source, antecedent or realization boundary is missing, mark the particular judgment unavailable. Do not treat an absent label as an absent function or fill a source gap with a plausible fact.
- If two structural readings remain defensible, show the practical choice or retain both. Do not force a winner to make the diagram tidy.
- If an observation is out of scope for the form, audience or unit, do not turn it into a warning. If the exact form is unknown, proceed from purpose and state the assumption.
- If a new edit breaks a declared limit or a source-supported condition, repair that edit or show the conflict. Do not silently weaken a claim to meet a metric.
- If repeated advice is rejected, stop rephrasing it and retain the scoped decision. Reopen it only for new evidence, a changed task, or an author request.

## How we would demonstrate advantage

Compare the same model with **A: the current writing/editing skills** and **B: those skills plus the harness** on fresh real writing tasks. Give both the same author materials, source access and task constraints, and log actual elapsed work, model usage, corrections and interaction burden. Use matched task sets and counterbalance order; do not let the author rewrite the same remembered task twice and call it an independent comparison. Separate later ablations—persistent decisions, structural alternatives, and sparse feedback—from the initial package comparison.

Before testing, define form-specific reader tasks and author priorities. For explainers, test whether readers can recover and apply the intended distinction. For procedures, test completion and recognition of the expected result. For memos, test whether the choice and its conditions are recoverable. For personal writing, the author's recognition of voice and intended experience is primary; comprehension quizzes cannot replace that goal. Compare source fidelity and preservation of key qualifications separately, using reviewers unaware of the assigned arm where feasible.

Measure whether the author reaches an acceptable draft with less repair, whether important ideas and terminology survive, whether readers perform the intended task, and whether the author can make the same kind of choice on a later unaided task. The last measure addresses learning rather than dependence. Record author corrections and false-positive feedback, not just accepted suggestions: quick acceptance can mean fatigue or compliance. Retain failed cases and do not average away damage in a form that is central to the user's work.

Choose practical success criteria before seeing the results. A discovery pilot can establish feasibility and reveal failures; a later adequately designed comparison is needed for general benefit claims. Declare an advantage only in the contexts where the measured author/reader benefit warrants the extra interaction and factual/voice harm has been assessed. Better agreement among annotators is useful diagnostic evidence, but it cannot substitute for this result.

Our defensible claim today is narrower: we have a strong author-centered specification, reusable source material, mechanical prototypes, and an honest failed calibration. The proposed advantage remains to be earned through a small working product and comparisons against the already capable skills.
