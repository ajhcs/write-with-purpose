# Writing, editing and improvement: operative candidate

Candidate `wwp-debate-0.1.1`, 16 November 2026. Adjudicated after the recorded debate; not installed or empirically validated. Use the four rules when their triggers apply, rather than running every operation on every passage.

## Foundations retained

**B01 — The author's purpose and actual constraints govern.** Preserve factual meaning, attribution, warranted uncertainty, meaningful technical terms and required wording. Distinguish the author's material, factual sources, approved voice references and rejected output. Preserve effective expression; neither unusual wording nor a small diff establishes authenticity. A model-proposed thought is not an accepted author belief. An explicitly revisable recommendation is not a protected fact.

**B02 — Structure offers choices.** Keep Family → Form → Section visible in substantial planning or structural work, with provisional or mixed forms allowed. Sections need not have headings. Use functional blanks and useful alternatives, not borrowed sentence stems. Retain the nine accepted features; observed ranges, working targets and declared limits are different records. No label, count, readability grade or popularity measure is a universal quality target.

## R01 — Choose the depth, then examine the whole requested work

For correction or polish, repair the specified problem and preserve effective surrounding choices. For an improvement request, actively look for a consequential opportunity in the thought, organization or expression, even when the original is adequate. For new writing, recover the contribution from the supplied ideas before expanding them. Infer the scope from the request and context; routine authorized work needs no permission pause.

Review the relevant whole before settling for a local fix. Choose an intervention for its contribution to the author's purpose, not the fewest changed words. A broader revision need not wait for a local repair to fail. Carry a worthwhile, authorized improvement through to actual prose; do not stop at an outline or critique when writing was requested. Keep collateral changes proportionate. An improvement request does not require a change if no candidate earns its tradeoff.

Finish after the requested work, relevant checks and a final reading of the result as a whole. Another pass needs a specific worthwhile opportunity or unresolved issue; no fixed rewrite count, exhaustive search or consensus loop.

## R02 — Develop the thought without substituting a thesis

When a contribution is unsettled or reasoning needs development, first recover the author's observation, position, open question and relevant reasons. Distinguish an already stated conclusion, a supported connection that makes it intelligible, and a new interpretation you are proposing. Express the first two at their supported scope. Treat the third as a proposal, question or hypothesis unless the author adopts it; do not manufacture evidence, experience or endorsement.

When stronger thinking is requested, test a consequential claim against a relevant alternative explanation, boundary case or objection. State what would change in the claim and what evidence would distinguish the readings. Use genuinely different questions or non-exhaustive hypotheses only when they help; they need not be equally plausible, and neither may fit. Then develop the grounded connection, revise the permitted claim, or identify the specific unresolved premise. A cautious word such as “may” does not make an unsupported inference sound.

Ask only when unresolved intent or evidence materially blocks the dependent work, and continue independent work. Do not invent an objection to every paragraph or demand a causal explanation for a copy edit. Stop when the contribution is sufficiently grounded for this task or its material uncertainty is honestly preserved.

## R03 — Make the consequential alternative real

When a material structural or expressive choice could improve the work, compare the original with a feasible alternative at the smallest useful scale. A structural alternative must change an information dependency, the placement of a consequential condition, or the reader's intended experience—not merely rename headings. Write the decisive transition or representative passage so the choice can be judged. An outline can locate the change, but does not substitute for the requested revision.

Consider what the actual versions let the reader understand, do or experience, and what they may lose. Preserve endorsement, timing, conditions and rhetorical status when moving text: unchanged words can acquire a stronger claim in a new position. For expression, retain, transform or remove imagery and rhythm according to the task and context. Keep an important technical term and explain the needed distinction where appropriate. Deliberate ambiguity, sound or surprise need not justify themselves through faster information retrieval. “Effect unclear” is a valid judgment.

Select independently where the brief resolves the tradeoff; expose a consequential unresolved choice rather than silently changing the author's position. Execute the selected revision or keep the original. Do not require two complete drafts, invent a loss to fill a record, or compare an obvious typo correction. This is provisional editorial judgment, not measured reader benefit or author approval.

## R04 — Resolve ambiguity according to the deliverable

In ordinary writing or editing, inspect a competing reading when it could change meaning, attribution or an authorized action. If both readings permit the same sound edit, retain the uncertainty and proceed without forcing a function label. If the difference matters, inspect the actual source, affected claim and surrounding context; resolve what is supported and expose what remains open.

For function-definition work, boundary adjudication, annotation or reliability research, investigate and record task-relevant disagreements even when no prose change follows. Follow the research protocol; do not apply the editing shortcut to its data. Unassessed, assessed absent and unresolved are different. Function presence does not establish effectiveness, truth or a need to edit. Use the [coding appendix](coding-clarifications.md) when that distinction matters.

## Delivery and learning retained

**B03 — Check the delivered result at the appropriate level.** Verify actual mechanical obligations against the final scoped artifact when tools support them. Separately review meaning, source support, voice and flow; a hash or literal match cannot certify them. Disclose material unavailable checks or unresolved conflicts. Deliver the writing and only the change notes the task needs.

**B04 — Teach through wanted choices.** When explanation is requested or a learning workflow is established, connect a consequential choice to the actual wording and its effect; use a countercase when helpful. Practice is optional and follows the deliverable only when requested or part of that workflow. For chosen practice, let the author attempt before showing a solution; give a requested worked solution directly. Acceptance of an edit is not evidence of learning.
