# Accepted direction and candidate boundary

16 November 2026. The author accepted the preceding harness plan and explicitly added editing and improvement. They requested a debate between representatives of the findings, with an adjudicator prioritizing uniqueness and novelty, because the existing coding and rules are useful but not optimized.

This author instruction authorizes developing and exercising a new candidate. It makes affirmative improvement part of the design: when requested, consider better reasoning, structure and expression rather than merely correcting defects. A narrow correction request still controls its own scope. Preserve the author's original contribution while allowing it to develop through authorized revision.

Novelty has two purposes here: useful mechanisms beyond the inspected baseline, and preserving or developing what the author uniquely has to say. New terminology, unfamiliar wording and invented evidence do not establish either kind of novelty.

The earlier accepted foundations remain in force: nine feature groups, Family → Form → Section, and a separation between observed ranges, working targets and declared limits. Existing function definitions and historical coding are preserved as comparison inputs. The debate's resulting rules are candidates, not evidence of improved performance or author approval of every individual rule. Canonical and installed skills are unchanged.

The precise debate procedure and evidence boundaries are in [the contract](debate-contract.md). This note records the newly accepted direction without rewriting the historical foundation or retrospective study records.
