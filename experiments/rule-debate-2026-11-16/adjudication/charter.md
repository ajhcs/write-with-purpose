# Adjudication charter

16 November 2026. Published before reading the advocates' positions or their exchanges. This charter governs the later decision; it does not select rules. All seven files in `baseline-snapshot.json` matched their recorded SHA-256 hashes when checked. Final adjudication waits for the complete positions, cross-challenges and replies and the parent's notice that those stages are ready.

The deliverable is a compact, operative candidate for **drafting, editing and improvement**. It must change what the assistant does in consequential cases. A literature summary, a renamed version of existing guidance, or a more elaborate annotation form will not satisfy that objective.

## Eligibility before preference

A proposal is eligible when it respects the author's explicit scope and constraints, makes honest claims about its evidence, and presents an intelligible path to a useful writing outcome. An untested mechanism can meet these conditions. It need not have a paper proving the exact intervention or an existing implementation.

The following boundaries apply:

- Author-supplied facts, experience, intended certainty and actual preservation requirements cannot be silently replaced. A proposed new interpretation or inference must be distinguishable from the author's accepted position; it may be offered and developed within authorized improvement work.
- Accepted foundations remain: the nine features; visible Family → Form → Section; observed distributions separate from chosen targets and declared limits. Provisional names, categories and twelve function IDs are open to challenge through an explicit migration. Historical labels and coding packets remain intact.
- A proposal must name a trigger, an action, the improvement it seeks, a tradeoff or counterexample, and the practical division of model, code and author work. A new prohibition alone is insufficient unless it removes a demonstrated obstruction to the task.
- Empirical associations, scoped experiments, pedagogical proposals and local observations retain their actual scope. Novel recombinations and hypotheses are admissible without being presented as established effects.

These are admission conditions, not a conservative scoring system. They do not require unchanged structure, minimal word differences, a citation for every design decision, author approval for every routine edit, or exhaustive annotation before action.

## Priority order among eligible proposals

1. **Substantive novelty relative to inspected baselines.** Prefer a meaningful new decision procedure, representation or interaction that changes behavior. Show precisely where the current skills, integration candidates or inspected predecessors would behave differently. A familiar component can form a novel mechanism when the combination changes what can be done or decided; combining familiar words does not suffice.
2. **Development of the author's contribution and actual improvement.** Favor mechanisms that help discover, sharpen, test, connect or express what matters to the author. They must improve the work in a specified way, including structure, reasoning, evidence use, explanation, voice or expression. Surface polish alone is not the default definition of improvement.
3. **Operative clarity.** A model or author should be able to act on the rule and recognize when it does not apply. Code should verify only what it can establish. A rule may depend on contextual judgment without pretending that judgment is a deterministic check.
4. **Benefit relative to burden.** Prefer the mechanism that delivers the needed improvement with proportionate interaction and recording. Burden is a tradeoff, not an automatic veto on ambitious work or a reason to select the smallest edit.

This is an ordered deliberative preference, not a numerical weighted score or a mechanical winner selection. Novelty without a plausible improvement fails eligibility. Once that threshold is met, unfamiliar mechanisms get a fair hearing rather than losing automatically to familiar safeguards. If a familiar alternative is retained over a materially novel one, the decision must state the concrete reason and preserve the novel alternative's strongest case.

## What counts as novelty

The comparison baseline includes both canonical skills, their relevant references, the twelve proposed function definitions, the Astra revision and failed calibration, the eighteen-paper integration candidates, the earlier Astra strategy, and the actually inspected AcaWriter/RWT and corpus/codebook mechanisms. Being new to one file is not enough. AcaWriter already separates configurable checks and feedback; RWT already combines goals, examples and draft analysis. We cannot claim these patterns as our invention.

Each candidate's novelty ledger will state:

`current behavior → proposed behavioral difference → nearest inspected precedent → residual distinctiveness → consequence in a worked case → uncertainty`

It will distinguish **inherited baseline**, **incremental operational change**, and **substantive mechanism change** without assigning fake precision. Separate evidence classifications will be **established principle**, **scoped empirical finding**, **novel recombination**, or **new hypothesis**. These are descriptions of what supports the claim, not a hierarchy of quality. A mechanism may contain more than one classification, recorded at claim level. “Established” will not mean universally true or proven by this debate.

No claim of worldwide novelty will be made. New names, more fields, forced contrarianism, arbitrary quotas, and unsupported factual invention do not constitute substantive novelty. Conversely, deleting a harmful requirement, splitting an overloaded decision, or changing the order of reasoning can be a meaningful innovation without adding a category.

## Challenge to my previous strategy

My earlier strategy emphasizes sparse feedback, durable author decisions and minimal source-bound edits. Those are useful candidates, but they could produce a polite preservation tool that never develops an argument beyond its first draft. This debate must be able to replace that strategy.

In particular:

- “No named defect” must not prohibit searching for a stronger arrangement when improvement is requested. An adequate draft can have a materially better alternative. The assistant should be able to identify a promising opportunity, construct the alternative and compare it with the original.
- Preserving the author's contribution does not mean preserving every surface sentence, current outline or unexamined inference. The relevant question is which choices carry the author's meaning and which are revisable means of expressing it. Actual preservation requirements still control.
- Sparse feedback is not shallow editing. Considerable reasoning and coordinated restructuring can produce a short, useful review. A displayed issue budget must not limit the quality of the internal comparison.
- Storing decisions may preserve mistaken early assumptions or fossilize a voice. A mechanism for reopening a consequential choice with new reasons can be better than perfect persistence of the original decision.
- A compact runtime need not require stable exhaustive labels. But the failed annotation pilot does not justify abandoning communicative analysis where it reveals a real improvement. Challenge its granularity and recording obligations rather than assuming the whole approach is useless.

I will also challenge the contract if an advocate uses its safeguards to avoid proposing improvement. Factual integrity and author authority are compatible with substantive exploration, disagreement and a stronger candidate. The author has authorized this search; routine work within it does not need repeated permission.

## Required editing and improvement demonstration

For each selected mechanism, the final decision must identify a concrete difference from the baseline and a development case that makes its consequence inspectable. Across the operative set, examples must cover drafting from ideas, targeted editing, and at least one improvement that changes structure or reasoning enough to test the preservation-versus-improvement tension.

An editing demonstration should expose the original passage or outline, the relevant author purpose and constraints, what the baseline rule tends to permit or miss, the candidate transformation, what improves, and what might be lost. It should not rig the comparison by making the baseline deliberately incompetent. When both approaches produce the same useful result, record that as limited distinctiveness rather than an invented advantage.

Examples must include a case where the proposed intervention should not run or should lose to keeping the original. A personal essay's open ending, a necessary technical term, a warranted qualification and a structurally complete passage are possible stress cases, not mandatory style recipes. Synthetic examples are demonstrations of operation, not empirical evidence that the new rules work or outperform the skills. Actual improvement remains a judgment to test with the author and appropriate readers.

## How the debate will be decided

After the exchange is complete, I will read each original position, the exact challenge against it, and its defend/revise/withdraw reply. The ledger will preserve those IDs and distinguish an advocate's revised wording from an adjudicator's synthesis. Majority agreement is not a correctness criterion. An unanswered criticism remains visible; it need not defeat a useful mechanism if its scope can be bounded honestly.

Possible decisions are retain as inherited foundation, select, merge, revise, defer for a stated unresolved issue, or reject for a specific reason. Rejection cannot rest only on unfamiliarity or lack of a directly matching study. Merging cannot erase the mechanism's decisive trigger or author responsibility. Any adjudicator-created mechanism must be marked as a synthesis and open to subsequent challenge; it must not be retroactively described as the advocates' consensus.

The output will separate the inherited foundation from the compact innovation set, supply structured rule records and an annotation/coding clarification appendix, and map old rules and function IDs to the candidate. A minority report will preserve the strongest rejected or unresolved alternatives and the conditions under which they deserve reconsideration. Validation will inspect whether the rules can be executed and expose their tradeoffs; empirical optimization will not be claimed.

No rule has been chosen by this charter. The next action is to wait for the completed debate.
