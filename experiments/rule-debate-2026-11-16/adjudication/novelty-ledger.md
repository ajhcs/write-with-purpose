# Novelty ledger

Candidate `wwp-debate-0.1.1`. Novelty is assessed against the inspected local skills, earlier Astra proposals, integration candidates and prior-work inspections. This is not a worldwide novelty search or evidence that the candidate outperforms them. No numeric novelty score is assigned.

The core set has **two comparatively distinct mechanisms and two operational refinements that make the set usable**. It is not four unrelated inventions. R02 combines bounded thought development, competing readings and ownership of inference; R04 decides annotation depth by the actual objective and consequence. R01 makes improvement affirmative even for adequate prose. R03 turns a meaningful choice into inspectable writing while avoiding duplicate drafts. Necessary inherited foundations remain visible without filling the innovation set.

## What the sources support

**Established principle** here means a retained local rule or a documented predecessor practice; it is not shorthand for a universal experimentally proven effect. **Scoped empirical finding** describes a study's actual sample and outcome. **Novel recombination** identifies a new arrangement or trigger relative to the inspected baseline. **New hypothesis** identifies the untested mechanism's expected benefit. A rule may contain several claim types, which [rules.json](../candidate/rules.json) distinguishes.

P15–P17 describe genre variation, rhetorical relationships and pedagogical implications. They motivate flexible functional inquiry and constrain claims about universal sequences. They do not prove R02 or R03 improves the author's work. P18 and the local calibration motivate preserving disagreement and measuring the right units; neither tests R04's action-sensitive triage. Audience, headline and funding studies do not validate a global simplicity, emotion or promotional-word rule. Reuse of those studies is principally scope discipline, not a new editing recipe.

The old skills can produce the worked E02 improvement. That fact weakens any claim of a previously impossible capability, but does not settle whether a clearer opportunity/inference procedure improves reliability. A fair test holds the model, material and task constant, compares the current skills with the candidate, and examines author-recognized contribution, factual/semantic harms, reader task or experience, interaction burden and—when learning is intended—unaided transfer. Faster acceptance, larger edits and more uniform labels cannot substitute for those outcomes.

## Selection and unresolved alternatives

No advocate was guaranteed a core rule. Twenty-two proposals reduce to four operative rules because repeated structural comparisons, local-versus-broad repair competitions and gain/loss checks are one mechanism with different applications. Learning and artifact checking remain useful outside the claimed innovation set. This favors substantive distinctions over a larger catalogue.

The [minority report in the decision record](decisions.md) preserves the case for mandatory branching, smallest-sufficient intervention, stronger imagery preservation, explicit creative tradeoff records and delivery automation. Their strongest benefits and reconsideration triggers remain visible. Rejecting the blanket versions does not prohibit them when the author requests them or a particular decision needs them.
