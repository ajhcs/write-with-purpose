# Adjudication: drafting, editing and improvement

Candidate `wwp-debate-0.1.1`. All 22 original proposals and all 17 challenges/replies received a disposition. The five position, five challenge and five reply JSON files matched their frozen stage receipts. Canonical skills and historical experiments remain untouched.

**Select four operative mechanisms, retain four foundations separately, and retain the twelve function IDs with explicit coding clarifications.** The outcome is a new combined procedure, not four claimed inventions. The strongest distinct mechanisms are bounded development of an author-owned inference (R02) and objective-gated ambiguity handling (R04). Active improvement scope (R01) and concrete comparison (R03) refine substantial capabilities already present in the skills.

## What changes in actual use

- **R01:** An improvement request triggers a whole-work search for a consequential opportunity even if the original is adequate. A structural candidate need not wait for a failed local repair. Correction/polish still stays within the brief.
- **R02:** Separate a stated conclusion, a supported connective and a proposed new interpretation. Use a relevant alternative or boundary to develop the actual claim when stronger thinking is requested; avoid ghostwriting belief or merely appending a hedge.
- **R03:** Compare a genuinely different dependency, condition placement or intended experience through an actual passage. Preserve rhetorical status when moving words; use a short asymmetric comparison when enough, and keep uncertain aesthetic effects uncertain.
- **R04:** Investigate a semantic disagreement according to what the deliverable needs. Editing can proceed without a settled label when the action is unaffected; definition/boundary/reliability work must preserve that disagreement as research data.

No rule demands two full drafts, exhaustive runtime annotation, a numeric voice/novelty score, a formulaic counterargument, or evidence that a creative choice increases retrieval speed. No model verdict is labeled measured reader gain.

## Minority and unresolved alternatives

All received challenges had replies, many revised toward convergence. That does not eliminate the substantive alternatives. These positions retain a serious case even where their original proponents later narrowed them. They are not attributed as continuing dissent against the final candidate.

**M01 — Require local repair to fail before exploring structure; choose smallest sufficient change.**

Reject as prerequisite; retain restraint for correction/polish. Strongest case: Reduces speculative rewriting and protects effective voice. Decision reason: Could miss a better structure or expression in an adequate draft despite explicit improvement scope. R01 permits direct whole-work opportunity selection. Reconsider when: Fresh comparisons show opportunity search causes excess rewriting without corresponding benefit; tighten trigger rather than invent a global diff score.

**M02 — Generate two questions, warrants or structural maps on every applicable substantial task.**

Reject mandatory number; retain useful non-exhaustive contrast. Strongest case: Explicit branching can prevent premature commitment and expose original alternatives. Decision reason: False choices and renamed-heading alternatives can add burden or substitute a model thesis. Reconsider when: Author requests brainstorming/comparison or evidence shows recurring premature convergence; expose alternatives then.

**M03 — Always record a named strongest gain/loss for creative choices.**

Reject mandatory formalization. Strongest case: Makes silent loss of a condition or distinctive effect visible. Decision reason: Can reward articulate rationalization and penalize sound, ambiguity or surprise the model cannot explain. Reconsider when: A consequential uncertain tradeoff warrants explicit comparison; allow effect unclear.

**M04 — Preserve a distinctive image first; offer plainer wording only under a separate plain-language request.**

Reject preservation inference from strangeness; keep actual protected imagery. Strongest case: Guards against flattening author individuality. Decision reason: Awkwardness is not proof of intentional voice and can obstruct an authorized improvement. Reconsider when: Author confirms the imagery or supplied style/context supports its deliberate effect.

**M05 — Routine saved obligation/receipt mapping and automatic final-delivery hash gate.**

Defer automation; retain scoped checks in B03. Strongest case: Could prevent stale checked artifacts being delivered and expose unavailable obligations. Decision reason: Not implemented; much semantic preservation is not a literal constraint; cannot claim chat binding from file hash. Reconsider when: A concrete delivery adapter exists and tests distinguish artifact identity, scope and semantic remainder.

**M06 — Treat optional practice and delayed transfer as a standalone novel core mechanism.**

Retain teaching in B04; reject independent novelty claim. Strongest case: Author explicitly wants to learn professional writing through real choices. Decision reason: Inspected RWT and prior Astra strategy already contain examples/feedback/transfer; novelty is limited to local workflow scope. Reconsider when: A distinct exercise mechanism demonstrates useful unaided transfer without obstructing requested output.
