# Independent audit: preregistered rubric and findings

16 November 2026. This rubric was written after reading the debate contract, accepted direction, all eight synthetic development cases, the two current skill instructions and accepted foundations, but **before reading the adjudicated candidate**. It is a diagnostic standard, not proof of general writing quality or measured improvement.

## Rubric

1. **Operative delta:** identify actual triggers, actions, outputs and stopping behavior that differ from existing skills; do not credit renaming or rewording as a new mechanism. Compare each novelty claim with the inspected prior workflow, feedback and corpus tools; limit claims to this inspection.
2. **Authorized improvement:** when improvement or idea development is requested, actively test changes to reasoning, structure or expression and allow substantial scoped revision. Under grammar-only or narrowly constrained requests, obey scope and keep effective language; improvement can include keeping the original after an honest comparison.
3. **Meaning and evidence:** separate author statements, model hypotheses, supplied evidence and unsupported inference. Preserve literal constraints, negation, numbers, evidence scope, uncertainty and distinctive choices; do not invent experience, motives, causal rationales or approval. Verify mechanical constraints separately from semantic judgment.
4. **Independent annotation objective:** allow standalone research coding without a prerequisite prose defect or suggested edit. Preserve original taxonomies, multiple and unresolved labels, actor and target provenance, and an evidence anchor separately from a complete realization span. A quote or short anchor is not a measured full span; do not silently revise frozen records.
5. **Traceable adjudication:** account for all 22 distinct advocate proposals and all 17 challenges with explicit retain/merge/revise/reject decisions and reasons, including contested or minority positions. Reconcile candidate mechanism wording with decisions and preserve previously accepted nine features and Family → Form → Section. A label change requires definitions, counterexamples and migration mapping.
6. **Operational and empirical honesty:** specify model, code and author roles; triggers and outputs must avoid unnecessary ceremony or silent acceptance. Distinguish a self-generated development exercise, independent reader outcomes and measured optimization. No sample-derived lengths, popularity proxy, imagined comparative advantage or imported relation label becomes a universal quality rule.

## Baseline before candidate

Existing drafting already asks for the contribution, reader question, contextual source grounding, protected technical terms, qualifications and a substantive reader edit. Existing editing explicitly permits a substantial rewrite on request, but normally seeks the smallest set of changes resolving a named defect. The material opportunity is an affirmative, task-authorized search for better structure, reasoning and expression even when no discrete defect has been identified; neither an unrequested rewrite nor a claim of proven improvement follows. Existing guidance also already requires checking explicit word limits, protecting voice and preserving the distinction between source fidelity and usefulness. A candidate must make its behavioral delta concrete relative to those instructions.

Cases E01–E08 test narrow correction, unsupported staffing inference, explicit repetition and open ending, novice explanation, original thought versus invented claims, faithful Methods description, standalone annotation and declared hard limits. These authored scenarios are development checks, not unseen held-out data or actual reader outcomes.

## Findings

### Core rules and first development exercise

The candidate’s meaningful behavioral delta is the affirmative search for a worthwhile change when improvement is authorized, even when no discrete defect has been named (R01). The baseline already permits explicit substantial rewriting, term protection and reader editing; the candidate describes an incremental operational refinement, not a demonstrated novel writing theory. R02 makes alternative interpretations and proposed inference bridges explicit; alternatives and author-controlled choice already appeared in earlier strategy, so novelty is scoped to the adjudicated procedure, not first invention. R03 permits substantial change without requiring complete competing drafts. B01 and R04 preserve narrow requests and standalone coding research; the appendix keeps evidence anchors and complete-as-assessed extents separate, original IDs and relations unchanged, and historical coding untouched.

**Resolved numerical defect:** E01's worked example initially stated 27 whitespace tokens, although both versions have 26. The candidate author corrected the example; independently checked with `split()`. The revised count does not itself prove semantic fidelity.

**Resolved learning-scope ambiguity:** The original B04 said to let the author attempt before showing a solution in a learning workflow. That could withhold an explicitly requested worked solution. Revision `wwp-debate-0.1.1` now limits attempt-first behavior to chosen practice and directly delivers requested worked solutions. The revision receipt preserves both guide hashes. The targeted synthetic checks now show L01 delivering the worked 80/100 solution and explanation directly, and L02 providing facts while awaiting the expressly requested practice attempt. These check initial sequencing, not learning outcomes. The eight-case exercise used frozen 0.1 inputs; none requests teaching or practice, so it must not be represented as an execution of the revised B04.

The saved E01–E08 synthetic exercise supplies actual prose or standalone annotation rather than only advice. In particular E02 distinguishes observed backlog flow from ungrounded headcount; E03 retains repeated language and an unresolved ending after considering an alternative; E04 explains the scoped idempotence behavior without removing the term; E06 removes an unsupported rationale and directive; E07 retains hypothetical status, competing interpretations and separate anchors with extents `not_collected`; E08 retains the condition and prototype-only test. These are development checks on author-supplied examples, not unseen holdout performance, observed reader benefit, author acceptance or measured optimization. Parent-run constraint checks are a separate receipt; exact anchors and word counts cannot certify semantic function presence or fidelity.

### Final adjudication and migration readback

The adjudication ledger contains all 22 proposal IDs and all 17 challenge IDs exactly once; each proposal has a disposition, reason and novelty assessment, and each challenge has an adjudicated resolution. All 15 frozen-stage receipts match current source hashes. Minority and unresolved alternatives are retained. Every file listed in the final-file inventory now exists.

The [migration](../candidate/migration.md) accurately distinguishes the baseline's existing substantial-rewrite capability from the candidate's affirmative opportunity search, preserves nine features, hierarchy and the twelve identity-mapped function IDs, and forbids silent conversion of historical anchors into complete spans. Its proposed annotation representation is not an implemented validator. The [novelty ledger](../adjudication/novelty-ledger.md) compares against current skills, earlier Astra strategy, AcaWriter/RWT and source methods, identifies two stronger proposed mechanisms and two operational refinements, and explicitly limits novelty to inspected baselines. No comparison has established superiority. These are defensible bounded design claims, not proof of worldwide novelty or improved reader outcomes.

The parent-run checks report five nonempty declared prose contracts passing, with E02/E05 advisory only, and six E07 anchors match their source. Mechanical conformity does not settle meaning, function labels or value to a reader.

No material outstanding issue was identified within this bounded review. The self-generated cases are development checks only; their results cannot establish optimization, accuracy, author acceptance, learning or an advantage over the baseline. Comparative evaluation on fresh work remains necessary.
