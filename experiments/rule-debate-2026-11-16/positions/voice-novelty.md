# Round 1 position: voice, originality and novelty

The harness should help the author **discover and improve the contribution**, not merely protect existing wording. The current skills already preserve technical terms, qualification, author voice and reader context. Their instruction to make the smallest set of changes can nevertheless bias an improvement pass toward a local repair when the real problem is the order or strength of the argument. Keep narrow correction-only work narrow; when the author asks for substantive improvement, authorize a larger change if it has a specific reader benefit and respects actual constraints.

These five mechanisms are candidates, not validated improvements. Their full triggers, roles, examples and evaluation plans are in [voice-novelty.json](voice-novelty.json). Novelty is claimed only relative to the inspected skills and prior Astra proposal.

## VN-01 — Recover the author's live question

When notes or a draft contain an observation without a settled contribution, separate author observations, asserted positions, unresolved questions and model inferences. Offer two genuinely different questions that the supplied material could answer, identifying the evidence and gap for each. The author can choose, amend or reject; a provisional choice is acceptable if the task can proceed without an answer. Develop the chosen inference, then select and reorder supporting material accordingly.

For example, notes saying that a wheelchair request became a budget item while staff feared a fall could support an inquiry into how a safety concern loses context, or an inquiry into why a purchase was delayed. The first can be a qualified hypothesis; the second needs decision evidence. Neither establishes what the budget reviewer thought. This extends the existing instruction to identify a contribution and Astra's idea-recovery proposal with explicit competing questions and provenance. It should not interrupt a settled copy edit. The model proposes, code may track anchors, and the author owns the interpretation.

## VN-02 — Compare structures by reader work

For a substantial draft with credible alternatives, produce two concise maps using the same facts and required content but a different order of discovery or inference. Name what each lets the reader do earlier and what it delays; carry the selected route through to a revised passage. For instance, an experience → problem → argument route lets a reader encounter the issue before its name, whereas proposition → reasons → boundary lets a decision-maker assess the thesis first. These are different architectures only if their dependencies or intended experience differ, not if the headings are merely renamed.

This makes substantial relocation and rewriting a normal option when it resolves a real problem. Current guidance recommends an outline and allows moving passages, but does not require comparing two equivalent-evidence routes. The local pattern test found varied structures in a small purposive sample, not a winning structure for all professional writing. Exact constraints, protected sequences, and correction-only requests still govern. Code can display moves and retained spans; it cannot prove semantic equivalence.

## VN-03 — Repair friction while testing the distinctive choice

When unusual language appears difficult, identify both what it contributes and the reader problem. Compare the original in context against a repair that retains the distinctive image or rhythm, and, when the brief permits, a plainer alternative. Choose the version that makes the intended relationship recoverable, even if the best repair requires changing the paragraph substantially. If neither alternative improves the effect, keep the original.

A hypothetical “The request walked the hallway without its reason” could become “As the wheelchair request moved through the organization, the reason behind it could be lost.” This retains the movement/loss idea while making its referent and uncertainty explicit; it does not assert a reviewer's motive. If the author wants plain technical language, the metaphor can instead go. This makes voice a local, contextual decision, rather than treating novelty of wording as quality or small diffs as fidelity. The approved style passage is one useful reference, not a complete model of the author's voice.

## VN-04 — Challenge the contribution without ghostwriting belief

When asked to strengthen thinking, derive a relevant alternative explanation, boundary case or objection from the actual material. State which claim it challenges, what would change if it held, and what evidence would distinguish the alternatives. Then offer a concrete revised claim or unresolved question. A test is not an authored fact, and an unsupported possibility is not automatically equally credible.

For a draft claiming “the purchasing process ignored the risk” where the notes show only that the concern became a purchase request, test whether the risk was communicated but weighed against other constraints. A qualified revision can describe the possible loss of context while requesting the decision record before asserting a motive. This changes the claim rather than appending a formulaic hedge. Do not require objections in a personal reflection or a correction-only edit. P02 concerns observational, self-selected online discussion; it does not establish that any objection causes persuasion. P06 concerns sharing and intention, not evidence that emotional framing improves understanding.

## VN-05 — Make a chosen edit teachable when requested

For an author seeking to learn, explain one meaningful edit using original and revised wording, the reader consequence and a tradeoff. Offer an optional transfer exercise grounded in different supplied material; when practice is requested, let the author decide before showing the model's solution. For example, compare a recommendation with its condition delayed versus the same condition placed alongside it, then ask the author to solve a different scope problem. Do not impose a lesson on prose-only or urgent work, and do not turn a quickly accepted suggestion into proof of learning.

Astra already proposes concise explanations and later unaided evaluation; this adds a contrastive exercise linked to a real author decision. Test actual delayed transfer against the existing skill, alongside author preference, reader performance, source fidelity and time. P12's randomized sentence comparisons measured perceived importance and expected impact, not learning; its observational section associations cannot justify inserting first person or changing tense.

## Boundaries and questions for debate

The twelve provisional paragraph functions remain available as diagnostic language, with multiple and unresolved labels. They do not fully represent a developing thought, pacing, humor, voice or a reader's experience; this position proposes no taxonomy migration. Distinguish author's observations from model suggestions, genuine verbatim obligations from inferred preservation, and author approval from a demonstrated reader benefit. None of the cited click, sharing or citation outcomes is a general writing-quality score.

The open question is how to give the model enough latitude to improve a weak structure without imposing a preferred reading. The next round should test these proposals against concrete failures: unwanted interruption, invented implications, hollow alternatives, ambiguity mistakenly preserved, and useful author language flattened into the model's default prose.
