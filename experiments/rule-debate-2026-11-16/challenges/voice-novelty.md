# Round 2 challenges: author voice and originality

The [structured challenges](voice-novelty.json) preserve the target IDs, failure cases, proposed repairs and novelty assessments. These are disagreements about the operative rule, not claims that the advocates intended the failures.

## VN-C01 → EI-02: a plausible bridge can still replace the author's thought

I disagree with treating a model-written bridge as the default whenever an observation and conclusion need connecting. The leaking-window example's distinction between updates and completed work is plausible, but the author's intended point might instead be the burden of repeated follow-up or the uncertainty residents experience. “Can” limits certainty; it does not establish which interpretation belongs to the author.

**Repair:** distinguish a source-stated conclusion and a necessary connective from a new interpretive thesis. Insert a supported connective within scope. When competing theses would materially change meaning, label the model's inference as provisional and offer the alternative or ask the author, while continuing independent editing. A prose-only request can receive the best-grounded version with a concise material uncertainty note.

**Novelty:** the current writing skill already says “Make the relationship intelligible” and “Show how the relevant fact supports the conclusion.” The incremental mechanism is testing competing bridges and retaining who supplied the inference, not bridge-writing itself.

## VN-C02 → EI-03: recomposition changes emphasis and endorsement

A map of claim, reasons and conditions can make a decision clearer, but moving an idea can promote a tentative thought into a directive without changing its literal words. A reflective essay that ends “I may need to leave” can lose its uncertainty or intended discovery if that sentence becomes the opening thesis; changing it to “I should leave” is still more consequential.

**Repair:** compare rhetorical status, attribution, scope and temporal force as well as retained facts. A decision-first route is appropriate when a decision is actually the contribution; compare a discovery-led route when exploring the question is the experience. Proceed with a reversible rewrite when the brief settles intent; flag a material choice when it does not.

**Novelty:** existing `structure-and-purpose.md` already asks the editor to make a decision, reasons and consequences recoverable and to try movement or combination. The useful addition is a material alternative architecture tested with counterconsiderations intact, not the act of mapping a claim.

## VN-C03 → CE-02: do not turn semantic preservation into literal locks

The trigger “high-stakes preservation obligation” can produce needless records or promote a semantic obligation into an exact-text rule. For “keep the uncertainty in the diagnosis,” protecting the original sentence may block a clearer equivalent. Worse, the phrase can survive in a quotation while the main claim becomes definitive and still passes the literal check. A hash cannot prove the author endorsed the interpretation or that a checked file matches a later chat response.

**Repair:** make receipts for explicit mechanically expressible obligations when a scoped final artifact and check exist. Review material semantic obligations in context without requiring a saved record for every edit. Bind the checked artifact to delivery where supported; report unavailable chat verification honestly. Never convert an idea or voice preference into a protected literal.

**Novelty:** `checks.md` already limits contracts to actual constraints, warns that literal presence does not establish context, and fingerprints inputs. Automatic final-artifact linkage and explicit coverage status would be a useful, currently unimplemented engineering addition—not an originality mechanism.

## VN-C04 → CE-03: losses can be uncertain too

An explicit loss comparison is useful, but the model may overvalue losses it can articulate and dismiss an intended effect it cannot. A delayed, odd detail might be labeled “delays the point” even when delayed recognition is the purpose of a personal essay.

**Repair:** record a gain or loss as a hypothesis, allowing “effect unclear.” Test the author's intended experience before imposing a decision-memo reader task. Materialize alternatives where a consequential choice warrants them, without making a record or author interruption mandatory for an ordinary edit.

**Novelty:** current guidance already compares removal, movement and combination while considering lost information. Comparing different intervention levels and explicitly naming plausible losses is the increment. It overlaps substantially with VN-02 and VN-03; merge related mechanisms in adjudication rather than counting them as independent innovations.

## Concessions

EI-05's concrete warning against inventing a new dependency is stronger than my broad inference-challenge rule and should be included in the combined version. CE-02's final-artifact discipline strengthens provenance where a receipt is warranted. CE-03's loss comparison improves the structural-alternatives proposal if uncertain effects are allowed. EI-01 makes the permission to improve more decisively than my Round 1 wording: an ordinary reversible rewrite need not wait for the author's choice when the request and context support one.
