# Round 3: checks and evaluation replies

The challenges expose three real overextensions: a semantic obligation is not necessarily a literal contract, a creative choice need not have a model-invented “loss,” and an empty issue list does not prove an improvement request was examined. I revise CE-02 through CE-04 and clarify CE-01. The frozen Round 1 proposals remain unchanged.

## VN-C03 → CE-02: revise

A formal receipt is appropriate only when the task contains a mechanically testable obligation and the workflow supports a scoped check of the relevant final artifact. Preserving diagnostic uncertainty is a semantic duty; the model must not turn it into an exact-string lock unless the author actually protected the wording. Nor is an observed phrase automatically protected because it occurred in the source. An unchanged phrase in a quotation can pass the literal check while the revised claim loses its uncertainty. A hash identifies checked bytes, not author approval or, without technical binding, the delivered chat.

**Revised behavior:** “For an explicit, mechanically testable obligation, use a scoped check of the final artifact when supported, inspect its status, and bind any receipt to the checked bytes. Distinguish exact protected wording from observed wording and semantic preservation. Review material meaning, attribution and uncertainty in context, without requiring a saved record. If the check or delivery binding is unavailable, state the limitation when it matters. Do not infer literal or numerical constraints from a general preference.”

This preserves the semantic remainder without requiring a new record for every edit. `checks.md` already states most of these limits; reliable automatic linkage to the delivered artifact is a proposed engineering extension, not a current feature or a creative originality claim.

## VN-C04 → CE-03: revise

A model's stated gain or loss is a hypothesis, not an authoritative valuation of rhythm, tension, beauty or surprise. “Effect unclear” is a legitimate outcome; an aesthetic choice can be worthwhile on its own terms and need not demonstrate analytic productivity or faster answer recovery. A decision-oriented task should not displace an essay's intentional delayed recognition.

**Revised behavior:** “When an authorized change presents a material, uncertain tradeoff, compare the original and the relevant alternative at the least costly level that makes the choice intelligible. Consider possible gains, losses and intended experience, allowing the effect to remain unclear. Make or retain the change that best serves the author’s task and protected constraints; surface alternatives when a consequential preference cannot be inferred. Do not require a formal record, multiple complete drafts, a quantified score or a model-invented rationale for each creative choice.”

A short passage, outline or direct source-to-edit comparison can suffice. Materialize a second complete version when necessary to evaluate a consequential choice or requested by the author. This extends the existing removal/movement comparison across intervention scales, with substantial overlap with voice proposals; it is a candidate to merge, not a separate proven innovation.

## FF-C01 → CE-04: revise

I accept that “no named unresolved issue” can reward an editor who looked too narrowly. Before stopping a substantive improvement, the model must make a task-scaled whole-passage assessment, including opportunities for stronger organization, development or expression where supported and authorized. A better aesthetic alternative can be worth considering even when the original contains no identifiable defect. The inspection is not an unlimited search or a requirement to fabricate alternatives. For punctuation-only work, the limited task controls.

**Revised behavior:** “For substantive improvement, examine the whole passage against the author's purpose and intended reader or experience; consider consequential opportunities to improve reasoning, organization or expression, as well as material losses. Execute a supported, authorized improvement where warranted and complete relevant checks. Stop when that task-scaled review is complete and no further authorized alternative offers a specific expected benefit worth its tradeoff; do not create fixed rewrite or consensus loops. A model's judgment remains provisional. For a separately requested annotation or research deliverable, follow its frozen sampling, units, independent coding, uncertainty, adjudication and agreement protocol regardless of whether a label would change the edit.”

Research annotation and ordinary runtime editing remain separate. Evaluation of any claimed rule gain requires independent task-appropriate evidence; P18's fixed sentence units and our 21/36 versus 18/36 model pilot do not establish semantic correctness or reader benefit.

## CE-01: clarify without a direct incoming challenge

**Status: revised for consistency.** “When a material authorized revision has a specific reader or experiential objective, make the intended change and compare the affected original and revised material against that objective and any material loss. For an aesthetic objective, consider the intended effect without requiring an analytic productivity claim. Use the original when the alternative does not earn its tradeoff. A comparison may be a local passage; a second full draft is not required. Model assessment is provisional, not observed reader success.”

This retains the useful source-to-candidate test while recognizing that improvement may be an artistic opportunity rather than repair of a named failure. Existing guidance already contains reader edits and concrete comparisons; the candidate increment is the explicit before/after objective and scope-aware decision, which must be tested against that baseline.

## Remaining disagreement

I retain a lightweight gain/loss comparison for **material uncertain tradeoffs** because it can expose a silently lost condition or intended effect. I do not require an explanation or record for every creative decision, nor do I require the model to decide an effect is measurable. Where the author’s intended experience is genuinely unclear and changes the choice, author feedback is stronger than the model’s preference; an ordinary reversible edit does not require a permission interruption. Final acceptance and an independently observed benefit remain separate.
