# Round 3 reply: voice and originality

I accept all three challenges and revise the proposals below. The [structured reply](voice-novelty.json) retains the exact operative wording and disposition of all five original IDs. These are candidates for adjudication, not proven improvements.

## EI-X01 → VN-03: revise

Unusual language does not establish author intent. A request to make prose clearer and stronger can authorize transforming or removing an ineffective image, without requiring a separate plain-language instruction. The original hallway example could also imply a route the source does not establish.

**Revised rule:** When unusual wording may obstruct the requested effect, identify the intended effect as supported by the brief or context and the specific reader difficulty. Compare the original with a context-faithful revision that may retain, transform, or remove the image or rhythm. Preserve explicit wording constraints and supported meaning; do not infer a preservation requirement from strangeness alone. Select the revision that best serves the request, or keep the original if neither helps. A broader rewrite is appropriate when authorized and necessary.

For the hypothetical safety concern that became a purchase request, a permissible candidate is: “The safety concern became a purchase request, and its reason could be lost along the way.” The possible loss remains conditional; the sentence does not invent an administrative route or decision-maker's knowledge. This implements the inherited principle of preserving effective author choices while repairing actual problems. Its added operational hypothesis is to test the choice without treating oddness itself as evidence of intent.

## EI-X03 → VN-02: revise

Two maps with renamed headings are not real alternatives. Nor should a time-sensitive author have to choose between them when the task already settles the tradeoff.

**Revised rule:** When substantive drafting or improvement presents a consequential, feasible structural choice, compare an alternate route only if it changes an information dependency, placement of a material qualification, or the intended reader experience. Render the decisive transition in actual prose, test what each route makes recoverable and delays, and preserve the author's endorsement, uncertainty and necessary evidence. Choose independently when the brief resolves the tradeoff; present alternatives or identify a material uncertainty only when intent remains unresolved. Use one route if no genuinely distinct feasible alternative exists.

This can merge with EI-03 and CE-03: their attention to preserved counterconsiderations and the loss caused by a change strengthens the comparison. It implements the existing instruction to compare moves in context; the incremental hypothesis is the conditional, executable dependency test. It permits coordinated paragraph or section rewriting when that is the authorized improvement.

## RL-CH02 → VN-05: revise

An explanation is not a request for homework. A writer asking why a condition moved should receive the answer alongside the finished revision, not a new exercise.

**Revised rule:** If asked, explain a consequential edit briefly using actual wording and the reader consequence. Offer a transfer exercise only when the author requests instruction or practice, or a previously established learning workflow calls for it, and defer it until the deliverable is complete. When practice is requested, let the author attempt first and decline freely; do not treat acceptance as evidence of learning.

This can merge with RL-04. Explaining revisions and testing transfer are inherited teaching ideas; separating a rationale from an opt-in exercise is the concrete behavioral refinement.

## Disposition of the remaining proposals

- **VN-01 survives, narrowed:** distinguish observation, author position, open question and model inference. Offer competing questions only when they are genuinely different, supported and useful; otherwise develop the best-grounded one. Do not interrupt when the intent is clear.
- **VN-04 survives for a potential merge with EI-02 and EI-05:** when stronger thinking is requested, test a material alternative or boundary against a specific claim, say what evidence would distinguish it, and revise scope or reasoning when warranted. Preserve author endorsement. In particular, reject a seemingly helpful rewrite that introduces an unsupported causal or procedural dependency. A model's possible objection is not an established fact.

The remaining tension is substantive: an open-ended improvement request can authorize a model to make a strong, coordinated revision without a permission pause. When equally plausible interpretations would materially alter the author's belief or intended experience, the choice must remain visible rather than be silently settled. Reader task performance, author preference, fidelity and actual learning remain separate outcomes.
