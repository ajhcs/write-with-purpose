# Critical reviews of works and theory/conceptual papers — bounded corpus pass

Snapshot: 2026-11-16 UTC. The target was 200 eligible examples **per form**. Neither form reached it. The records preserve discovery, source-label screening and measurement as separate stages; none has received a complete editorial suitability review. See `records.jsonl`, `frontiers-records.jsonl`, `frontiers-index.json` and `historical-candidates.jsonl`. The earlier interrupted `first-pass-records.jsonl` is retained as provenance only; it used a broader product-element search and must not be merged into results.

## Selection and current state

- **Reviews of works:** Europe PMC search for `PUB_TYPE:"Book Review" AND OPEN_ACCESS:Y`, descending Europe PMC citations, first 250 records from 40,960 matching records. **67** have a JATS `book-review` label, reviewed-product metadata in article metadata, and measurable body text. **183** remain candidates, including 65 retrieval/parse failures and 18 missing/short bodies. Label agreement is a useful source check, not an independent determination that the review is a strong writing model. Other JATS `book-review` results without product metadata are not counted as eligible: e.g., the highly cited research article “Estimates of penetrance for recurrent pathogenic copy-number variations” is incorrectly labeled `book-review`. The sample mainly reviews medical/scientific books; it does not represent reviews of art, films, or other works.
- **Conceptual title search:** Europe PMC `TITLE:"conceptual framework" AND OPEN_ACCESS:Y`, descending citations, first 250 of 1,309 matching records. **2** carry an explicit conceptual/theoretical JATS category, pass a basic empirical-section-heading exclusion, and have measurable body text. **248** remain candidates; 47 retrievals failed. A title alone cannot rule out an empirical study or establish genre.
- **Publisher-defined conceptual analyses:** 40 paginated pages of the Frontiers in Psychology *Theoretical and Philosophical Psychology* section published through 2026-09-24 yielded **98** unique publisher-labeled Conceptual Analysis cards. Europe PMC metadata matched 98 cards by DOI; two extra search results could not be matched and are excluded from counts. Of the 98, **20** have the corresponding conceptual/theoretical XML category, pass the basic heading exclusion and have measurable prose; 78 remain unmeasured or unverified (31 retrieval/parse failures, 2 without retrieved XML, 45 with XML but failing the category/heading screen). This is a specialist publisher-selected sample, not a most-cited or globally representative sample. There is no overlap between these 20 and the two conceptual title-search measurements. Both are source-label screens: manual assessment is still needed to exclude empirical or methods-centered papers and establish suitability.
- **Historical library leads:** `historical-candidates.jsonl` holds **600 unmeasured candidates**, 200 each from OpenAlex's citation-sorted pre-2015 records for the New York Review of Books, *Theory & Psychology*, and *Academy of Management Review*. OpenAlex identifies 29 of the latter two as book reviews, so they are flagged for the review-of-a-work form, not as conceptual papers. The remaining 200 NYRB records need verification of the specific work reviewed; NYRB also publishes essays and other material. The 371 remaining journal records need screening for actual conceptual argument versus empirical work, research synthesis, and methods. The file records source type, DOI or landing page, citation count/provider/date, and OpenAlex access status; these are library leads, not verified eligibility. OpenAlex marks all 200 NYRB leads closed, 174 of 200 *Theory & Psychology* leads closed, and 184 of 200 *Academy of Management Review* leads closed. Access metadata is not proof of library availability. Citation counts for a book-review record can be conflated with citations to the reviewed book, so treat especially large values skeptically.

The substantive publisher definition is [Frontiers Conceptual Analysis](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/sections/theoretical-and-philosophical-psychology/for-authors/article-types): a novel argument, interpretation, model or critique examining concepts; that journal excludes original unpublished data. The formal [JATS book/product review guidance](https://jats.nlm.nih.gov/articleauthoring/tag-library/1.3/chapter/tag-review.html) locates reviewed-product metadata within article metadata. The [JATS article-type definition](https://jats.nlm.nih.gov/articleauthoring/tag-library/1.4/attribute/article-type.html) distinguishes book reviews from research-literature reviews. These definitions establish selection rules, not universal form boundaries or quality standards.

## Measurement policy and limitations

`collect.py` retrieves openly accessible Europe PMC JATS XML transiently; no full texts are redistributed. It counts Unicode alphabetic word tokens in body `p` elements, including paragraph text inside lists, while excluding figure, table, box, supplement, display-formula and reference-list subtrees. Title, abstract, captions and back references are excluded. Top-level section headings and their nested paragraph word counts are retained per document; direct body paragraphs need not belong to a section, so section totals may not sum to the document total. Sentence splitting is a punctuation/capital heuristic, not validated sentence parsing. Paragraph and sentence medians are within-document, then summarized across documents. Character length includes token-internal apostrophes/hyphens. XML citation cross-reference counts are occurrences, not unique sources, examples, quotation counts or verified claims. Figure, table and list element counts are retained, not their prose proportions. The source text SHA-256 hashes the newline-joined extracted paragraphs, not raw XML. Non-English material, abbreviations, formulas, malformed markup, shared xrefs and nested structures can distort measures. The code cannot establish rhetorical function, argument quality, audience fit or whether a work was read and evaluated critically.

Full-text availability and retrieval are distinct: open-access flags do not ensure Europe PMC XML; 10-second requests failed frequently. The publisher-index pool also includes recent publications whose XML may not yet have arrived. Many paywalled historical works are outside the measured population. Citation counts change, are database-specific, and are discovery signals, not measures of quality or global rankings. This bounded medical/psychology-heavy open-text convenience sample cannot establish genre-wide norms, representative periods or disciplines. Reproduce with `python3 collect.py`, `python3 frontiers_collect.py`, and `python3 historical_collect.py` from this directory; network timeouts, live databases and citation changes will change results. The publisher-index collector paginates an editorial section, not a publisher-wide or ranked catalogue.
