# Ranking and retrieval resources

Checked 16 November 2026. These resources answer different questions; none independently proves that a work is a good writing exemplar. The queue is based on Europe PMC results and is **not** a Web of Science, Scopus, ESI, OpenAlex or Crossref ranking.

- [Europe PMC REST search and sort](https://dev.europepmc.org/RestfulWebService): `SORT_CITED:y` orders search results by the provider's citation counts, highest first. Filter an explicit form cue, date and language, record the exact query, retrieve the `core` metadata and screen the full document. Coverage is weighted toward life and biomedical sciences. The raw counts are not field/year normalized and cannot rank all writings worldwide.
- [Clarivate ESI overview](https://esi.help.clarivate.com/Content/overview.htm?Highlight=esi+essential+science+indicators): Highly Cited Papers are the top 1% of papers in the same field and publication year within ESI's defined database. ESI covers regular articles and reviews, uses 22 broad fields, and excludes many other document types. Its approximately ten-year rolling window is particularly unsuitable as the sole route for the requested older material. Institutional access may be required. Record the ESI edition, field, year and source when used; do not label an ordinary Europe PMC high-count result ESI highly cited.
- [Scopus document search results](https://service.elsevier.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/11423/): query a carefully defined form, year and subject, then select “Cited by (highest)”; citation counts are citations indexed by Scopus. Save search expression, filters, retrieval date, titles, DOI and counts; library access may be required. Scopus and Europe PMC have different source coverage, so counts are not interchangeable. [Citation overview](https://tutorials.scopus.com/EN/AuthorDetails/sc_AuthorDetails_textOnly.html) allows inspection of citing records.
- [OpenAlex work attributes](https://help.openalex.org/data/works/attributes/): `cited_by_count` counts matched citing works; year percentile and field-weighted impact are distinct fields. Record the actual version, snapshot, query, field/type filters and values. Matched references and underlying coverage determine counts; the results are not automatically equivalent to ESI. Confirm current API access terms before planning automated use.
- [Crossref Cited-by](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/cited-by/cited-by-participation/): citation links derive from deposited references matched to registered DOI metadata; deposit and matching influence coverage. Publicly available counts are useful as a second signal but are neither comprehensive nor equivalent to Scopus, Europe PMC or ESI. Crossref is not a full-text entitlement service.
- [PLOS article metrics](https://plos.org/metrics/) and [usage-data definitions](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/usage-data-help): article pages provide views and downloads, useful for a publisher-bounded most-accessed list. They count requests across formats, and the same user can request multiple versions. A view or download does not prove reading, quality, or impact; the measure does not cover readership on other platforms. Record publisher, format, time window and snapshot separately from citations. A [PLOS Biology historical editorial](https://biologue.plos.org/2015/03/02/metrics-and-impact-looking-beyond-research-articles/) identifies its most-read 2014 article, an example of a clearly bounded list rather than a universal ranking.

## Library workflow

Search a DOI from the queue in the institution's catalog/link resolver. If an institutional license or interlibrary loan provides legitimate access, obtain the version available under its terms, record the source/version/rights and store restricted full text only in an approved local location. Verify complete main body, original article type, editorial suitability, publication date and current retraction or correction status before measurement. Do not share credentials, bypass a paywall, or redistribute restricted full text. A Europe PMC `isOpenAccess=N` flag is a provider metadata observation, not proof that no repository version, freely accessible version, or institutional access exists. The present queue does not test entitlements or access outside Europe PMC and does not establish whether the provider has source XML for any given item.
