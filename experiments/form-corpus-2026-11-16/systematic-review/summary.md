The query selects open-access records whose title contains “systematic review” and for which Europe PMC reports full text; it misses eligible reviews with other titles or unavailable XML and favors a biomedical, citation-rich sample. The screen excludes title signals for scoping reviews, protocols, umbrella reviews, reporting guidance and certain methodological articles. The remaining full-text XML must have body text, recognizable automated search and eligibility-method signals, at least 500 counted prose words and five paragraphs. These are heuristic checks, not human confirmation of form, quality, completeness, or fitness as writing models. No deduplication algorithm was applied; the measured records have distinct PMC identifiers. Hybrid articles can contain additional analyses. No claim of unique study populations is made.

Era groups overlap the method-setting groups and are shown separately to show differences by publication period. Meta-analysis is a method setting. Positive title/abstract patterns identify a with-meta-analysis stratum; explicit statements of non-performance identify a without-meta-analysis stratum. All other cases are uncertain, including unrecognized network or other meta-analyses. Do not interpret uncertain as without meta-analysis. Automated labels require review against full methods before use in comparative conclusions.

## Measurement policy and limits

XML is parsed from the Europe PMC fullTextXML endpoint. Main prose comprises text in body `<p>` elements, including prose in subsections, excluding lists, captions, figures, tables, boxes, formulas, supplementary material, reference lists, labels and cross-reference text. Whitespace is normalized. Words are Unicode alphanumeric sequences with internal apostrophes or hyphens; character counts sum word characters, including retained internal punctuation. Title and abstract words are separate. Captions are counted separately; references are counted as `<ref>` elements. Figures and tables are body `<fig>` and `<table-wrap>` element counts; lists are body `<list>` counts. List words separately count top-level prose lists, including nested lists once, while excluding figure/table/caption/supplement lists; list count includes all body lists. Citation markers count body `<xref ref-type="bibr">` elements, not cited works or fully resolved placements. Object and cross-reference counts can include objects inside other body structures even where their prose is excluded. Except for references, these object counts do not cover artifacts outside the JATS body.

Sections are XML `<sec>` nodes with recorded heading, document order and depth. Each counted paragraph is assigned to its closest section; unsectioned body prose is assigned separately. `section_words` records direct assignments, so parent sections do not include child-section totals and percentages can be calculated against `main_words`. Duplicate headings are kept distinct by numeric IDs. The sentence heuristic splits on terminal `.`, `!`, or `?`, followed by whitespace and an uppercase letter, digit or opening parenthesis; abbreviations, initials, decimals, unusual casing and citation formatting can cause errors. These mechanical signals do not establish rhetorical function, comprehension, or writing quality.

`manifest.jsonl` records screened candidates, source IDs, metadata, citation counts, outcomes, and XML SHA-256 hashes for measured articles. `metrics.jsonl` has article-level structural measurements; `aggregate.json` records selection and distributions. `build.py` performs discovery and measurement; `recompute.py` remeasures the original selected cohort from cached XML and checks hashes; `summarize.py` renders this summary. Source XML remains outside this repository at `/workspace/tmp/builderbob/writing-corpus/systematic-review`; only metadata and derived measurements are recorded here. The query snapshot is cached there as `search.json`. Live retrieval may change or fail; cached SHA-256 values bind the measurements to the retrieved XML, not to a permanent publisher version.
