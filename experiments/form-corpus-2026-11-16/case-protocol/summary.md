# Clinical case reports and protocols: provisional corpus

Retrieval: 2026-11-16 UTC, Europe PMC REST search and open-access full-text XML. These are citation-ranked discovery samples, not rankings of writing quality or representative random samples. The main sample keeps the first 200 measurable bodies in each search's returned citation order. `search.json` and each manifest record retain the precise query, selection basis, publication date, citation count, source identifiers, canonical link, XML hash, and retrieval status. `SORT_CITED:y` is in each query; the API response's separate `sort` field is blank. Citation counts in each original stratum were checked and were non-increasing. Citation counts are time-sensitive.

Publication-year bands use Europe PMC `pubYear`, falling back to first-publication date. The full attempted search set per stratum is 250, including failed retrievals; cases: 239 measured, eight failed transfers and three missing bodies; study protocols: 248 measured, one transfer failure and one missing body; review protocols: 250 measured. The first 200 measured are the analysis cohort; the remaining attempts are retained with `included_in_cohort: false`. Review any transfer status before interpreting it as inaccessible. Numbers in parentheses are empirical quartiles, not minimum/maximum limits or recommended targets. More derived features and ranges are in `metrics-summary.json` and the record-level manifest.

**Eligibility is unresolved.** The clinical query uses the NLM/Europe PMC Case Reports publication type and is limited to biomedical articles; it is not a corpus of case studies across disciplines. Among the 200 candidates, the JATS publisher article-type field is `case-report` for 31, `research-article` for 104, `letter` for 28, `brief-report` for 26, `review-article` for eight, and `article-commentary` for three. Therefore NLM indexing alone does not establish that all 200 are suitable individual-patient case-report writing models. The protocol samples use publisher titles containing the selected phrase, not an independently verified prospective plan: among 200 study candidates, 24 have JATS `protocol`; among 200 review candidates, 35 do. JATS is optional, open-text and publisher-specific; absence of `protocol` does not itself make a title-labeled item ineligible. Manual inspection should reject completed studies, completed reviews, comments, incorrect labels and other mismatches and verify prospective intent. For orientation only, median body words in the explicitly matching JATS subsets are 1,908 for the 31 `case-report` items, 4,841.5 for the 24 study `protocol` items, and 2,404 for the 35 review `protocol` items. These subsets are small and still require eligibility review. No automated metric here establishes CARE, SPIRIT or PRISMA-P compliance.

The body word total counts words in JATS `<p>` elements, including paragraphs in lists, after excluding figures and tables (including captions), references, abstracts, acknowledgements, appendices and supplements. Abstract and caption word counts and reference entries are recorded separately. Word, sentence and paragraph measurements are mechanical approximations; sentence boundaries do not reliably resolve abbreviations. Section counts include nested sections; their heuristic heading categories do not prove communicative function. Section word counts use nearest-section paragraphs; unsectioned paragraphs remain in body total. Figure, table, list, code, quotation and bibliographic cross-reference counts reflect tagged XML and may miss untagged or differently tagged material. Citation cross-references count XML xref elements, not distinct citations or claims supported. These measures cannot establish quality, role fulfilment, correctness, or reader vocabulary comprehension. No article body text is redistributed in this directory; XML is cached separately under `/workspace/repos/builderbob/.writing-corpus-cache/case-protocol/` and rights attributes remain source-specific.

Authority and scope: [NLM publication types](https://www.nlm.nih.gov/mesh/pubtypes.html), [NISO JATS article-type](https://jats.nlm.nih.gov/articleauthoring/tag-library/1.4/attribute/article-type.html), [CARE reporting checklist](https://www.care-statement.org/checklist), and [Europe PMC REST service](https://europepmc.org/RestfulWebService). These establish candidate labels and reporting contexts, not the observed ranges. The separate pre-2015 supplemental search and its attempts are documented in `supplement-search.json`; supplement items are labeled `supplement_pre2015` and do not replace the original cohort. After excluding records already attempted in the main sample, it measured 58 of 60 additional clinical-case candidates, 60 of 60 study-protocol candidates, and 43 of 43 review-protocol candidates. The review query returned only 68 hits in all, of which some were already attempted; it cannot supply 200 additional records under this exact title query. Across primary attempts and supplements, measured pre-2015 records total 136, 157 and 68 respectively; none of these form strata reaches 200 pre-2015 records. These discovery samples remain unreviewed for suitability.
