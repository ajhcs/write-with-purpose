# Historical personal-writing discovery queue

This is a **316-record discovery queue**, not a corpus of 316 verified personal essays. It contains 305 Longform essay-tag records dated before 2015 in Longform's display, plus 11 manually transcribed entries from Robert Atwan's [Notable Essays and Literary Nonfiction of 2014](https://www.hmhco.com/~/media/sites/home/at-home/featured-shops/by-brand/best-american/2015-docs/2015-notables/ba%20essays%202015%20notables.pdf?la=en). All are distinct by normalized title plus author string. Full-text personal-form eligibility was assessed for **zero** of these records in this expansion. Individual texts may be criticism, argument, reporting, fiction, or book excerpts; identify and exclude out-of-scope works after reading.

The Longform subset has 252 distinct normalized author strings and 119 displayed outlet labels; all 305 have an author string and a captured discovery-page SHA-256. These are counts of metadata strings, not authority-controlled author identities or necessarily distinct people. No Paul Graham author-archive or Europe PMC biomedical proxy records are used. The most frequent Longform author string, John Jeremiah Sullivan, appears six times. Top displayed outlets are New Yorker (20), Harper's (12), New York Review of Books (12), The Atlantic (10), and four outlets at nine each. Outlet and author diversity here describe discovery, not a representative sampling frame.

[records.jsonl](records.jsonl) and [records.csv](records.csv) retain title, author, outlet, display date, selection evidence, discovery-page hash and destination for each record. A Longform destination is a link chosen by its editors; it may be a reprint or broken and is **not verified to be the original publication URL**. Three of the 11 Atwan leads have individually located publisher links; eight need article-level location. Publisher and library availability is unknown unless explicitly recorded. An earlier [source inventory](../../essays/longform_dated_candidates.jsonl) contains 2015–19 leads separately; this queue retains the requested historical preference.

The publisher's list is a separate editorial-notability signal, not a prize or a measure of readership. Eleven entries were selected manually as a bounded test; they are not a complete transcription. The PDF was inspected through a web reader, but direct binary retrieval timed out, so its binary hash is unavailable and is marked null rather than invented. Source page and line references accompany each transcribed seed. By contrast, [audit.json](audit.json) retains the hashes of the source inventory, discovery-page manifest and seed file. Longform page hashes and retrieval timestamps are copied per record. The Atwan list says July/August for “The Case for Reparations,” while [The Atlantic](https://www.theatlantic.com/magazine/archive/2014/06/the-case-for-reparations/361631/) shows a June issue; retain the discrepancy until the edition is resolved.

[Form-boundary examples](boundaries.jsonl) distinguish personal/memoir, argument, criticism and reporting as *provisional* categories based on a publisher label, a visible opening, or a stated position; none substitute for a full-text assessment. In particular, [A Paint-Factory Education](https://www.vqronline.org/fall-2014/memoir-essays/paint-factory-education) is a workplace-related memoir candidate and its publisher page requests a subscription. The 2014 notables entry “How It’s Done: A Criminal Defense Investigator at Work” is a law-workplace lead whose personal form remains unresolved. [The Case for Reparations](https://www.theatlantic.com/magazine/archive/2014/06/the-case-for-reparations/361631/) is retained as an argument boundary, not counted as a personal essay. Literary/personal is the selection context; professional and disciplinary subject labels have not been inferred from author or publication.

No article body was acquired or measured in this expansion, and no paywall was bypassed. To turn this into a useful personal-essay reference set, confirm original publication and year, obtain full texts through publishers or library holdings, assess experience and reflection alongside overlaps with argument or reporting, and audit decisions across readers. Source selection, citation/readership signals, form eligibility, content access and body measurement are separate fields and stages. Run `python3 -B experiments/form-corpus-2026-11-16/domain-expansion/personal/build_queue.py` to regenerate the metadata queue and audit from the retained inputs.
