## Scope and use

Sources were selected to contrast management theory, empirical research, mixed methods and practitioner communication. The [Academy of Management Review](https://journals.aom.org/journal/amr) describes its theoretical contribution mission; [Academy of Management Journal](https://www.aom.org/publications/journals/journal/) describes empirical research. [Strategic Management Journal](https://sms.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/hub/journal/10970266/homepage/productinformation.html) permits theory, testing, synthesis, and methods; [Organization Science](https://pubsonline.informs.org/page/orsc/editorial-statement) publishes research and conceptual perspectives; and [Management Science](https://pubsonline.informs.org/page/mnsc/editorial-statement) welcomes theoretical and empirical contributions on management and decision-making. These are publisher descriptions of venue scope, not classifications or peer-review assurances for each record. HBR is tracked separately as practitioner-oriented editorial material; its individual articles must be examined before calling them cases, recommendations, research reports, or any other form. [Harvard Business Publishing](https://hbr.org/about-hbp) describes its management-leadership mission. A Harvard teaching case and an HBR article are not assumed to be equivalent.

As a discovery rationale, the [University of Texas at Dallas research database](https://jsom.utdallas.edu/the-utd-top-100-business-school-research-rankings/list-of-journals) includes all five scholarly venues among the journals it tracks for business-school research output. That list supports venue selection; it does not establish the quality of an individual paper, cover every important business publication, or validate an article as a writing model. HBR is not presented as part of that scholarly-journal list.

OpenAlex's original work `type` is preserved exactly and is **not** equivalent to the project's writing form. For example, AMR's 27 `book-review` records are not conceptual-paper evidence merely because they appear in AMR; they may be useful candidates for reviews of individual works after verifying their targets. A `review` may be a literature synthesis, and `article` does not distinguish conceptual argument from empirical or methodological work. Editorials and paratext also appear. Code did not classify a record as theory paper, case study, business decision, or any other form, and no form is assumed to be present in all business fields.

Illustrative retrieval leads, **not form-verified exemplars**, include [*An Integrative Model of Organizational Trust*](https://doi.org/10.5465/amr.1995.9508080335) (AMR, 1995), [*Exploration and Exploitation in Organizational Learning*](https://doi.org/10.1287/orsc.2.1.71) (*Organization Science*, 1991), and [*A resource-based view of the firm*](https://doi.org/10.1002/smj.4250050207) (*Strategic Management Journal*, 1984). The HBR query includes *The Balanced Scorecard: Measures That Drive Performance* (1992); its indexed landing URL and authors are in the data and require publisher/library confirmation.

## Data and limits

[`records.jsonl`](records.jsonl) and [`records.csv`](records.csv) preserve title, indexed author names, publication date, full citation assembled from the available metadata, source/type, identifiers, volume/issue/pages where supplied, landing URL, OA fields, citation count/provider/time, exact query and provisional status. The assembled citations are **metadata-based, not publisher-verified**. Three records have no indexed authors; 193 records lack DOIs, all in the HBR subset. A landing page may be an indexing service rather than the publisher. OpenAlex flagged 1,031 of 1,200 records as closed, but this says neither whether a library has licensed access nor whether a lawful copy is actually accessible. Do not assume a text unavailable solely from this flag.

The query for each venue selects its top 200 OpenAlex records with `primary_location.source.id` matching that venue and `to_publication_date:2014-12-31`, sorted by `cited_by_count:desc`; complete encoded URLs, hit counts, source IDs, collection time and provenance appear in [`sources.json`](sources.json). OpenAlex citation counts are time-sensitive provider signals within the indexed venue, not global rankings, literary quality, form validity, or evidence of user suitability. In particular, indexing can conflate a review's citation count with that of the reviewed book; a high citation value is not confirmation of the cited object. Only the first 200 matching works per venue were sampled, so the queue is not comprehensive and excludes many less-cited pieces, small journals, books, non-English work, and contemporary practice. A source-level ranking can also reflect disciplinary and citation biases.

Reproduce with `python3 collect.py` in this directory. This makes live unauthenticated OpenAlex requests; results, metadata, access flags, and counts can change, and live service or shared unauthenticated quota may fail. Prior AMR matching depends on the historical file remaining at the path recorded in `sources.json`. As in the accepted foundations, the nine features remain measurement targets, not fixed profiles or rules; none is measured here. Library access and individual eligibility must be checked before calculating any distributions or using these works as models.
