# Historical engineering: library retrieval leads

This collection contains **350 DOI-bearing bibliographic leads** published or indexed by OpenAlex in 2000–2014, across seven journal queries in mechanical design, heat transfer, structural and construction engineering, electrical power systems, software engineering, and systems engineering. Each query contributed 50 leads. These are library-discovery records, not examined articles, validated genre examples, or a measured full-text corpus. They do not establish universal engineering-writing conventions.

The selection ranks each journal's matching articles by **OpenAlex's reported citation count**, not by downloads or the IEEE Xplore ranking. The exact query, source ISSN, retrieval time, provider rank, citation count and API response counts are recorded in `sources.json` and on each row. It is a purposive convenience sample of highly cited journal articles, with explicit 2000–2014 and venue boundaries, not a systematic field bibliography; older important work is omitted. Citation counts are time-dependent, source-dependent, affected by article age and field practices, and not direct evidence of quality or writing suitability. The [IEEE Xplore search results guide](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/Xplorehelp/searching-ieee-xplore/search-results-page) is an additional search aid, not the source of the ranks here. The guide could not be accessed by our web reader during this run.

`records.jsonl` and `records.csv` contain author lists, title, venue, volume, issue, page information when supplied, DOI, dates, OpenAlex work ID, citation count/provider, access indication, query URL, and provisional form label. Twenty-nine records lack a page or article number in the initial metadata and need library or publisher completion. Article-level OpenAlex type does **not** distinguish original empirical studies, methods papers, conceptual papers, standards, or narrative reviews. All form assignments remain unresolved. No practitioner standard or user guide is included in this journal-only collection; those need separate source populations and should not be mixed into research-article profiles. Duplicate OpenAlex IDs were removed inside this collection; duplication against other domain collections was not checked.

**Date and citation caveat:** OpenAlex's `publication_date` may reflect online availability rather than issue publication. Eight Crossref checks succeeded, and all eight checked titles matched; two have different OpenAlex and Crossref print years. A separate spot check also showed 2006 online versus 2007 print for the metamodeling review and 2010 indexed versus 2011 print for the MATPOWER article. An automated check received 342 HTTP 429 rate-limit responses; it was stopped without retries. The 342 unsuccessful records explicitly label their citation year as an indexed year whose issue year is unverified. Consult the DOI/publisher record and the library before final citation or classifying a work as pre-2015. For the eight successful records, Crossref's print or issued year is shown separately from the OpenAlex date; neither establishes when an underlying manuscript first circulated.

OpenAlex reports 317 records as closed, 25 green, seven bronze, and one hybrid. These are provider metadata, not checked access or permissions. Closed does not establish that a university library cannot supply a copy; an OA indicator does not prove a usable authorized full text is presently available. License flags are as provided by OpenAlex for the primary location and may be absent or refer to a particular version. No paywall was bypassed, no article text was downloaded, and no access claim has been tested.

`collect.py` reproduces the public OpenAlex selection against current metadata and counts; rerunning it replaces the snapshot with current results and does not recreate the Crossref augmentation. `crossref_check.py` is an optional deliberately rate-limited terminal-only spot check that stops on HTTP 429. The existing snapshot and recorded `crossref_error` values preserve the actual limited verification from this run. The [OpenAlex API](https://api.openalex.org/) and [Crossref REST API documentation](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/rest-api/) describe the providers; metadata is not a substitute for the source publication.
