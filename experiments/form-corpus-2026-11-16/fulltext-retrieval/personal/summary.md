# Personal-writing full-text retrieval

The 316-record historical discovery queue has an outcome for every record. [manifest.jsonl](manifest.jsonl) retains source metadata, each attempt and redirect, robots decisions, HTTP result, local path and SHA-256 where retained, and identity/body review flags. [manual_needed.csv](manual_needed.csv) is the complete review and library-access queue; its rows distinguish possible bodies from unavailable URLs and other failures.

The outcome counts in [counts.json](counts.json) include 61 HTTP 404 responses, 32 pages with a declared paywall, 17 HTTP 403 responses, five entries with no located article URL, one HTTP 429, and robots, network, redirect, and extraction deferrals. A refusal or paywall was not bypassed. The five missing URLs remain candidates for publisher searches, subscriptions, library holdings, or interlibrary loan. Some apparently accessible pages can still be incomplete or contain menus, commentary, author biographies, recommendations, or subscription text; word counts in raw extraction records are preliminary and must not be used as corpus metrics without checking boundaries.

Manual checks demonstrate why extraction counts are not full-text counts. Two different requested articles both redirected to the same unrelated culture.org homepage, and a Solstice page yielded an author biography and recommendations rather than its named essay. All three remain visible as captured files and are excluded from the 129 body candidates. The exact-URL, hash-verified earlier [On Self-Respect](https://www.vogue.com/article/joan-didion-self-respect-essay-1961) capture contains essay text and a closing paragraph along with an editorial introduction, but its original-edition and completeness status remain open. The publisher-linked 24-page [My Nazi Dagger](https://www.wlajournal.com/_files/ugd/134164_2a24522e6c234ce4bf25d3efcb3becc3.pdf) PDF has the expected title and author on its first page; spaced-out author typography caused the automatic surname test to miss it. Neither manual check establishes full completeness.

A targeted publisher search located [My Mother and Mercy](https://www.thesunmagazine.org/articles/24264-my-mother-and-mercy). A later [publisher correction](https://www.hmhco.com/~/media/sites/home/at-home/featured-shops/by-brand/best-american/2016-docs/2016-notables/ba_essays-notables.pdf?la=en) identifies its author as **Jaquira Díaz** and explains that her name was mangled in the 2014 notable list. The source queue's original misspelled author is preserved; the follow-up record retains the correction and the publisher page's title and author. Its body completeness remains unverified. The publisher's 10-second crawl delay was observed for that follow-up.

## Access-policy and quality audit

The initial HTTP client automatically followed some robots.txt redirects before applying the intended one-second per-host delay. The defect was detected, the run stopped, and the code changed to pace same-host redirects and defer cross-host robots redirects. **217 record traces contain an earlier automatic robots redirect**; this figure counts affected traces, including reused robots results, rather than asserting 217 separate redirect events. The error cannot be undone and must not be described as a fully compliant initial run.

A subsequent [robots audit](robots-audit.jsonl) rechecked source robots metadata for retained files without fetching article bodies again. Among the 129 candidate bodies, 86 have unchanged robots bytes and no detected `ai-input=no` content signal on recheck; this does not undo the earlier pacing error or prove a right to redistribute the work. The remaining 43 need policy review: 39 have cross-host robots redirects the corrected checker deferred, three have changed robots bytes, and one reused capture lacks capture-time robots rules. Current rules, terms, and permitted use still need verification before further processing.

Source bytes are held outside the repository at `/workspace/repos/builderbob/.writing-corpus-cache/fulltext/personal/`. The repository contains only structured metadata, derived counts, scripts and the review queue. PDF parsing uses the installed `medsci-pdf-python` runtime; no source code was executed from acquired articles. `retrieve.py` checkpoints attempts, `audit_robots.py` records the later policy recheck, and `finalize.py` verifies retained hashes and regenerates the manifest and review queue. Respect current robots terms, crawl delays, host circuits and source permissions before re-running acquisition.
