# Where the seventeen forms still need evidence

This is an applicability assessment of downloaded annotations and guides, not a census of texts eligible for each form. A source's broad genre, field, citation count or classifier label does not establish professional quality, readership, audience level or our exact form. Actual form eligibility and complete section/paragraph boundaries still need checking.

| Our proposed form | Useful downloaded ingredients | Gap before a form-specific profile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personal / reflective essay | GUM relations; AcaWriter reflective feedback patterns; BAWE family/stage guidance | Authentic personal experience, author voice and reader-specific reflection; school assignments and cue detection are insufficient |
| Argument / perspective | Webis editorials; microtext argument graphs; PERSUADE roles; GUM evidence and concession relations | Link evidence to claims, preserve opposing voices; distinguish editorial, perspective and student essay patterns |
| Critical review of a work | GUM evaluation/comparison relations; register metadata and family/stage definitions | Work-specific evaluation criteria, summary versus judgment, and complete professional reviews |
| Conceptual explainer | Definition, cause, example and elaboration annotations; scientific explanations | Beginner versus specialist explanations, terminology assumptions and non-research examples |
| Tutorial | DITA tutorial/action fields; WLP and OpenPI procedural structures | Learning progression, learner prerequisites and evidence of successful practice |
| How-to / procedure | DITA commands/results; WLP actions/participants; OpenPI state changes | Reader-directed complete tasks, executability and exceptions beyond lab protocols or excerpts |
| Reference entry | README section roles; DITA concept/reference/task distinctions | Complete lookup entries, parameter/option behavior and professional API/reference conventions |
| Empirical report | AZ full conference papers; MuLMS scientific annotations; GUM academic texts; legal role comparisons | Stratify discipline/study design and distinguish reported results, inference, claims and source attribution |
| Methods paper | Scientific method descriptions; DITA/WLP action distinctions | A paper introducing or evaluating a method is different from the Methods section of another paper |
| Theory / conceptual paper | Scientific hypotheses, assumptions, explanations and comparison relations | Complete theoretical argumentation and conceptual definitions; scientific zones alone do not identify this form |
| Case report | Legal event/fact annotations; narrative and interpretation relations | Legal court cases are not clinical or organizational case reports; preserve field-specific obligations |
| Study / review protocol | Scientific method descriptions; procedure prerequisites and state representations | Planned study/review methods, eligibility and reporting obligations; lab procedures are a different object |
| Narrative / critical literature review | AZ OTHER/BASIS/CONTRAST; GUM evidence/attribution; scientific synthesis | Whole-review organization, synthesis versus recap and complete professional texts with eligible form labels |
| Scoping review | General report/compare/interpret distinctions; available guide definitions | Explicitly eligible scoping-review sample, mapping purposes, scope decisions and complete section labels |
| Systematic review | General scientific and evidence relations; corpus/move-analysis methods | Explicitly eligible systematic reviews; study selection/synthesis/evidence certainty cannot be inferred from generic scientific roles |
| Proposal | BAWE purpose/stage definitions; AcaWriter configurable feedback; methodology and argument components | Complete professional proposals with actual audiences, decision criteria, feasibility and resource commitments |
| Decision memo | Argument/evidence/alternative comparisons; workplace intent and email-zone data; business-report methods | Real professional decisions, tradeoffs, recommendations and conditions; email intent and judgments are not memos |

The corpus downloads reduce the amount of new annotation we need. They do not eliminate deliberate selection for the particular form, audience, discipline and era. Use our collected professional works to test transfer and fill these specific gaps. Preserve multiple successful structures within a form rather than average them into one template.
