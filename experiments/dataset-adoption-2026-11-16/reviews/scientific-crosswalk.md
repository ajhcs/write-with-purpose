# Scientific labels compared with the twelve proposed paragraph functions

16 November 2026. [The accompanying JSON](scientific-crosswalk.json) records each original label, definition location, annotation unit, suggested mapping, needed context, counterexample and possible deterministic check. Mappings are hypotheses for human review, not automatic conversions. The writing harness and its catalogue remain unchanged. Multiple functions may coexist or span paragraphs; source labels applied to sentences or relations cannot establish a paragraph label by themselves.

The review inspected the [MuLMS-AZ annotation guide](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/boschresearch/mulms-az-codi2023/main/annotation%20guidelines/MuLMS-AZ_Guidelines.pdf), the [AZ scheme](https://aclanthology.org/E99-1015.pdf), [SciPara paper](https://aclanthology.org/2024.codi-1.2.pdf), and [SciDTB paper](https://aclanthology.org/P18-2071.pdf). It also counted original AZ values across the downloaded 80-paper archive and parsed all 50 MuLMS XMI files, and inspected a SciDTB dependency example. SciPara's pickle was not executed. All proposed mappings below require the full text and relevant surrounding context.

| Source label | Potential function | Necessary distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AZ `BKG` / MuLMS `Background` | report, define, orient or explain | General background is not always orientation; inspect its reader-facing role. |
| AZ `OTH` / MuLMS `Background_PriorWork` | report; sometimes compare or support | Preserve source attribution. A citation or faithful account of earlier work does not itself support a claim. |
| AZ `OWN` / MuLMS `Experiment`, `Exp_Preparation`, `Exp_Characterization` | principally report; possibly explain or instruct | Distinguish completed methods from actions addressed to a reader. An experimental-method label does not imply instruction. |
| AZ `CTR` | compare; sometimes qualify or orient | Novelty or criticism need not compare two alternatives on a shared dimension. |
| MuLMS `Explanation`, `Explanation_Assumption`, `Explanation_Hypothesis` | explain, assert, qualify or interpret | Its guide includes mechanisms, assumptions and unsupported post-hoc hypotheses; a proposed assumption is not automatically a mechanism. |
| MuLMS `Results`, `Conclusion` | report, compare, interpret or assert | Identify whether a result is reported, an inference is made, or another author's finding is summarized. A closing location is insufficient. |
| SciPara Supporting Sentence | explain, illustrate, report or support | Its “support” includes thematic elaboration and examples, unlike the harness's evidence connected to an identifiable claim. |
| SciDTB `elab-example`, `comparison`, `cause`, `explanation` relations | illustrate, compare, explain or support conditionally | Inspect both connected elementary discourse units, relation direction and affected claim; a relation is not itself a paragraph function. |

AZ long names in the 1999 guide correspond to the archive's serialized values. Local counts of `AZ` attributes are `OWN` 8,623; `OTH` 2,019; `BKG` 789; `CTR` 600; `AIM` 314; `BAS` 246; `TXT` 227. They include abstract and body sentence or item elements, not paragraphs. Counts are descriptive only.

MuLMS allows human annotation of parts of sentences, then projects labels to sentences for modeling. We observed 20,700 `MatSci_Sentence` elements across 50 XMI files; these include overlapping annotations and additional layers, not 20,700 unique sentences. For example, 1,470 elements carry raw `Exp_Preparation`, while the source paper reports 962 projected sentence labels. The two count bases cannot be substituted. The XMI includes `MEASUREMENT` and `QUAL_MEASUREMENT`, absent from the inspected AZ guide; their definitions and layer need further verification. Guide-only `SYNTHESIS PROCEDURE` has no located definition and no observed occurrence claimed. Structural `Abstract`, `Heading`, `Caption`, and related values are not paragraph functions.

SciPara studied only biomedical introduction and discussion paragraphs, and counted 724 Topic, 2,869 Supporting, 273 Concluding and 188 Transition sentences, plus 3 Off-Topic and 14 Redundant sentences. These are the paper's counts, not a local recount. Its definition of a “complete” discourse structure requires certain sentence types; it is specific to that study and is not a harness requirement or quality rule. The data lack a verified repository license. SciDTB's authors state that its underlying ACL source material follows CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 and CC BY 4.0; confirm the applicable license per source before redistribution. AZ is distributed under CC BY-NC 2.0 UK; MuLMS corpus is CC BY-SA 4.0 and its software AGPL-3.0, with original papers' rights recorded separately.

The datasets offer no universal mappings for all twelve functions. In particular, narration is not established by these source labels, and illustration, instruction and qualification demand contextual identification. Deterministic checks can validate label spelling, offsets, links, explicit metadata and count provenance; they cannot decide whether a source genuinely supports a claim or whether a passage explains, qualifies or instructs. The next useful work is independent paragraph-level coding of complete spans with source attribution and sentence boundaries retained, separate boundary and label agreement, and held-out domain/author evaluation. Published counts and classifier predictions are not writing targets or quality scores.
