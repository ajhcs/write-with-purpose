# Reuse existing writing research and data

16 November 2026. Existing work can replace substantial parts of a new raw-corpus and annotation effort. The strongest near-term path is to adopt documented metadata and original label schemes, then test a small crosswalk to the writing harness. The evidence remains an exploratory map, not a meta-analysis or proof of a universal writing profile. The eight scouts contributed 67 records, consolidated to [66 DOI-deduplicated records](merged-records.json); related releases and corpus-derived studies are linked sources, not independent samples. The [audit](audit/report.md) distinguishes verified facts, scope, licensing and unresolved counts. The [research retrieval list](paywalled-research.md) identifies studies about writing and methods for further reading.

The [integration proposal](reuse-plan.md) adds two separately verified system precedents: Iowa State’s Research Writing Tutor and UTS’s open-source AcaWriter. Their learning, example-exploration and context-specific feedback designs are directly relevant to the writing harness. Several corpus leads were already present in the project notes; this pass inspects their actual releases and adoption limits.

## What to inherit and adapt first

1. **BAWE genre classification.** [Gardner and Nesi's submitted manuscript](https://pure.coventry.ac.uk/ws/portalfiles/portal/3978905/gardnercomb.pdf) describes genre families through social purpose, stages and networks. Inherit those *fields* and preserve its 13 labels as an external comparison to the project's Family → Form → Section hierarchy. Adapt with a many-to-many, uncertain crosswalk; these are university student assignments, not professional norms. Text licenses and manuscript reuse terms require separate checks.

2. **GUM's structured text and rhetorical relations.** Its [pinned XML](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/amir-zeldes/gum/1d999e5a0327ecb83015007774f6a9be23ecbc41/_build/src/xml/GUM_academic_art.xml), [relation data](https://github.com/amir-zeldes/gum/tree/1d999e5a0327ecb83015007774f6a9be23ecbc41/rst/rstweb) and [guidelines](https://wiki.gucorpling.org/gum/rst) offer paragraph, sentence, EDU and relation layers. Inherit original IDs and relation labels and test alignment between layers. First exclude front matter from eligible body paragraphs; source text and annotations have distinct rights. GUM's eRST relations are not paragraph-function annotations.

3. **Webis-Editorials-16 for real public argument.** The [archive and release description](https://zenodo.org/records/3254405) provide 300 news editorials, paragraph-separator markers and six original human-labeled argumentative unit types. [Our importability proof](audit/reusable-data-proof.json) locally counted all 300 portal files and label rows. Adapt a parser that preserves markers, original types and outlet. The release reports 14,313 units while local code counts 14,334 substantive-type rows; the difference remains unresolved, so use neither as a reconciled count. The 4,664 separator rows are not automatically a count of full paragraphs. Check publisher rights, completeness, and boundaries before reusing text. This is a three-outlet editorial sample, not a general norm.

4. **MuLMS-AZ for scientific multilabel definitions.** Its [guidelines](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/boschresearch/mulms-az-codi2023/main/annotation%20guidelines/MuLMS-AZ_Guidelines.pdf) and [paper-level source metadata](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/boschresearch/mulms-az-codi2023/main/data/mulms_corpus/MuLMS_Corpus_Metadata.csv) offer material-science sentence roles, explicit source IDs and licenses, and multiple labels. Adapt definitions and boundary cases to a pilot without translating its categories into assumed paragraph equivalences. Source reports 50 papers; assess text and paragraph completeness before lengths. Corpus and software have different licenses.

5. **SciPara for paragraph context.** The [paper and dataset](https://aclanthology.org/2024.codi-1.2/) describe selected paragraphs and their sentence roles and topic information, with previous-paragraph context. Reuse the protocol and schema as an explicit comparison for context-sensitive span annotation. The 981 selected paragraphs from 62 articles are not complete papers, and the inspected repository did not establish a license. Verify access and rights before copying data; do not run untrusted pickle files.

6. **README section codes.** [Prana et al.'s CSV](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gprana/READMEClassifier/master/input/dataset_combined.csv) exposes IDs, headings and voted codes, and the [paper](https://ctreude.ca/pdf/categorizing-the-content-of-github-2019.pdf) documents the original What, Why, How, When, Who, References, Contribution and Other categories. Adapt these as a software-documentation-specific pilot, including multilabel and excluded cases. The classifier's merged labels differ from the original codebook. The 4,226 reported sections do not equal every CSV row. Preserve code versus original README rights.

7. **Operational move analysis.** [Upton and Cohen](https://doi.org/10.1177/1461445609341006) provide a worked protocol for 46 birthmother letters, while [Moreno and Swales](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.esp.2017.11.006) explain segmentation, category design and reliability in research discussions. Adapt their codebook structure, context rules and independent coding, not their genre-specific labels as mandatory functions. Their study-level reliability figures do not establish agreement in this project.

8. **Agreement and ambiguity.** [Kim, Qiu and Wang](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S277276612400003X) systematically reviewed 65 EAP genre studies and compared agreement measures using three researchers' annotations of 35 introductions; the underlying COSSRAI contains 600 introductions. The [Lu, Yoon and Kisselev COSSRAI study](https://pure.psu.edu/en/publications/matching-phrase-frames-to-rhetorical-moves-in-social-science-rese/) describes those manually move-step-annotated introductions across six disciplines; a downloadable gold corpus was not verified. Its phrase-frame categories can inform functional templates, but source wording should not become default drafting stems. Reuse the question of appropriate multilabel agreement after choosing shared units and missing-value rules. [RSTmulti](https://github.com/discourse-lab/RSTmulti) offers paired annotations for relation disagreements but shared segmentation cannot measure independent boundary agreement. Preserve disagreement instead of treating adjudication as certainty.

9. **Technical procedures.** The [WLP annotation example](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chaitanya2334/WLP-Dataset/master/train/protocol_100.ann) provides action offsets and links; the 2018 and 2020 WLP releases overlap and should not be summed. [DITA task elements](https://docs.oasis-open.org/dita/v1.2/spec/DITA1.2-spec.pdf) distinguish prerequisites, steps, results and examples. Adapt these as optional procedure fields and fixtures, checking text rights and treating a conforming structure as separate from a usable procedure.

10. **Legal and workplace comparison.** [Legal arguments](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/trusthlt/mining-legal-arguments/master/data/agent/001-100469.csv) separate actor attribution and span labels inside legal paragraphs. [Sappelli's workplace schema](https://cs.ru.nl/~msappelli/data/) describes independently coded email actions and expectations. Both help test attribution and disagreement; neither substitutes for professional decision memos. Check source rights and sampling before use.

11. **Register and hybrid cases.** [CORE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TurkuNLP/CORE-corpus/master/README.md) supplies register labels, document IDs and text; use its original labels and hybrid-vote logic to test the form taxonomy. Its unrestricted web texts are not a quality-selected sample; annotation license and webpage rights differ. [PERSUADE](https://github.com/scrosseye/PERSUADE_corpus) gives explicit span-oriented argument annotations and review procedures on school essays; use for boundary examples rather than adult-professional norms.

12. **Measurement implementations.** Compare [textstat](https://github.com/textstat/textstat) or [quanteda.textstats](https://github.com/quanteda/quanteda.textstats) counts on a small manually checked, rights-cleared fixture with headings, lists, citations and code. Pin version, tokenizer, denominator and language. Treat readability and audience knowledge as separate questions, and review tool, dependency and data licenses. The INCEpTION guide URL cited by a scout returned 404 and needs repair before adopting its documented workflow.

## Work that remains

Define complete function realization spans and their relationship to paragraphs; represent multiple functions, uncertain and missing labels without forcing a primary label. Build a small test crosswalk that preserves each source's original unit and labels, then independently annotate a rights-cleared, diverse sample and compare boundaries separately from function labels. Test against held-out authors and outlets. The prior calibration's 21/36 versus 18/36 exact-set agreement does not demonstrate improvement, and quoted evidence fragments are not complete spans.

For whole-document length, section proportions or presentation features, first establish text completeness, structural boundaries, eligibility, sample provenance and licenses. Specific gaps remain in complete professional business proposals, decision memos and policy writing. New, deliberately scoped collection may still be needed for those gaps, but it should follow reuse and rights review. The evidence supplies observations and methods, not universal ranges, quotas, a mandatory readability grade or a claim that existing schemes equal the project's functions.
