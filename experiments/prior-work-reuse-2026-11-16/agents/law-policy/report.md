# Legal and public-policy writing: reuse findings

The best available materials here support **function and relation annotation design**, not estimates of how professional proposals or decision memos are normally structured. Seven records in `findings.json` separate original claims, observable files, access, licenses and limits. No systematic review or quantitative meta-analysis specifically covering legal document genres was verified in this search; the 2024 manuals comparison below is a single study with a narrative literature review, not such a synthesis.

## Three strongest opportunities

1. **Argument boundaries and attribution.** [Habernal et al., Mining Legal Arguments in Court Decisions](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10506-023-09361-y) provides an explicit 16-type argument scheme and separate actor labels, with spans bounded by paragraphs but independent of sentence boundaries. Inspectable [token/BIO example](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/trusthlt/mining-legal-arguments/master/data/agent/001-100469.csv) shows what is actually released. Reuse the concepts of separate attribution, type, segmentation and context for F09 and evidence relationships. Its legal types, mandatory single labels within each layer, court-only sample and selected legal portions must not become generic paragraph functions. Triple-coded agreement was measured on batches, not the whole corpus. Resolve annotation license and Court source terms independently before redistribution.

2. **Relations that can target relations.** The [argumentative microtexts corpus](https://angcl.ling.uni-potsdam.de/resources/argmicro.html) supplies 112 controlled or teaching texts, professionally translated from German into English, plus a separate 178-text crowdsourced part. Its [actual XML](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/peldszus/arg-microtexts/master/corpus/en/micro_b003.xml) shows textual units, argumentative units and typed directed edges, including an edge directed to another edge. Use the annotation guide and graph format to test whether support, rebuttal and qualifications need relation targets beyond adjacent spans. Part 1 is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 per its [license](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/peldszus/arg-microtexts/master/LICENSE); policy-topic prompted microtexts are not professional decision memos or proposal samples. Parallel RST/SDRT layers offer a direct way to keep discourse and argumentative relations distinct.

3. **Judicial role definitions and context.** The [2019 Indian Supreme Court corpus](https://github.com/Law-AI/semantic-segmentation) includes 50 human annotated cases and seven role labels; its [tab-separated text example](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Law-AI/semantic-segmentation/master/data/text/1953_L_1.txt) exposes sentence segmentation and at least one unlabeled row. Use it for role-boundary challenges, sequence context and missing-label handling. Separately, [BUILD](https://github.com/Legal-NLP-EkStep/rhetorical-role-baseline) gives more detailed 13-role definitions and a declared CC BY-SA dataset license, but the direct hosted dev file returned HTTP 401 in this check, so data format and access remain unverified. Repository code licenses do not independently establish rights to underlying judgments. Both sets describe judicial decisions, not legal articles or policy proposals.

## New work still required

Identify and independently clear complete, professionally authored policy proposals and decision memos; sample by authoring setting and purpose. Develop and test a crosswalk from existing operational definitions to the accepted paragraph/span functions, retaining original labels, multiple functions and unresolved cases. Check segmentation and attribution disagreements separately from function classification, with repeated independent annotation. No source here establishes paragraph function lengths, a universal primary function, or normative thresholds. For the pre-2015 foundation, [Hachey and Grover (2006)](https://www.ltg.ed.ac.uk/np/publications/ltg/papers/Hachey2006Extractive.pdf) describe the nested judgment/speech/paragraph structure and human sentence-role annotations for 69 of 188 UK decisions; actual annotated files and rights were not located. The [2024 judicial manuals and corpus comparison](https://doi.org/10.3390/languages9040119) supplies a useful way to compare advice with observed practice and backreferences; it does not generalize beyond its sampled opinions.

## Library retrieval candidate

Girolamo Tessuto (2015), “Generic structure and rhetorical moves in English-language empirical law research articles: Sites of interdisciplinary and interdiscursive cross-over,” *English for Specific Purposes* 37, 13–26, [doi:10.1016/j.esp.2014.06.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.esp.2014.06.002). Publisher preview reports 90 randomly selected papers from three journals, examines IMRD variation and moves. Abstract, introduction and data-collection snippet were inspected; direct full-text open returned HTTP 403, and no legitimate author copy was verified. Request through a library before extracting detailed methods or treating this as a reusable annotated corpus. It is a single descriptive study, not a systematic review.
