# Scientific rhetorical roles and structural corpora

16 November 2026. This is a targeted evidence map, not a meta-analysis. Full records, actual artifact links, source scopes and rights caveats are in [findings.json](findings.json). Numbers are source-reported unless explicitly marked as a repository count. Mappings to the nine features are hypotheses, not adoption of these schemes.

## Three strongest opportunities

1. **[MuLMS-AZ](https://github.com/boschresearch/mulms-az-codi2023)** supplies an unusually actionable materials-science comparison: 50 CC-BY source papers across seven subfields; human multilabel sentence roles; a [full annotation guide](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/boschresearch/mulms-az-codi2023/main/annotation%20guidelines/MuLMS-AZ_Guidelines.pdf); explicit offsets, paper IDs and splits; and [paper-level license metadata](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/boschresearch/mulms-az-codi2023/main/data/mulms_corpus/MuLMS_Corpus_Metadata.csv). The corpus is CC BY-SA 4.0 while software is AGPL-3.0. Directly extract definitions, counterexamples, and ambiguity policy and test a tentative F09 crosswalk against held-out papers; preserve original multiple labels and report disagreement. The study reports transfer differs by label, so a classifier trained here is a domain-specific benchmark, not an editor or a source of required frequencies. Verify actual text and paragraph completeness before computing length.

2. **[SciPara](https://aclanthology.org/2024.codi-1.2/)** directly addresses the present gap between sentence tags and paragraph context. Its 981 selected paragraphs and 4,071 sentences from 62 papers have professional-editor annotations of topic information, sentence discourse roles and links. The [notebook](https://github.com/annamkiepura/SciPara/blob/main/Read_Dataset.ipynb) describes paragraph text, labels and previous paragraph. The paper reports agreement separately, including lexical overlap for topic spans; these values do not prove exact boundary agreement. Reuse the protocol and labeled examples to pilot function and span decisions and inspect mixed or unresolved cases. The repository has no identified license in the inspected files; verify rights before copying or training. Selected paragraphs are not full-document length observations, and sentences' roles are not automatically whole-paragraph roles. Do not load the pickle as trusted code.

3. **[Argumentative Zoning](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/~sht25/AZ_corpus.html)** provides 80 computational-linguistics conference articles with human sentence-level rhetorical zones, citation markup and self-citation indicators, in SciXML under CC BY-NC 2.0 UK. The [1999 scheme](https://aclanthology.org/E99-1015/) can inform provenance and knowledge-claim distinctions; the distribution describes one annotator per sentence, and abstract/body correspondences are semi-manual. Together with chemistry's [CoreSC and AZ-II](https://aclanthology.org/L10-1440/), this gives a disciplined way to keep *who owns a claim*, *what role a sentence plays*, and *what concept an investigation describes* separate. Original chemistry authors report 265 papers, but AZ-II should not be assumed to annotate all of them. The SAPIENTA project asks users to contact the authors for all ART papers, while the ELG metadata describes downloadable CC BY XML; actual distribution and rights need resolution.

[Dr. Inventor](https://aclanthology.org/L16-1492/) adds computer-graphics citation-purpose and advantages/disadvantages layers, but its live corpus distribution and license remain unverified. [SciDTB](https://aclanthology.org/P18-2071/) has 798 unique computational-linguistics abstracts, 1,355 annotated trees and repeat annotations for 506 abstracts. It supplies relations between elementary discourse units, not paragraph functions; the repo's license was not identified. [PubMed 200k RCT](https://github.com/Franck-Dernoncourt/pubmed-rct) has large heading-derived silver labels limited to structured clinical trial abstracts; no full-paper or human sentence-gold interpretation is justified. [SciTex](https://fedora.clarin-d.uni-saarland.de/scitex/) offers a historical, cross-disciplinary structural corpus worth checking for complete-document measurements, but its format, rights and completeness need validation. S2ORC, GROBID-derived sections, and SciTex document markup are structural extraction; those alone do not establish human rhetorical-role labels.

## What remains to do

Read the guides in full and preserve the source coding decisions, segmentation units, category definitions, sample selection and paper dates. Select a few accessible examples with documented text rights and independently annotate complete function spans with multiple labels or unresolved status allowed. Compare boundary and label disagreement separately; do not equate a quoted evidentiary fragment with the complete span. Group held-out data by author and publication venue, and report per-label results and class imbalance. Any numerical F01–F05 measurement requires verified complete article body, section and paragraph boundaries, treatment of abstracts/references, and sampling documentation. No observed distribution creates a target or limit.

For methodological synthesis, [Pearson and Abdollahzadeh (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lingua.2023.103561) systematically reviewed 370 metadiscourse studies and cautioned that marker counts often omit contextual interpretation. Its abstract and selected methods passages were inspected; direct publisher access returned 403 although the article is described as open access. This is a review of research, not pooled effects or a paragraph-function dataset.

For the library shortlist, the already-known [Kanoksilapatham (2005)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.esp.2004.08.003), *Rhetorical structure of biochemistry research articles*, remains relevant for historical complete-article move analysis; publisher preview was inspected in the baseline, full coding protocol still needed. [Moreno and Swales (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.esp.2017.11.006), *Strengthening move analysis methodology towards bridging the function-form gap*, is a methodological synthesis focused on sample choice, move coding, reliability and expert validation; publisher abstract inspected and a legitimate [author preprint](https://buleria.unileon.es/bitstream/handle/10612/10667/Strengthening_Move_Analysis_Methodology.pdf?sequence=6) located but not fully appraised. Neither is a statistical meta-analysis or a universal writing template.
