# Personal and public nonfiction: prior work worth reusing

16 November 2026. This is an evidence map, not a statistical meta-analysis. The seven records in [findings.json](findings.json) preserve original units, populations, access limits and license distinctions.

## Three strongest opportunities

1. **Webis-Editorials-16: actual published editorial structure.** The [release](https://zenodo.org/records/3254405) contains 300 editorials, equally split among Al Jazeera, Fox News and The Guardian, and 14,313 human-coded argumentative discourse units. The downloaded archive's README specifies plain-text rows with a unit number, label and text, and explicit paragraph separators; XMI records add source metadata. A time-ordered 180/60/60 split is provided. Three annotators labeled the units and the [2016 study](https://aclanthology.org/C16-1324/) reports Fleiss κ = 0.56. This is a concrete starting point for paragraph lengths and location of argument/evidence types, with labels retained verbatim. The Zenodo record declares CC BY 4.0 for the dataset; check publisher text rights separately. Verify text completeness, conversion, and paragraph separators before calculating; code additional functions and ambiguous boundaries if needed. Three outlets and one period do not establish a universal professional profile.
2. **CORE: existing register hierarchy and hybrid cases.** The [2022 open release](https://github.com/TurkuNLP/CORE-corpus) supplies a TSV with register labels, document ID and text plus a reusable [label dictionary](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TurkuNLP/CORE-corpus/master/register_label_abbreviations.txt). Labels distinguish personal blog, opinion blog, editorial, persuasive article/essay, review and instructional material. Its [paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10579-022-09624-1) explains four independent coders and a minimum two-vote rule allowing hybrid labels. Reuse the distinctions and vote logic to test whether a form, purpose or medium actually reduces uncertainty; retain original labels. CORE is an unrestricted web sample, not an excellence selection, and document register is not paragraph function. Audit full-text and paragraph preservation before measurement. Its license explicitly covers annotations; webpage rights require separate assessment. Original 2015 research and the nearly 50,000-document 2022 release are distinct counts.
3. **PERSUADE 2.0: operational span scheme and rating protocol.** The [repository](https://github.com/scrosseye/persuade_corpus_2.0) links data and an effectiveness rubric for more than 25,000 US school essays over 15 prompts. The [1.0 schema](https://github.com/scrosseye/PERSUADE_corpus) specifies full text, start and end offsets, and seven human discourse types; it describes independent double review and third-rater adjudication. Reuse the explicit boundary representation, definitions and review procedure to audit current proposals, and compare with professional-editorial coding. The 2.0 release adds holistic and element effectiveness ratings; their population is grades 6–12, so these are neither professional standards nor measures of authentic voice. The data files and full rubric were not downloaded here.

## Other useful comparisons

- [AAE v2](https://aclanthology.org/J17-3005/) extends the known 2014 baseline to 402 learner essays with argument components and support/attack relations. The indexed TU Darmstadt record identifies the ZIP and custom license, but the live host returned 403; license and actual data need inspection before use. Its [2023 semantic-type extension](https://github.com/discourse-lab/arg-essays-semantic-types) supplies offset annotations and guidelines, CC BY-SA 4.0 for that extension, but does not redistribute original AAE component annotations.
- The [2007 Brown-family manual](https://icame.info/icame_static/manuals/FLOB-Manual-tagged.pdf) provides matched genre groupings, structural markup explanations, tag methods and POS frequency tables across Brown, LOB, FLOB and Frown. Each comprises 500 approximately 2,000-word samples. It is useful for genre/register comparisons, not whole-document length or complete section profiles; sample truncation can also compromise boundary paragraphs. Texts were published in 1961 or 1991–92, not 2007.
- The known [BAWE manual](https://llds.phon.ox.ac.uk/llds/xmlui/bitstream/handle/20.500.14106/2539/BAWE.documentation.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=5) documents student assignment encoding and genre metadata. Keep 2,761 assignments distinct from 2,858 component texts and retain the 13 original families. Its successful assessed writing offers a student comparator, not publication-quality personal writing.

## Work still needed

Reconstruct and inspect a few complete Webis paragraph/annotation examples; compare unit boundaries to paragraphs and preserve their many-to-many relationship. For CORE, check whether text extraction retains full documents and paragraphs, sample source and outlet coverage, and resolve underlying content rights. For PERSUADE, read the actual rubrics and sample rows, then compare annotation reliability and uncertainty against the current calibration without treating adjudication as proof of general validity. Establish whether any existing scheme can represent unannotated, multiple-function and unresolved passages before replacing current coding. Maintain observed distributions separately from working targets and limits.

## Gated or restricted material

The 2012 Nesi and Gardner book and the 1994 Atkinson and Biber register review are already catalogued in the baseline; their publisher previews, not full methods, were inspected there. The present investigation found no new gated synthesis warranting a separate shortlist. AAE's dataset release/terms are a separate access limitation: its indexed record listed a custom PDF license, but the host returned HTTP 403 on follow-up. No conclusions about its permissions or continued availability follow from that failure.
