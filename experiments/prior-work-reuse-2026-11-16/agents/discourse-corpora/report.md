# Discourse corpora: reuse investigation

16 November 2026. Nine records and exact artifact links are in [findings.json](findings.json). These are corpora, tools and methodological studies, not a statistical meta-analysis. The existing foundations already named GUM, AMALGUM and RST-DT; this investigation inspected their schemas, examples, releases and terms. GDTB, GENTLE, DISRPT and the independent-coder corpus add useful layers and controls.

## Three strongest opportunities

1. **Import the original GUM eRST and text structure as separate layers.** At [commit 1d999e5](https://github.com/amir-zeldes/gum/tree/1d999e5a0327ecb83015007774f6a9be23ecbc41), the `rst/rstweb/` tree contains 301 `.rs4` files, including the 26 GENTLE challenge documents. The [sample rs4](https://github.com/amir-zeldes/gum/blob/1d999e5a0327ecb83015007774f6a9be23ecbc41/_build/src/rst/GUM_academic_art.rs4) directly records `segment id`, `parent`, `relname` and segment text; its header defines 32 relations such as `explanation-evidence`, `adversative-concession`, `elaboration-attribute`, `joint-sequence`, and `purpose-goal`. XML supplies `<p>`, `<s>`, headings, document metadata and source URLs. **A paragraph tag can hold author information:** the [academic sample](https://github.com/amir-zeldes/gum/blob/1d999e5a0327ecb83015007774f6a9be23ecbc41/_build/src/xml/GUM_academic_art.xml) contains names and affiliations inside `p`, so identify front matter before measurement. Preserve original labels, endpoints, signals, document IDs and paragraph boundaries; align via tokens and test alignment rather than assuming offsets are identical. The 301 count is a file count, not a count of complete eligible works. This gives inherited human rhetorical links and text segmentation, **not human labels for our paragraph jobs**. [Guidelines](https://wiki.gucorpling.org/gum/rst) and [layer documentation](https://gucorpling.org/gum/annotations.html) define what was actually annotated.

2. **Use the same texts' GDTB annotations as a distinct shallow-relation layer.** The [actual GDTB relation file](https://github.com/amir-zeldes/gum/blob/1d999e5a0327ecb83015007774f6a9be23ecbc41/rst/gdtb/disrpt/GUM_academic_art.rels) records both argument token spans, text, direction, relation type, `orig_label` and simplified `label`. For example, `contingency.cause.result` is preserved separately from `causal`. The [2024 paper](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.684/) explains that GDTB is produced by semi-automatic conversion of GUM's RST, followed by targeted manual correction; its test set received full correction. Thus it is useful to compare representations, but it is **not an independent coder's second opinion**. On 1,531 test relations, exact type, label and span match to the corrected set was micro-F1 .9218; an eight-document double-annotation experiment focused on implicit instances produced level-3 sense κ=.77. These figures concern different tasks from paragraph function. Its complementary `arg1`/`arg2` relations may help identify evidence, contrast or causal explanation, but each proposed relationship to a reader job needs manual testing and may be many-to-many.

3. **Use RSTmulti to study ambiguity and disagreement, rather than treating adjudication as certainty.** [Hewett and Stede (2025)](https://aclanthology.org/2025.law-1.3/) publish paired trained-human analyses of English and German texts, [rs3 files, comparison outputs and scripts](https://github.com/discourse-lab/RSTmulti/tree/4e4eca188b19edc87ce801bc0760b25d31dd9e10). The study covers 156 texts including 26 RST-DT articles whose source data are not redistributed. Retain relation, attachment, nuclearity, direction and scope disagreements as separate fields. The paired analyses intentionally share EDU segmentation, so this resource **cannot establish independent span-boundary agreement**. Its English public examples are UN Security Council speeches; this is an uncertainty-method resource, not a professional writing norm. Repository licensing is CC BY-SA 4.0; independently check the original source rights.

## Other resources and boundaries

- [GENTLE](https://aclanthology.org/2023.law-1.17/) supplies 26 manually annotated, out-of-domain challenge documents across eight types, including three dictionary entries and two legal documents. It is valuable for testing edge cases, not estimating genre distributions. 
- [AMALGUM](https://github.com/gucorpling/amalgum) offers approximately four million tokens across eight genres. The historical 4,960-document count belongs to its specified v0.1 composition; v0.2 and extra folders must be counted independently. Its RST trees are machine annotations: reported relation score 44.07 on GUM test is a reason to treat labels as noisy candidates, not as independent human evidence. Texts are sampled/excerpted, with source and genre confounded.
- [DISRPT 2023](https://github.com/disrpt/sharedtask2023/blob/2afec39f8f32c00a238b8a33daf5b0c7341a31fa/data/eng.rst.gum/README.md) standardizes segmentation and relation evaluation, retaining `orig_label` alongside collapsed target labels. It is a collection of distinct source frameworks and rights, not a universal shared taxonomy. Some text is underscored pending licensed reconstruction.
- [RST-DT](https://catalog.ldc.upenn.edu/LDC2002T07) contains 385 WSJ articles and is licensed through LDC. [PDTB 3](https://catalog.ldc.upenn.edu/LDC2019T05) is also licensed; its [manual](https://catalog.ldc.upenn.edu/docs/LDC2019T05/PDTB3-Annotation-Manual.pdf) is public. [DiscAlign](https://catalog.ldc.upenn.edu/LDC2021T16) provides approximately 6,700 alignments between RST-DT and **PDTB 2**, available to licensees of both. These are overlapping news sources, not independent cross-genre samples.

The GUM release licenses annotations CC BY 4.0, but underlying text terms vary: for example, academic source text is generally CC BY, while essay and wikiHow samples carry noncommercial/share-alike conditions. Reddit text is omitted and reconstruction is subject to separate terms. Check every source and intended use, and distinguish code, annotation and text permissions. Public availability alone does not establish redistribution or commercial rights. See the [release license](https://github.com/amir-zeldes/gum/blob/1d999e5a0327ecb83015007774f6a9be23ecbc41/LICENSE.md).

## Work still needed

Select eligible complete texts by form and subject, audit rights and visible boundaries, then build a reproducible token-level alignment between structural XML and graph relations. Keep front matter, missing spans, discontinuities, text excerpts and uncertain alignment explicit. Test whether the reader benefits from distinguishing individual relation types in context; a text can contain multiple relations and multiple functions, and a relation spans linked units rather than naming the function of a paragraph. Any crosswalk to the twelve proposed jobs is a hypothesis, not inherited ground truth. Independently code a small stratified sample, retain coder-level labels and unresolved cases, and report segmentation and interpretation agreement separately. None of these resources establishes form-specific length targets or universal prose quality.

## Gated synthesis shortlist

No additional gated research synthesis was sufficiently inspected in this scout's corpus-focused search. LDC dataset licensing is described above; public manual access does not grant access to corpus text. The existing baseline's gated genre syntheses remain separate from these datasets.
