# Genre and move-analysis reuse: research report

16 November 2026. This is a targeted primary-source evidence map, not a systematic search or a new meta-analysis. Eight source records with exact scope, rights, units and links are in [findings.json](findings.json). Existing project leads are marked; newly inspected methods are distinguished from newly discovered studies. No corpus download, annotation or code release is claimed on the strength of a paper alone.

## Three strongest opportunities

1. **Reuse an actual pre-2015 coding workflow, with a complete worked protocol.** [Upton and Cohen (2009)](https://scholarworks.indianapolis.iu.edu/bitstream/handle/1805/2332/Approach_to_Corpus_based.pdf), DOI [10.1177/1461445609341006](https://doi.org/10.1177/1461445609341006), gives a seven-step method and Appendix 1's move/step definitions and examples. Its 46 birthmother letters provide a non-academic example of coding full text, analyzing individual spans, then comparing whole-document sequences. Particularly useful: two passages about pets have different functions depending on their context. Use the process and codebook organization as a model, not its letter-specific labels as generic paragraph functions. The study’s 84% illustration concerns a separate 242-letter direct-mail study, not its 46-letter sample. No underlying labeled release was verified.

2. **Reuse a detailed boundary, codebook and reliability procedure.** [Moreno and Swales (2018), institutional preprint](https://buleria.unileon.es/bitstream/handle/10612/10667/Strengthening_Move_Analysis_Methodology.pdf?sequence=6), DOI [10.1016/j.esp.2017.11.006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.esp.2017.11.006), includes segmentation guidance, definitions and bilingual examples, a 25-category table, training, repeated coding, independent coding and expert consultation. Their test-2 source-size-weighted mean agreement was 99.51% and mean kappa 0.71; a rare category had 99.84% agreement but kappa 0.38. These numbers are tied to the study’s coding-comparison procedure and cannot be compared to our 21/36 exact-set score. Notably, their logical presentation of functions is explicitly not a fixed observed sequence. Their mutually exclusive scheme was a design choice for research discussion sections; it does not justify imposing a single label in this harness.

3. **Keep family, stage and function distinct.** [Gardner and Nesi (2013), submitted manuscript](https://pure.coventry.ac.uk/ws/portalfiles/portal/3978905/gardnercomb.pdf), DOI [10.1093/applin/ams024](https://doi.org/10.1093/applin/ams024), describes thirteen families through educational purpose, generic stages and genre networks, including embedded genres. It explicitly notes that Empathy Writing lacks one shared generic structure. Reuse those fields and the source labels for a testable crosswalk; do not claim their student-assignment categories are professional norms. The manuscript permits personal non-commercial study but restricts reproduction and commercial use. BAWE's underlying text rights are a separate question.

## Other useful evidence and remaining work

[Kim, Qiu and Wang (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rmal.2024.100097) systematically reviewed 65 research-article genre studies and compared three agreement measures using three raters and 35 social-science introductions. It is directly relevant to multi-valued judgments. Only indexed publisher methods and abstract were inspected: full text returned 403. Choose a metric after defining shared units, multiple-label behavior, missing values and segmentation; record boundary and label disagreements separately. Full-text review and implementation checks remain necessary. Their corpus and results cannot directly certify our paragraph functions.

[Cuevas-Alonso and Míguez-Álvarez (2021)](https://www.mdpi.com/2304-6775/9/4/56) selected 44 studies from 334 candidate documents and includes an academic-study appendix with corpus details. Its marker frequencies refer to heterogeneous source texts and sections. Re-extract individual source studies by discipline, form and section; do not use the pooled means as writing targets. The article is CC BY 4.0, but this does not license the underlying corpora. Its data-availability statement says not applicable.

[Cotos, Huffman and Link (2017)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.esp.2017.01.001) reports a three-move, sixteen-step model of 900 Methods sections across 30 fields, with within- and between-discipline sequences. This is a corpus study, not a review. Obtain full methods and data-access terms before using its labels or distributions; source text and annotation release have not been verified. The freely listed [Bawarshi and Reiff (2010)](https://wacclearinghouse.org/books/referenceguides/bawarshi-reiff/) reference book offers chapters on workplace and public genres and an annotated bibliography for expanding beyond academic writing; direct fetches of two chapter PDFs failed in this session, so their contents were not appraised.

New work is still needed to define function-span boundaries, represent overlap and uncertainty, independently annotate comparable examples, measure agreement on both boundaries and labels, and evaluate usefulness on held-out authors and outlets. These studies do not provide validated paragraph-function length distributions across the requested forms, and no numeric observed range becomes a target or limit. Search gaps include professional decision memos, law/policy and personal essays; a negative finding here is not evidence that prior work does not exist.
