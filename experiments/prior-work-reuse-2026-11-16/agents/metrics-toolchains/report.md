# Reusable measurement and annotation tools

16 November 2026. This is a source-checked evidence map, not a meta-analysis. [Detailed records](findings.json) distinguish inspected material, license uncertainty, and proposed feature mappings. No tool was installed or run and no numeric writing profile was validated.

## Three strongest opportunities

1. **Keep raw measurements and test implementations independently.** [textstat](https://github.com/textstat/textstat) exposes sentence, word, character and syllable counts; [quanteda.textstats](https://github.com/quanteda/quanteda.textstats/blob/master/R/textstat_readability.R) documents definitions such as words/sentence and characters/word. Create a small manually counted, rights-cleared fixture with abbreviations, headings, lists, code, citations and technical terms. Decide scope and boundaries explicitly; compare the two tools at a pinned version, record numerator, denominator, tokenization, language and syllable method. This supports F01 and F04–F06. Paragraph segmentation, section structure/proportions, evidence and display inventories still need a structured source representation and new extraction. textstat's repository is MIT but dependencies and the 2,941-line [easy-word file](https://github.com/textstat/textstat/blob/main/textstat/resources/en/easy_words.txt) need separate rights/provenance review; quanteda.textstats is GPL-3.

2. **Reuse a real feature-table schema, not its sample as a genre norm.** [TAACO](https://github.com/LCR-ADS-Lab/TAACO) releases [packageTest.csv](https://github.com/LCR-ADS-Lab/TAACO/blob/main/packageTest.csv), whose inspected header contains lemma TTR, lexical density and adjacency indices, alongside code and examples. The sample is associated with ELLIPSE learner writing, not professional writing. Its v2.1 switch from CoreNLP to spaCy changes segmentation and other preprocessing, and the README explicitly says outputs differ: replicate only with exact version, model, data and options. Code is described as CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, and sample/COCA-derived resource rights require separate checking. Cohesion is not a communicative-function annotation. [TAASSC](https://github.com/kristopherkyle/TAASSC) is a possible F05 research comparator but its README's CC BY-NC-SA claim conflicts with GitHub's GPL-3 metadata; resolve before reuse. Its v1 and v2 also use different parsers and reference corpora. [TAALES](https://www.linguisticanalysistools.org/taales.html) demonstrates per-item and text-level vocabulary coverage diagnostics; its tool page asserts noncommercial terms. We did not independently run or reproduce published values.

3. **Use INCEpTION for independent annotation, with an external overlap-aware analysis.** Its [guide](https://inception-project.github.io/releases/38.6/docs/user-guide.html) describes separate annotators, curation, exports and pairwise per-feature agreement; UIMA CAS JSON retains custom annotation layers. The guide also says overlapping units may be skipped and its coding measures do not handle partial overlap suitably. Configure human-blind coding before adjudication, retain all separate spans, uncertainty and disagreements, preserve text offsets and schema version, and evaluate boundaries separately from multilabel function agreement. Build a documented span-aware analysis outside its default agreement view and test it on deliberate overlap and boundary disagreements. The platform code is Apache-2.0; annotation and source-text rights are separate. Operational definitions, coder training and reliability evidence remain new work. It does not resolve the existing 21/36 versus 18/36 calibration or supply missing full realization spans.

## Audience vocabulary and available norms

[Gardner and Davies' Academic Vocabulary List](https://academic.oup.com/applij/article-abstract/35/3/305/146569) is grounded in 120 million words of academic texts from COCA. Its [official site](https://www.academicwords.info/x.asp) describes a top-3,000-lemma list; the [downloaded-format family example](https://www.academicwords.info/download/families_user734.pdf) illustrates a different unit. Lemma/POS and family counts cannot be treated as interchangeable. The list can support a clearly scoped coverage analysis, subject to verified redistribution rights and lemmatization, but academic frequency is not what a particular audience knows. The textstat familiar-word list is also a historical formula component, not direct reader evidence. Audience suitability requires evidence from the intended readers and task. No cross-genre professional feature table or transferable audience grade was established in this search.

## Methodological synthesis shortlist

- Shi Huawei and Vahid Aryadoust, “A systematic review of automated writing evaluation systems,” 2023 issue, 2022 online, [doi:10.1007/s10639-022-11200-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10639-022-11200-7). The [institutional author version](https://repository.nie.edu.sg/server/api/core/bitstreams/223f6952-ce18-4df4-8f63-b51cd2f44141/content) was inspected at abstract and search-scope level: 105 Scopus papers, 1996–2020, heterogeneous educational contexts and insufficient domain-description validity. Full methods are not yet critically appraised. This is a methodological review, not a pooled claim that a single measure predicts quality.
- Minjin Kim, Yuan Qiu and Yuanheng Wang, “Interrater agreement in genre analysis: A methodological review and a comparison of three measures,” 2024, [doi:10.1016/j.rmal.2024.100097](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rmal.2024.100097). Known baseline: 65 EAP genre studies. Publisher indexed excerpt inspected; direct page returned HTTP 403, so full methods and fit to overlapping spans remain unverified. Obtain a legitimate version or library access before choosing measures on its authority.

[L2SCA](https://sites.psu.edu/xxl13/l2sca/) is a pre-2015 foundation: the indexed official page describes nine syntactic counts and 14 ratios for advanced learner English. The live open failed with 502; its package, dependency licenses and reproduction behavior remain uninspected. This failure does not prove the package is unavailable.
