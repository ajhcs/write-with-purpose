# Business and workplace writing: reuse findings

Nine checked records are in [findings.json](findings.json). This is an evidence map, not a pooled review. None establishes professional decision-memo or proposal norms, and no professional long-form move-coded corpus with verified redistribution terms was identified.

## Three strongest opportunities

1. **Professional report evidence:** Yeung's [2007 study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.esp.2006.06.004) examines 22 authentic reports, primarily from Hong Kong, across contexts. The institutional abstract describes macro-organization and recommendation-oriented moves; the full article is gated. Secure lawful access before transcribing its definitions, counts or examples. Its findings are comparative evidence, not a universal report template. New work: check annotations, reliability and distribution rights, and sample contemporary professional decision documents.
2. **Detailed case-analysis framework:** Nathan's [2010 thesis](https://etheses.bham.ac.uk/id/eprint/711/1/Nathan10PhD.pdf), particularly its move model and comparison with BAWE, and the [2016 options-analysis author manuscript](https://durham-repository.worktribe.com/preview/1401020/34717.pdf) supply definitions, organizational distinctions, repeated options cycles and frequency evidence for 23 student extracts. These can seed operational comparisons for context, options, feasibility and evaluation; keep their labels and unit of analysis. New work: annotate real workplace material and measure agreement; do not turn observations called obligatory into authoring mandates. Both studies involve educational assignments, not professional norms.
3. **Paired workplace annotations:** Sappelli and colleagues' [actual CSV](https://cs.ru.nl/~msappelli/data/AllDataENRON-transposed-final.csv) is downloadable and contains 1,145 data rows (local count), message IDs, paired annotator columns for acts, reasons, response expectations and tasks. The [author's schema](https://cs.ru.nl/~msappelli/data/) defines the fields. It permits a concrete study of disagreement and activity labels after clarifying reuse rights; it is message-level email annotation, not paragraph move boundaries. The [Parakweet sentence dataset](https://github.com/ParakweetLabs/EmailIntentDataSet/wiki) offers binary intent examples, but its wiki's 4,213 training count conflicts with 3,657 physical lines in the current training file, so reconcile before use. Its repository declares Apache-2.0; underlying Enron text rights are not established by that declaration. Both are single-company email evidence, not decision memo evidence.

An additional structural annotation lead, [Quagga](https://hpi.de/naumann/projects/web-science/mimir-corpus-exploration-and-knowledge-management/email-structure.html), specifies character-offset zones and manual annotations for approximately 800 Enron messages; the linked archive returned HTTP 403 to our local client. Treat this as a documented lead, not verified downloadable training data. Its zones are email boundaries rather than rhetorical functions.

## Research syntheses and access

The openly accessible [Dudley-Evans and St. John report (1996)](https://www.ets.org/Media/Research/pdf/TOEIC-RR-02.pdf) is a historical critical narrative review of business English research and teaching materials; it distinguishes communicative purpose and contextual business genres. It is not a quantitative meta-analysis. [Yates and Orlikowski (1992)](https://doi.org/10.5465/amr.1992.4279545), *Genres of Organizational Communication: A Structurational Approach to Studying Communication and Media*, is a conceptual account linking recurrent organizational situations, form and purpose. Its publisher abstract was inspected, but the PDF fetch returned HTTP 403; assess the full paper through authorized access. Yeung (2007), listed above, is an empirical corpus study, not a synthesis; its publisher offered PDF purchase. No account or bypass was used.

**Remaining gap:** Existing records cover student cases, a small professional report study, and email acts. They do not supply a validated open corpus of professional business proposals or decision memos with span boundaries, inter-annotator agreement, and clear text/annotation reuse rights. The next step is rights and access checking followed by a deliberately scoped practitioner sample, preserving disagreement and context.
