## Nonadditive and unequal evidence

GUM, GDTB, GENTLE and DISRPT include shared documents, transformed annotations or shared sources. GDTB conversion and correction are not an independent second human sample. WLP 2020 reannotates material from the 2018 task; BAWE classification draws on BAWE assignments; PERSUADE releases are related; Webis 2016 and 2018 are versions; and RST-DT/PDTB/DiscAlign have overlapping WSJ materials. Classify them as linked families and measure overlap before any pooled estimate.

GUM can contain front matter within paragraph tags; AMALGUM includes excerpts and automatic annotations; SciPara samples paragraphs from papers; PubMed RCT consists of heading-derived silver labels in clinical abstracts; BAWE and PERSUADE concern student assignments; Webis covers three news outlets. None is a professional cross-genre writing norm or a direct annotation of the project's paragraph functions. Original labels, annotation units, uncertainty, selection and release provenance must remain visible. Do not claim that any source contains the project's twelve functions or imposes one primary function, quotas, length limits or readability targets.
