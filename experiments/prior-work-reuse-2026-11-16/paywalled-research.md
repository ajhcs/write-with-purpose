# Research access and library retrieval list

These are studies **about writing and annotation methods**, rather than more exemplar articles. The [12-row CSV](paywalled-research.csv) preserves titles, authors, dates, DOIs, source links, inspected scope and access evidence. Some publisher versions require access; others have author manuscripts. A failed HTTP request is not a demonstrated paywall.

## Prioritize for library retrieval or access checking

1. **Kim, Qiu & Wang (2024), _Interrater agreement in genre analysis: A methodological review and a comparison of three measures_.** [DOI 10.1016/j.rmal.2024.100097](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rmal.2024.100097). The most direct review for our coder disagreement problem: 65 EAP genre studies plus a three-researcher comparison on 35 introductions. Publisher indexed abstract/methods inspected; full methods not appraised. Direct fetch failed and OpenAlex lists closed access without an OA copy.
2. **Biber, Connor & Upton (2007), _Discourse on the Move: Using corpus analysis to describe discourse structure_.** [DOI 10.1075/scl.28](https://doi.org/10.1075/scl.28); ISBN **9789027223029**. A methodological synthesis with corpus studies across genres, rather than one pooled meta-analysis. Publisher contents inspected; complete book requires authorization.
3. **Cotos, Huffman & Link (2017), _A move/step model for methods sections: Demonstrating Rigour and Credibility_.** [DOI 10.1016/j.esp.2017.01.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.esp.2017.01.001). A corpus study of 900 Methods texts across 30 fields; not a meta-analysis. Publisher offers purchase/institutional access. [Possible institutional author-version lead](https://lib.dr.iastate.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1098&context=engl_pubs) appears in OpenAlex, but failed direct retrieval; a current downloadable copy and the annotated corpus are unverified.
4. **Yeung (2007), _In search of commonalities: Some linguistic and rhetorical features of business reports as a genre_.** [DOI 10.1016/j.esp.2006.06.004](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.esp.2006.06.004). Study of 22 authentic professional reports. Publisher abstract and corpus passages inspected; full paper offered for purchase. No verified open move-coded release.
5. **Tessuto (2015), _Generic structure and rhetorical moves in English-language empirical law research articles: Sites of interdisciplinary and interdiscursive cross-over_.** [DOI 10.1016/j.esp.2014.06.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.esp.2014.06.002). Publisher preview inspected; full methods not appraised. Direct fetch failed; no legitimate author copy verified.
6. **Lu, Yoon & Kisselev (2021), _Matching phrase-frames to rhetorical moves in social science research article introductions_.** [DOI 10.1016/j.esp.2020.10.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.esp.2020.10.001). [Institutional abstract](https://pure.psu.edu/en/publications/matching-phrase-frames-to-rhetorical-moves-in-social-science-rese/) describes 600 manually annotated introductions in six disciplines. Full article and downloadable gold corpus access not verified. Phrase-frame wording is not proposed as default drafting language.
7. **Yates & Orlikowski (1992), _Genres of Organizational Communication: A Structurational Approach to Studying Communication and Media_.** [DOI 10.5465/amr.1992.4279545](https://doi.org/10.5465/amr.1992.4279545). Genre theory useful for purpose, audience and organizational context. Abstract/metadata inspected; PDF returned 403, which does not establish a paywall.

## Legitimate author versions already located

- **Upton & Cohen (2009), _An approach to corpus-based discourse analysis: The move analysis as example_.** [DOI 10.1177/1461445609341006](https://doi.org/10.1177/1461445609341006); [author PDF](https://scholarworks.indianapolis.iu.edu/bitstream/handle/1805/2332/Approach_to_Corpus_based.pdf). Seven-step method and worked codebook examined by the scout; illustrative sample 46 letters. The paper's separate 242-letter example has a different denominator.
- **Moreno & Swales (2018), _Strengthening move analysis methodology towards bridging the function-form gap_.** [DOI 10.1016/j.esp.2017.11.006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.esp.2017.11.006); [author PDF](https://buleria.unileon.es/bitstream/handle/10612/10667/Strengthening_Move_Analysis_Methodology.pdf?sequence=6). Segmentation, definitions, contextual coding and reliability examined by the scout. Study-specific categories and agreement do not validate our functions.
- **Gardner & Nesi (2013), _A classification of genre families in university student writing_.** [DOI 10.1093/applin/ams024](https://doi.org/10.1093/applin/ams024); [submitted manuscript](https://pure.coventry.ac.uk/ws/portalfiles/portal/3978905/gardnercomb.pdf). Thirteen families with purposes and stages; student assignments, not professional benchmarks. Manuscript and underlying corpus terms differ.
- **Shi Huawei & Aryadoust (2023 issue; 2022 online), _A systematic review of automated writing evaluation systems_.** [DOI 10.1007/s10639-022-11200-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10639-022-11200-7); [institutional author PDF](https://repository.nie.edu.sg/server/api/core/bitstreams/223f6952-ce18-4df4-8f63-b51cd2f44141/content). 105 papers on AWE scoring; abstract and search scope inspected, complete methods not appraised. This addresses assessment validity, not form-length distributions.

## Open access; no library purchase indicated

**Pearson & Abdollahzadeh (2023), _Metadiscourse in academic writing: A systematic review_.** [DOI 10.1016/j.lingua.2023.103561](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lingua.2023.103561); [publisher page](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0024384123000852). A review of 370 empirical studies published 1990–2021. Publisher explicitly marks open access; our direct fetch failed. Data are available on request, not a verified public download. Abstract and indexed methods passages inspected.

Also directly useful and open: **Cuevas-Alonso & Míguez-Álvarez (2021), _Metadiscursive Markers and Text Genre: A Metareview_.** [DOI 10.3390/publications9040056](https://doi.org/10.3390/publications9040056); [full text](https://www.mdpi.com/2304-6775/9/4/56). Forty-four included studies compare marker frequencies across academic and non-academic genres. This does contain cross-study quantitative summaries, but pools some sections and disciplines; its broad averages cannot be imported as our narrowly scoped writing targets. It is in the main bibliography, not the 12-row access queue.

Access checks and their limits are preserved in [the independent audit](audit/report.md) and [parent access metadata](audit/parent-access-checks.json). Public availability of a paper does not establish availability of its source corpus.
