# Writing harness: accepted foundations

Recorded 15 November 2026 from Cole's explicit feedback in the writing-harness design conversation. This is the durable record of the agreed foundation. It records design decisions, not implemented capabilities or measured corpus findings.

## The nine features

Retain these nine feature groups as the initial measurement framework. Individual measurements can be refined without silently replacing or expanding this agreed list.

| ID | Feature | What we would measure | What it helps determine |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F01 | Document length | Main-text words; title, abstract, captions, and references separately | Overall working budget |
| F02 | Section structure | Number of sections, heading depth, common section roles and sequences | Available outlines |
| F03 | Section proportions | Words per section and percentage of the whole | How much space each part receives |
| F04 | Paragraph shape | Words and sentences per paragraph, including variation within a piece | How ideas tend to develop |
| F05 | Sentence shape | Words per sentence, median, spread, and frequency of short or long sentences | Expected rhythm and processing demands |
| F06 | Word length | Characters or estimated syllables per word | A descriptive language signal; technical terms need separate treatment |
| F07 | Examples and evidence | Frequency and placement of examples, citations, quotations, tables, and figures | How the form develops and supports its contribution |
| F08 | Presentation | Proportion of prose, lists, instructions, code, and other elements | The visible shape of the document |
| F09 | Communicative functions | Where the writing defines, explains, compares, supports, interprets, or qualifies | What each part should accomplish |

F07 concerns the supporting role and placement of material; F08 concerns its visible presentation. The same object can contribute to both without becoming two separate objects in the source inventory.

## Hierarchy: Family → Form → Section

This hierarchy is accepted as the organizing direction:

- **Family:** a group with useful shared conventions or measurable characteristics.
- **Form:** a more specific kind of writing with recognizable reader expectations and structural patterns.
- **Section:** a functional part of the selected form, with its own roles and distributions. It need not have a printed heading.

Paragraphs and sentences are units we inspect within that structure. Their communicative functions can be annotated without requiring every paragraph to perform exactly one job.

The family names and form inventory proposed earlier remain provisional. They must earn their place by reducing uncertainty about the writing. No number of families or forms is fixed by this decision.

## Observed Range, Working Target, Minimum and Maximum Limits

Keep these records separate:

| Record | Meaning | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Observed Range | What was measured in a stated reference collection; observed extremes, central tendency, and spread belong here | Describe and compare examples |
| Working Target | A chosen budget or pattern for the current piece | Guide planning and drafting |
| Minimum Limit | An explicit lower bound, when the author, task, or destination actually sets one | Enforce when mechanically testable |
| Maximum Limit | An explicit upper bound, when the author, task, or destination actually sets one | Enforce when mechanically testable |

Observed minimum and maximum values are sample statistics; they do not automatically become Minimum and Maximum Limits. Either declared limit can be absent. A working target is not automatically a hard limit. Each record needs its unit, scope, and source.

## Categories and reference selection

- Books are outside the initial scope.
- Broad publication labels such as “blog post” should not, by themselves, define a predictive family. Keep the destination or medium as metadata when that is what the label identifies.
- Do not pool a huge heterogeneous population merely because its documents share a publishing platform or file type.
- Build reference collections from deliberately selected, worthwhile examples, preserving their selection reasons and differences.
- Widely read, award-winning, and highly cited work can supply candidates. Suitability as a writing model must still be examined. The conversation's rough estimates of how many worthwhile blogs exist are not established corpus counts.
- Preserve distinct successful patterns within a form. An average across incompatible patterns should not become the default template.
- The objective is to make conventions and choices clear while preserving the author's contribution and meaningful variation. Convergence toward a single median is not a quality objective.
