# Library discovery: influence, readership, selection and historical versions

The author has extensive library access and has offered to retrieve works outside our public-source collection. Prioritize original publication through 2014, with 2015–2019 as a separate supplement. Keep later exploratory material separately identified. This changes source selection: open-access availability must not become a hidden definition of worthwhile writing.

This note identifies retrieval routes, not new form definitions or a universal ranking. Research books and annual anthologies may supply definitions and lists even though books are outside the target writing forms. For an anthology, the sample unit remains the individual originally published piece.

## Routes by writing category

| Forms | Sources to start with | What the selection signal means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Empirical reports, methods, theory, case reports, protocols, review forms | Web of Science and Scopus historical searches; Europe PMC/OpenAlex metadata for initial DOI discovery; disciplinary journals' article-type collections | Citations within a database and a defined query/period. Refine by subject and inspect form; raw cross-field citation counts do not rank prose quality |
| Personal/reflective essays and argument/perspective | *The Best American Essays* annual selections and Notables; ASME award/finalist lists; Longform/Longreads historical selections | Editorial or award selection, distinct from actual readership; original publication date can precede award/anthology year |
| Critical reviews of works | ASME Essays/Criticism and Pulitzer Criticism; disciplinary review journals and literary periodicals, located through library discovery | Award, editorial selection, or scoped citations. Confirm the work reviewed; book-review citation records can conflate the book and the review |
| Public conceptual/scientific explanation | Pulitzer explanatory reporting and selected historical science/technology editorial lists | Editorial/award evidence, not automatic equivalence between reported feature and conceptual explainer |
| Tutorial, procedure, reference | Historical editions of official project documentation and technical periodicals, versioned archives, recognized project reader/task classifications | Source authority and era; actual page readership usually unavailable. Modern edits cannot be treated as pre-2015 wording |
| Proposals | Full sample applications identified by funders/institutions and published author collections | Documented purpose and sometimes funding outcome. An award abstract, protocol or application guide is not a full proposal |
| Decision memos | Published/declassified decision archives and institutional collections with identifiable recommendation/decision tasks | Archival or editorial selection; memo title alone does not establish decision purpose |

## Verified list and metric resources

- **Scopus:** [official search-results guidance](https://www.elsevier.support/scopus/answer/how-do-i-work-with-document-search-results) documents sorting by “Cited by (highest).” Use explicit dates, subject, source and type filters, and preserve the exact search and snapshot. Its citation counts are not interchangeable with other providers'.
- **Clarivate Essential Science Indicators:** [threshold definitions](https://esi.help.clarivate.com/Content/highly-cited-thresholds.htm) define highly cited papers relative to field and publication year. Its recent rolling window makes current ESI unsuitable as the only route to pre-2015 examples; historical Web of Science searches or appropriately dated snapshots are needed. A high Europe PMC citation count is not an ESI designation.
- **Longform:** [Best of 2014](https://longform.org/lists/best-of-2014) provides genre/subject selections; [Most Clicked 2017](https://longform.org/lists/best-of-2017/most-clicked) is a scoped referral-audience list. A click is not proof of a complete read. Our generic sitemap inventory does not confer most-clicked status.
- **Longreads:** [Best of 2014](https://longreads.com/best-of-2014/) distinguishes favorite essays/reporting from its own popular exclusives. Record which list selected a work; do not flatten them into a common popularity rank.
- **ASME:** [2014 National Magazine Awards finalists](https://www.asme.media/national-magazine-awards-2014-finalists-announced) identifies categories, outlets and selected pieces. Historical categories change; preserve the category/year and original publication details.
- **Pulitzer:** [2014 winners and finalists](https://www.pulitzer.org/prize-winners-by-year/2014) and [category archives](https://www.pulitzer.org/prize-winners-categories) supply award-based discovery. Some entries are series/portfolios, so resolve constituent works and avoid treating a portfolio as one paragraph-length observation.
- **The Best American Essays:** [series site](https://www.thebestamericanessays.com/) identifies annual volumes. The annual Notables lists can substantially broaden authors and outlets. Confirm the original citation and distinguish anthology edition year from the year of the underlying work. The [essay branch's historical discovery note](../../../experiments/form-corpus-2026-11-16/essays/historical-discovery.md) records inspected leads.

## A small library batch before bulk retrieval

Choose a diverse first batch from the screened retrieval priorities, preserving original form hypotheses. Include highly cited papers and different editorial/award selections rather than exclusively selecting the highest raw count. Obtain complete works through the available library access. Save source/version and original bibliographic details with each file; we can then inspect type, text boundaries and parser performance before requesting hundreds more.

Useful export columns are title, author, original year and first-online date if available, journal/outlet, DOI/stable identifier, source URL, abstract if exportable, source-provided document type, citation count/provider/snapshot, exact search/list/award source, and access/retrieval status. An RIS/BibTeX export supports citation matching; CSV better preserves ranking/filter provenance when the provider includes it.

Keep PDF/source text separate from derived measurements. Record whether a PDF contains the complete article, supplementary items, editorial notes or a reprint. Publisher/article rights and library terms determine reuse. No credentials or bypass are needed for preparing the metadata queue.

The next meaningful output is a reviewed eligibility ledger, followed by comparable measurements within each historical form/source stratum. These collections have not yet established form-wide numerical ranges or author-facing writing limits.
