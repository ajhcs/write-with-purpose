# Broaden the corpus beyond medicine

The author explicitly requested business, engineering, law and personal writing in addition to the existing medical material. This is a substantive sampling correction. The medical-heavy XML collection and DOI queue remain a bounded domain cohort, not the default population for the harness.

## Keep form and context visible

For sampling, business, engineering, law, medicine and personal/public writing are separate source groups. Keep subject domain, writing context, purpose, form, audience and medium as distinct metadata where possible. Personal writing can concern engineering, business, law or health; its voice and reflective purpose should not disappear under the subject label.

Useful comparisons are made within **form × domain/context × audience × era**, with outlet/project and author also recorded. Do not combine all domains into a single median for an essay, research paper or reference entry. Do not force every form into every field merely to populate a grid.

## Priority source and form connections

| Source group | Forms and distinctions to investigate | Appropriate discovery resources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business and management | Empirical organizational research, conceptual/theory papers, critical syntheses, practitioner arguments, organizational case studies, proposals and decision memos | Academy of Management journals, Organization Science, Management Science, Strategic Management Journal; HBR and institutional practitioner collections as separately identified sources |
| Engineering | Empirical and methods papers, system/design arguments, technical explanations, tutorials, procedures and reference; proposals and engineering case studies where actual documents are available | IEEE Xplore, ASME, ASCE and systems-engineering sources; standards-body and versioned product/project documentation |
| Law | Doctrinal/normative arguments, empirical legal studies, theoretical analyses, criticism, practitioner memoranda; legal briefs and judicial opinions as potential additional forms requiring their own definitions | HeinOnline, historical law-review citation studies, law-school repositories and original court/agency collections |
| Personal/public writing | Reflective essay, personal argument, criticism and explanation grounded in experience | Multi-author literary periodicals, annual essay selections/Notables, ASME/Pulitzer selections, historical Longform/Longreads lists |
| Medicine/health | Existing clinical study/report/review/protocol strata | Existing Europe PMC/NLM collection with its original boundaries retained |

The journal/source examples are acquisition anchors, not blanket claims that every article has a particular form or high writing quality. Published teaching cases, research case studies and clinical case reports have different purposes. A legal doctrinal article need not use IMRAD. A systematic review in management is not automatically governed by clinical reporting conventions. Legal briefs and judicial opinions are not silently added to the established seventeen-form inventory.

## Authority and ranking checks

- [Academy of Management's formal-theory guidance](https://support.aom.org/hc/en-us/articles/360059744013-Does-AMR-publish-papers-using-formal-theory) identifies theoretical contribution and accessibility to management scholars as criteria. Its definition distinguishes theory development from merely formalizing a model. This is useful for conceptual-paper eligibility, not a substitute for screening each article.
- [IEEE Xplore search-results guidance](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/Xplorehelp/searching-ieee-xplore/search-results-page) describes “Most Cited” ordering within the result set. Use domain/year/source filters and preserve the ranking snapshot; do not treat OpenAlex counts as IEEE counts.
- [HeinOnline's most-cited-article rankings](https://access.heinonline.com/HOL/scholarly-impact-ranking/comprehensive-articles) show citation and access columns. They concern the provider's coverage and defined windows, not global readership. Scholarly and judicial citations, article access, and influence are different signals.
- [Shapiro and Pearse (2012), The Most-Cited Law Review Articles of All Time](https://repository.law.umich.edu/mlr/vol110/iss8/2/) supplies a historical, field-specific research anchor: its abstract describes all-time and recent-period top-100 tables using HeinOnline and Web of Science. The two lists can overlap and are not automatically 200 distinct articles. Access to the abstract does not mean all ranked references were extracted in this step.
- [The Best American Essays series](https://www.thebestamericanessays.com/) and its historical Notables lists provide multi-author editorial discovery. Award/anthology year and original publication year must be kept distinct. Library retrieval can reach periodical essays unavailable in a public archive.

## Measurements to compare after full-text retrieval

Continue using the nine accepted feature groups, with domain-specific interpretation. For instance, separate legal footnotes from ordinary prose and distinguish formal authority citations from illustrative links. Separate engineering equations, code and specifications from explanatory prose. Separate business tables and cases from theoretical reasoning. Preserve personal narrative scenes, reflection and authorial language rather than treating them as excess background.

These are requirements for defining extraction and reviewing passages, not implemented parsers or automatic function judgments. Comparable counts and reviewed form assignments are prerequisites for interpreting cross-domain differences. Writing targets should emerge from the author's task and supported alternatives, not from the domain with the largest available archive.
