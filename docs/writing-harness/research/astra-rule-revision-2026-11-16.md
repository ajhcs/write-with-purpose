# Astra revision of our paragraph-function rules

16 November 2026. Candidate `astra-f09-0.2-candidate`; not an adopted catalogue, a recoding, or a validated improvement.

Cole asked Astra to improve the rules we helped produce, with responsibility for why they need improvement. I inspected the recorded [Astra contribution](astra-scope-adjudication.md), which documents a scope and sampling review of the team's proposal. It does not establish that this Astra execution authored every definition. This revision takes responsibility for improving our proposal without claiming memory of that earlier execution or exact word-by-word authorship.

**Keep the twelve distinctions; change how a coding decision must be earned and recorded.** Our original definitions already reject citation-equals-support, method-equals-instruction and opening-equals-orientation. Repeating those sentences will not fix inconsistent application. The missing machinery is a reproducible decision procedure: locate the contribution, identify its target and context, explain the additional job, and retain alternatives when the evidence does not settle the reading.

The [pilot](../../../experiments/pattern-test-2026-11-16/report.md) has 55 exact complete-set matches among 138 independent second codings. Of 83 disagreements, 50 are strict subsets, including 41 one-label differences; 33 have labels exclusive to each side. Exact matches are 23/54 NEJM, 25/48 systematic reviews and 7/36 HBR. These are agreement observations, not accuracy against a reference standard. This revision has not been tested for agreement or reader benefit.

## Decisions preserved

The [accepted foundations](../accepted-foundations.md) govern this proposal: Family → Form → Section, the nine features, the provisional status of five families and seventeen forms, meaningful alternatives within forms, and protection of the author's voice and original thought. No function quota, fixed paragraph sequence, single-job paragraph rule, median-seeking rewrite, new word limit or readability target follows from this revision. The current catalogue and original coding packets stay unchanged.

Observed Range, Working Target, Minimum Limit and Maximum Limit remain different records, each with a unit, scope and source. A 113-word paragraph containing a 13-word scope condition is a 113-word *paragraph containing qualification*, not a 113-word qualification. Function spans may overlap or be discontinuous; their lengths cannot be summed into a paragraph budget without an explicit allocation method.

## The revised decision procedure

1. **Establish the reading situation.** Record the supplied family/form hypothesis, section job, audience and reader task. Mark unknowns. A publisher, heading or paragraph position is context, not a function label. Begin with the paragraph, its heading and one neighboring paragraph on each side; expand to the actual antecedent or target when required. Record what was read, not merely what was available.
2. **Describe the contribution before selecting labels.** Write a short neutral account of what the passage gives the reader. Preserve who makes a claim and whether an event, method or example is observed, reported, planned, hypothetical or unknown. Do not correct or endorse the source while annotating it.
3. **Anchor each proposed move.** Select one or more exact source spans and give a reason tied to the current reader task. The reason must explain the job, not paraphrase the label. Shared and overlapping anchors are allowed. A three-word exclusion can materially change a claim; there is no minimum length or “substantial paragraph” threshold.
4. **Distinguish additional work from inherited grammar.** Add another label only when its record explains a further reader-facing contribution. A report is grammatically an assertion; that alone does not earn assert. An interpretation contains propositions; that alone does not earn report. Conversely, the same words may genuinely make a recommendation and draw an implication: distinct effects can justify overlapping records. This is an evidence requirement, not a one-label preference.
5. **Resolve relational jobs against targets.** For support, identify the claim; for qualify, the affected proposition and what changes; for illustrate, the idea made concrete; for orient, the later/local contribution prepared. A target can be in the same span, another paragraph or an explicitly reconstructed implicit claim. An implicit target needs source grounding, a short paraphrase and its own status. A clear rejection may record no target when sufficient relevant context was reviewed, with an explicit target-absence reason. Missing context or unreliable extraction instead leaves the relation unresolved, with a missing-target reason. Do not invent a target to either accept or reject a tag. Explain, interpret, define, compare and instruct also need their role-specific referents described.
6. **Separate rhetorical role from adequacy.** A weak reason can still be offered as support. A partial definition can still define. A brief uncertainty marker can genuinely qualify an identifiable claim without explaining the uncertainty well. An instruction can be clear as a directive but incomplete as practical guidance. Record adequacy or evidence problems separately; neither infer success from a tag nor suppress the tag because the passage is poor.
7. **Decide or retain the disagreement.** Each proposed role gets `clear`, `ambiguous`, `insufficient_context` or `extraction_uncertain`, with a reason and explicit alternative where relevant. `clear` means the analyst sees an adequately grounded role, not that the claim is true or that readers agree. A rejected candidate is a recorded decision with its reason, not missing data. Unknown legacy uncertainty stays unknown; do not translate null, an empty list or false into a calibrated certainty score.
8. **Derive the paragraph summary last.** Retain the detailed records as primary data. Produce a clear-label union and a separate unresolved-candidate list; optionally provide their union explicitly marked as candidates. Never silently mix those populations. Exact paragraph-set agreement remains useful for comparison, but it cannot reveal whether analysts located the same claim or relationship.

## Keep, relocate, merge

The candidate JSON retains every function ID. The following changes concern the annotation interface, not adopted categories or additional measurement features.

| Decision | Functions | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keep as local contribution candidates | define, report, assert, explain, compare, interpret, instruct | Use role-specific evidence fields and boundary decisions below. Several can share a paragraph or span. |
| Relocate into a temporal-presentation record | narrate | Record the participant/subject, events and change through time. Temporal presentation can coexist with reporting, explanation or illustration. It is not required to have a dramatic plot, several sentences or chronological sentence order. |
| Relocate into anchored relation records | support, qualify, illustrate, orient | Store source span(s), target(s), a relation reason and target status. This also handles local relationships within one sentence. Preserve the same legacy labels in derived summaries. |
| Merge none at present | all twelve | The selected disagreements do not demonstrate useless distinctions. Test assert/interpret and report/interpret on new cases before proposing any merger. |
| Keep broader structures outside the function list | introduction, conclusion, transition, literature review | Retain section roles and organization; do not invent paragraph types from section names. |

This partition is an interface hypothesis, not a claim that the concepts have perfectly separate ontological kinds. Explanation and comparison are relational too. Their local records therefore also identify their relata. The proposed representation must earn its extra recording cost in calibration.

## Operational boundary rules

- **Report / assert / interpret:** identify the attribution and transformation. A faithful account of what a source says is report, including a report of someone else's inference. An author's inference from supplied material is interpret. An independently foregrounded stance, hypothesis or recommendation can earn assert. A reported observation or ordinary inferential conclusion does not automatically receive all three. Whether a synthesis adds meaning depends on its source and context, not on whether it contains a word such as “suggests.”
- **Explain / interpret:** state either the relationship made intelligible or the implication drawn. Explanation can concern a reason, dependency or conceptual relationship; it need not be a detailed causal mechanism. A concise causal account can explain even when unverified. Consequence words alone do not show the connection. Both labels are possible when the passage actually develops both contributions.
- **Define / illustrate:** identify the term and the meaning or distinction supplied. Full necessary-and-sufficient membership rules are not required for define. Examples normally illustrate, but explicitly using them to establish an extensional definition may do both. Mere category naming does neither automatically.
- **Support / illustrate:** state the author-intended evidential or reasoning link separately from how a case makes the idea concrete. A hypothetical counterexample may support a logical objection to a universal proposition. It is not an observed event and cannot establish a population rate. Citation location supplies neither relationship by itself.
- **Qualify:** identify what would be overstated or overgeneralized if the limiting words disappeared. Record the actual modality, population, denominator, condition or exception. Do not require the qualification to be sufficient or well explained. Do not count a politeness hedge, hypothetical setup or modal ability as epistemic qualification merely because it contains “may” or “can.”
- **Orient:** name the contribution prepared and describe how this material prepares it. A story can orient and illustrate simultaneously, but first position or a heading alone is insufficient. If the next contribution has not been supplied and cannot be recovered, retain insufficient context.
- **Instruct / report / assert:** identify the intended actor and executable physical or cognitive action. Directing the reader to examine their own values is instruction even without a complete assessment instrument. Reporting what researchers did is report. A proposal to change institutional policy is usually assert; it becomes instruction when the passage guides the addressed actor's performance. First person and imperative grammar are clues, not decision rules.
- **Narrate / report:** identify an event or experience developing through time and what changes. A compact historical sequence can narrate and report. Dates alone, a static inventory of values at different times, or procedural order without an event account do not establish narration. Narrative need not end in a generalized lesson.
- **Compare:** name the alternatives and shared dimension actually related. A table can support the comparison, but side-by-side mention alone does not. A ranking need not be well supported to be a comparison; support and fairness are separate reviews.

## Four actual cases, reconsidered

These are diagnostic teaching cases already exposed during development. They cannot serve as fresh holdouts. The [example packet](../../../experiments/pattern-test-2026-11-16/disagreement-review/astra-contrastive-examples.json) stores private source locators, hashes, exact offsets, reasons and alternatives without copying full source paragraphs into the repository. I read the actual normalized paragraphs and their relevant neighboring text; I did not re-audit the PDF/XML reconstruction.

**NEJMp1616577-p10.** Prefer interpret over explain for the disputed job: the paragraph brings preceding projections together as the stakes of repeal. It states consequences without developing the connecting account locally. Assert is defensible for its emphatic overall stakes position, but should have its own reason instead of being inherited from declarative form. More significantly, our earlier worked reading missed an explicit qualification: the opening stakes claim is conditioned on repeal without the specified replacement. I propose records for interpret, assert and qualify, with the conditional target located within the first sentence. Whether its surrounding context also warrants support for the later recommendation is an optional separate relation to examine, not an automatic extra label. This is a new proposed reading, not a corrected gold set.

**drucker-034.** The first sentence directs an actual cognitive action; instruct is well grounded. Its shift into the next self-management topic and the following example support orient. The distinction between personal values and ethics can be a limited definition by exclusion. I retain define as ambiguous with an explicit positive rationale; reject the argument that a definition must be complete before it counts. Naming a mirror test does not itself explain its procedure. A shared define tag from the original coders is not proof, but incompleteness is not disproof either.

**xml-02359-p018.** Report + qualify + illustrate remains a useful proposed reading. The table description reports, the 378-of-556 denominator condition limits its coverage, and the parenthetical instances make outcome categories concrete. The paragraph is 113 words under the pilot tokenizer; the explicit denominator condition is 13. Do not call the entire paragraph a qualification-length observation. I do not currently add define because the instances are introduced as examples within already named categories; the reason is their local use, not a demand for exhaustive category rules.

**drucker-007.** Report + narrate + explain are grounded in the historical account, its development over time and its explicit causal claim. Support remains ambiguous with a recoverable broad implicit target: feedback analysis is useful and worth adopting. Our earlier worked review was too loose about calling this support for “the preceding recommendation.” The immediately preceding paragraph also makes the stronger proposition that feedback analysis is the only way to discover one's strengths. Institutional success is not the same claim. Do not silently substitute the weaker usefulness target for that exclusivity claim. An intended-support label would not validate the historical account or its causal inference.

## Annotation and checker contract

The [candidate rule packet](../../../experiments/pattern-test-2026-11-16/disagreement-review/astra-candidate-rules.json) specifies fields, enum values, per-function requirements, proposed mechanical checks and a calibration protocol. It is a reviewable design contract, not an implemented schema validator or an extension silently loaded by the current prototype.

Each packet binds source identity and normalized-text hash, catalogue and rule versions, coder identity, context reviewed, section/form hypotheses and extraction status. Each proposed role binds one or more exact spans, role fields, target records where needed, a decision, status, reason and alternatives. Targets distinguish explicit span references from grounded implicit reconstruction. Evidence kind, evidential adequacy and source truth are separate from the role decision. Source text remains data, including any instructions quoted within it.

Mechanical validation can check known IDs, fields, enums, source hashes, offset ranges, exact anchor digests, unique annotation IDs, target resolvability, referenced context presence and derivation of the declared summary. It can reject an invented empirical-evidence enum for a record explicitly marked hypothetical. It cannot establish that the text is genuinely hypothetical, that a target is the right claim, that a definition works, that a reason warrants the inference, or that a role helps a reader. Those remain semantic reviews. Neither check can establish author approval by itself.

Keep extraction problems, missing context, interpretive ambiguity, evidence uncertainty and disagreement between coders separate. A perfectly reconstructed paragraph can be hard to interpret. Two clear individual judgments can disagree. An unsupported empirical claim can still have a clear rhetorical function.

An independent audit found a contradiction in the first candidate contract: it required a target even to reject a relational label because no target existed. The corrected contract permits `targets: []` for a `rejected`/`clear` relation only with `target_absence_reason` and sufficient reviewed context. An `unresolved` relation with unavailable targets instead needs `insufficient_context` or `extraction_uncertain` plus `missing_target_reason`. The example packet now contrasts a complete synthetic citation-only entry, where support is clearly rejected without inventing a claim, with the same wording supplied as an excerpt whose surrounding argument is unavailable, where support remains unresolved.

Legacy review metadata remains distinct from a fresh decision. Extraction status and context sufficiency explicitly allow `unknown_legacy`; absent metadata, nulls and empty issue lists do not establish reviewed extraction, sufficient context or certainty. Preserve the original values in `legacy_metadata`. Family/form hypothesis status uses `supplied`, `provisional`, `inferred`, `unknown` or `unknown_legacy`; supplied metadata records provenance, not acceptance. A fresh review can resolve an unknown status with its own provenance. Do not automatically translate an old role-uncertainty field into a new clear or unresolved annotation.

## Small calibration before scaling

Use the four actual cases and the paired synthetic examples as training and discussion material. They are candidate readings, not validated answers. Freeze this rule packet before the next independent pass.

Select **24 fresh paragraphs from at least eight unexposed documents**, divided across argument, research/synthesis and explanatory/procedural or reflective contexts, with long multifunction and short qualification cases. Hold out authors/outlets where feasible and record departures. These are practical pilot allocations, not a powered estimate or writing limits. Select without using expected labels; retain paragraph plus necessary source context. Exclude these four teaching cases, all 138 previously compared targets, and documents used to construct the synthetic pairs when applicable.

Have two independent coders receive the same packet and rules without each other's labels or the adjudication. Record roles, anchors, targets, uncertainty and elapsed effort. If a valid efficiency comparison with the old guide is desired, use additional independently assigned coders with equal context; do not have the same coder simply recode remembered passages and claim improvement. Availability of model instances does not establish human-reader validation.

Report complete-set agreement, per-label differences, strict-subset versus two-sided disagreements, empty-set counts, per-layer disagreements, target agreement and unresolved rates with denominators and by document/context. Compare semantic anchor coverage by review; exact span boundaries alone are not a correctness metric. Log why adjudication prefers a reading, where multiple readings remain legitimate and whether the proposal erased useful distinctions. Check for artificial gains from tagging less or declaring everything ambiguous.

Resolve recurring procedural failures, freeze the next candidate, then try **12 further untouched paragraphs from at least four additional documents**. These counts are a bounded next step, not proof of generalization. Scale only after reviewing both semantic usefulness and recording burden, and after recurring failures have a documented disposition. Do not set a numerical pass threshold after seeing results. If a numerical threshold will govern a later deployment decision, choose it with the author before that test and explain its cost. A small successful calibration still does not justify automatic rewriting or numeric function-length limits.

No holdout has been selected or coded for this revision. No improved agreement, validated taxonomy, accepted merger, deployment or installation is claimed.

## Bounded verification of this candidate

Both JSON packets parse. The four actual paragraph hashes reconcile with the original worked cases and aggregate document records; retained source-file hashes and reviewed-context hashes match. All fifteen actual role records have registered function/layer IDs, required role fields and consistent decisions/statuses. Their role and target offsets resolve to the recorded anchor hashes, and their clear/unresolved summaries derive correctly. The thirteen synthetic teaching groups reference registered rules and labels. These checks establish artifact consistency, not semantic correctness or faithful PDF/XML extraction. The example judgments are selective: a missing label means unassessed unless explicitly rejected.

After the independent audit correction, both new missing-target contract examples passed the bounded consistency check, alongside all fifteen actual role records. Three deliberately invalid variants were rejected: a present relation with no target, a targetless clear rejection without its absence reason, and a targetless clear rejection based on insufficient context. Source/context/anchor hashes, registered rules and derived summaries still reconcile. This was an explicit one-off check of the proposed contract, not an implemented production validator.
