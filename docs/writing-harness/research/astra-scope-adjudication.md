## Recommendation

Retain the existing five family groups as provisional navigation. Pilot selected forms before accepting them as predictive profiles. Existing literature supports distinctions based on communicative purpose and context, but it does not validate our exact inventory or numerical ranges.

The strongest historical anchors are Bawarshi/Reiff's 2010 scholarly genre synthesis, Gardner/Nesi's BAWE classification methodology, and DITA 1.2's typed documentation units. Use the first to connect purpose and social activity, the second to derive classes from actual texts/context, and the third to structure documentation. None supplies universal writing-length norms.

The 2021 metadiscourse metareview and 2024 annotation-methods review are useful later supplements. The former pools distinctions our harness needs to preserve; the latter informs reliability procedures without validating our categories.

## Priority and sampling sequence

| Stage | Forms or compositions | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First pilot | Personal/reflective essay; argument/perspective; conceptual explainer; tutorial; how-to/procedure; reference entry; empirical report; systematic review | Covers the central user tasks and tests meaningful differences between reflecting/arguing, understanding/learning/acting/looking up, and original research/evidence synthesis |
| Composition pilot alongside those | README and manual | Exercises documents that combine several reader tasks, section types and navigation needs |
| Next | Narrative/critical literature review; scoping review; decision memo | Adds synthesis and decision purposes once initial extraction works |
| Retain provisionally | Other candidate profiles already proposed | Develop when concrete tasks/examples demonstrate a useful distinction |

Eight initial forms are a research allocation, not a proposed permanent taxonomy. Keep “personal blog” as a user-facing entry point that resolves into purpose and medium. Treat meta-analysis as a synthesis-method setting. Permit hybrid pieces and alternative structures.

Begin with **20–30 complete examples per first-pilot form**, **6–8 independently annotated in detail**. For eight forms, this is 160–240 screened examples and 48–64 detailed annotations if recruitment succeeds. These are practical allocations, not power calculations. Expand toward hundreds per supported stratum after extraction and boundaries work. Seek multiple authors/outlets and retain successful contrasting patterns; hold out authors/outlets for transfer assessment.

The parent agent adopts this staged priority in the accompanying corpus plan. Larger acquisition targets remain later coverage goals, not requirements to annotate thousands of works before trying a form.

## Best source fits and cautions

- Longform supplies editorially selected discovery candidates for essays, argument, reported narrative and explanation. The parent's acquired sitemap inventory is still unscreened.
- A List Apart is a promising professional technical-writing source; selected Quanta pieces can provide contrasting scientific explanation. [A List Apart background](https://alistapart.com/about/); [Quanta historical account](https://www.quantamagazine.org/the-new-familiar-quanta-20170502/). Individual works and date eligibility remain to be enumerated.
- The 393-README dataset directly supports composite section-role investigation; random repository sampling does not establish exemplary practice.
- AMALGUM supplies scale for procedures and parser experiments; GUM supplies richer reviewed annotations. Automatic annotations and source/genre confounding prevent treating those pools as curated excellent manuals.
- Purpose-selected PMC Open Access articles offer ample depth for research reports and evidence synthesis. Select manuscript type, discipline, era and editorial suitability; collection membership is not a quality screen.

Do not pool student/professional writing, excerpts/complete works, tutorials/procedures, abstracts/articles, or manuscript types sharing IMRAD headings. Paragraphs nested in one document are not independent document observations.

Accept a category split when it repeatedly improves planning, evidence requirements, section interpretation or reader performance. Merge when the distinction adds no useful guidance. Retain clusters and alternatives within forms. Author voice remains an author-specific choice rather than whatever remains after moving all features toward a corpus median.
