# Writing categories: research synthesis and corpus plan

16 November 2026. This extends the [initial historical search](pre-2015-writing-evidence.md) beyond instructional interventions. Four research agents investigated academic genres, technical documentation, essays/web writing, and cross-genre corpora; an explicitly requested Astra agent independently examined fit against the project's accepted foundations and proposals. Findings here are an initial evidence map, not an exhaustive umbrella review or a newly pooled statistical meta-analysis.

## Answer and consequences for the harness

There is substantial prior work on writing categories, purposes, structures, and discourse functions. The most useful evidence comes from several traditions rather than one all-purpose meta-analysis:

1. **Research syntheses** compare genre theories and summarize empirical register research.
2. **Typology studies** distinguish forms by purpose, practice, and structure.
3. **Cross-study descriptive syntheses** compare linguistic features across genres.
4. **Systematic mappings and methodological reviews** consolidate documentation knowledge and annotation practices.
5. **Annotated corpora** supply texts, metadata, and tested annotation schemes.

Our Family → Form → Section hierarchy has a particularly close empirical predecessor in Nesi/Gardner's work. Their corpus-grounded families are useful methodological models; their assessed-university setting does not establish a universal partition for professional or personal writing. The proposed paragraph functions remain a cross-cutting annotation layer, not substitutes for genre, section, subject matter, or linguistic register.

Keep the present family labels provisional. Choose forms through the reader's task, communicative purpose, evidence obligations, and useful structural alternatives. Measure whether a proposed form reduces uncertainty; do not accept a taxonomy because its labels seem familiar or a classifier can reproduce them.

### The closest existing classification

BAWE's thirteen families are **Case Study, Critique, Design Specification, Empathy Writing, Essay, Exercise, Explanation, Literature Survey, Methodology Recount, Narrative Recount, Problem Question, Proposal, and Research Report**. These authors' use of “family” need not have precisely the same granularity as ours. Their wider social functions concern demonstrating knowledge, independent reasoning, research skills, professional preparation, and writing for oneself/others.

| Their category | Possible connection to our design | Distinction to preserve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explanation | Conceptual explainer and explanation within another form | A successful assessed explanation is not automatically a professional web explainer |
| Essay / Critique | Argument, perspective, critical review | Assignment purpose and discipline can alter the expected reasoning |
| Literature Survey | Literature-review and evidence-synthesis candidates | A survey is not automatically a systematic review or meta-analysis |
| Research Report / Methodology Recount | Empirical reports and descriptions of methods | Similar headings can express different original contributions and assessment tasks |
| Narrative Recount / Empathy Writing | Narrative and perspective-taking resources | A recount is not interchangeable with a personal reflective essay |
| Proposal / Design Specification | Proposal and decision-oriented work | Genre purpose is distinct from a requirement's syntax or diagram format |

This is a provisional crosswalk for extraction and testing, not evidence that these categories are equivalent. Keep the original labels and definitions in the research data alongside our proposed labels.

### What a closer reading changes

**The 2021 metareview answers the existence question, but its aggregation is too broad for our desired profiles.** Its methods explicitly combine abstracts, conclusions and article bodies across disciplines. They also combine wikis, webpages, chats and forums as internet texts. Those decisions may serve its comparative question, but would conceal differences important to our harness. Re-extract individual study/section strata before transferring measurements. Marker frequencies are observations about language, not evidence that a paragraph adequately explains or qualifies a claim.

**Review methodology is not paragraph shape.** Grant/Booth's review types help determine what the work must do, including its search and appraisal obligations. Section moves and paragraphs then express that work. A systematic review can include a meta-analysis; the statistical method is not interchangeable with every review form. The paper's typology should inform a crosswalk and eligibility questions rather than automatically expand our form menu to fourteen items.

**Purpose can distinguish texts with the same headings.** Gardner's (2012) [Genres and Registers of Student Report Writing](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jeap.2011.11.002) shows why similar IMRD structures can belong to Research Report or Methodology Recount families. Copying an outline does not recover the reader task, author's contribution, or evidential obligation.

**Documentation needs an additional content axis.** An API design-rationale passage contains a kind of knowledge, performs an explanatory function, and may occur in a reference entry or conceptual guide. These are three useful descriptions. Adding “design rationale” as a competing general paragraph function would blur them.

**A formal document model can make some checks executable.** [OASIS DITA 1.2 (2010)](https://docs.oasis-open.org/dita/v1.2/os/spec/archSpec/dita_technicalContent_InformationTypes.html) distinguishes concept, task and reference material and provides task fields such as prerequisites, context, steps, results and examples. It is a standard, not a meta-analysis. A schema can validate declared structure; reader execution and explanatory adequacy still require assessment. Diátaxis and Kaplan-Moss's [2009 practitioner account](https://jacobian.org/2009/nov/10/what-to-write/) provide additional form distinctions with their own evidential status.

## Corpus design for the gaps

Use two complementary collections: a **comparison collection** to understand variation and test labels, and a **selected exemplar collection** to learn from worthwhile work. Existing corpora can supply the former. Editorial selections, awards, scoped readership lists, documented expert recommendations and field/year-stratified citation discovery can supply candidates for the latter.

### Proposed acquisition scale

These are operational sampling targets for review, not statistical guarantees, universal minimum sample sizes, or writing-length limits. Following the Astra review, **first pilot eight forms**: personal/reflective essay, argument/perspective, conceptual explainer, tutorial, how-to/procedure, reference entry, empirical report and systematic review. Aim for **20–30 eligible complete works per form** (160–240 total), including **6–8 independently annotated in detail per form** (48–64 total). Pilot README and manual composition alongside them. Refine boundaries before expanding.

The twelve-stratum grid below is the broader acquisition roadmap. Once the pilot works, a coverage goal of **100 eligible complete works per supported stratum** would yield 1,200 works across all twelve, with about 30 deeply annotated per stratum (360 total) and the others available for structural comparison and grouped holdouts. The two systematic-review rows are sampling strata to test method-related differences, not a decision to create two permanent forms. Revise allocation after disagreement, diversity and precision checks; rare forms may need a different plan. These targets have not been achieved by acquiring discovery URLs.

| Stratum | First sources and unresolved distinction |
| --- | --- |
| Personal / reflective essay | Longform Essays and editorial/award nominations; distinguish memoir scene, reflection and broader argument |
| Argument / perspective | Commentary/opinion selections; distinguish professional public argument from student persuasion |
| Critical review | Review/criticism awards and editorial selections; separate criticism of a work from systematic evidence synthesis |
| Conceptual / technical explainer | Longform Science/Tech, selected professional explainers, MDN conceptual guides; split by audience and topic |
| Tutorial | Versioned Python/MDN and at least additional independent projects; learning through a guided exercise |
| How-to / procedure | Versioned project documentation and diverse practical instructions; task completion rather than tutorial learning |
| Reference entry | API documentation and versioned reference; specify unit and avoid letting generated entries dominate |
| Research README | Published README dataset plus independently nominated examples; separate software overview from reproducibility obligations |
| Empirical research article | Field/year-stratified scholarly discovery; select independent disciplines and study types |
| Systematic review with quantitative synthesis | Scholar/PMC discovery, verified methods; control field and review purpose |
| Systematic review without quantitative synthesis | Verified methods and reasons for synthesis approach; avoid treating this as inferior work |
| Scoping review | Explicit mapping/scoping methods and reader task; compare with existing review typologies |

This sampling grid is a working coverage plan. It does not accept new family names or permanently split forms. Narrative/critical literature syntheses, protocols, methods papers, decision memos and proposals need subsequent strata or a focused pilot if they are selected for immediate authoring. Manuals are composite containers whose tutorials/procedures/reference sections contribute at their appropriate units. Books remain outside target scope.

Aim for diversity across authors, outlets/projects, subjects, periods, audiences and successful structural variants. A starting concentration cap of five works per author and 20 per outlet/project per 100-work stratum can expose overdependence; it is a proposed design choice, not a published norm. Preserve exceptional clusters separately when they are important. Split evaluation by author and outlet/project before fitting defaults; near-duplicate templates and series belong to the same group. The effective number of independent examples may be much lower than the document count.

For older scholarship, an additional historical stratum can separate pre-2015 from later writing. The preferred cutoff for research evidence is not automatically a demand that every future exemplar be pre-2015. Preserve original publication/version dates; today's edited documentation and sitemap last-modified dates do not prove historical wording.

### Minimum acquisition record

Each record needs a stable corpus ID; original/canonical URL and version; title/author/outlet; publication date and retrieval date; family/form candidates with rationale; section and whole-versus-excerpt unit; subject and intended audience; notability signal with source/date/window; rights and access status; selection/exclusion reason; duplicate/series/project group; and annotation/review status. Missing values remain missing rather than inferred from reach, outlet prestige, or word length.

For citation discovery, compare within field and publication era, inspect the actual work type and retraction/correction state, and save the ranking snapshot. For readership, use only a publisher's identified window/platform. For awards, resolve the underlying works in a portfolio. Avoid using long lists from one author's website as a substitute for independent breadth.

### What we can compute after screening

The [nine accepted feature groups](../accepted-foundations.md) remain the measurement framework. Compute counts only after selecting complete body text and section boundaries. Report observed minimum/maximum, median, central quantiles and distribution shape with sample size, source mix and parser version. Separate abstract/body/references, list/code blocks and quoted passages. Treat word length as descriptive; audience knowledge needs more than a readability formula.

Function labels and source/evidence relationships need annotation and review. Existing move/argument/discourse schemes supply coding precedents. Pilot their crosswalk to our functions on real examples, permit mixed/unresolved labels, and assess boundary and label disagreements separately. A dataset's existing genre label is not a ground-truth label for our paragraph functions.

Compare **within-form variation** against differences between proposed forms after accounting for topic/audience/source. Retain multiple successful patterns if the distribution is multimodal. Test useful distinctions on held-out authors/outlets. A predictive difference is grounds for a profile; it does not establish that its mean causes quality. Observed ranges, working targets and declared limits retain their distinct meanings.

## Search and evidence limits

Research agents used primary publisher records, institutional/author manuscripts, official corpus documentation, original papers, official standards and official source repositories. Representative search concepts included genre-family classification, genre/register research reviews, move-analysis methodological reviews, metadiscourse metareviews, review typologies, API-documentation systematic mappings, README content categorization, weblog genre analysis, web-register corpora, argument/discourse annotation, and notable nonfiction archives. Discovery was followed by targeted source inspection and local inventory counting.

This was parallel scoping research with a shared project question, not independent blinded reviews of the same complete search. No exhaustive database export, duplicate-screening ledger, pooled effect-size analysis or risk-of-bias assessment was completed. The strongest absence statement supported is that this search has not located a transferable synthesis of quality-conditioned paragraph distributions across professional personal essays and independent explainers. Gaps in our map remain search gaps until investigated further.

The [Astra adjudication](astra-scope-adjudication.md) records the independent recommendation for prioritizing extraction and corpus work.
