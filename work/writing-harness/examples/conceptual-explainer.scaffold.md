# Conceptual explainer: question and mechanism

Status: proposed scaffold for testing. No corpus-derived numeric ranges have been assigned. Labels and instructions below describe the writing task; they are not proposed published headings or boilerplate sentences.

Job: identify the subject and the explanatory promise.

Write: [title derived from this piece's actual question and contribution]

## Opening: establish the question

Job: give the reader a concrete reason to want the explanation.

Use: [the author's supplied situation, observation, or question]

Develop: [enough context to recognize the problem] → [the question the piece addresses]

Connect to: the concept or relationship the reader needs first.

Size: use the selected pattern's working allocation once resolved. The observed corpus range remains a separate comparison.

## Necessary concepts: prepare the explanation

Job: introduce only the concepts needed to follow the mechanism.

Use: [source-bound definitions and distinctions]

Develop: [concept] → [its meaning here] → [its relationship to the explanation]

Realization: embed a definition near its first use or develop a separate passage when that helps. Multiple concepts need not receive identical paragraph structures.

## Mechanism: show how the relationship works

Job: make the important steps, interactions, or causal relationships recoverable.

Use: [the author's explanation and supporting material]

Develop: [relevant actor or element] → [action or relationship] → [supported consequence]

Realization: repeat for the dependencies the reader needs; a move can occupy part of a paragraph or several paragraphs.

## Worked example: make the mechanism inspectable

Job: apply the explanation to a concrete instance.

Use: [the supplied example, with its actual evidential status]

Develop: [starting situation] → [how the mechanism applies] → [what the result demonstrates]

Placement: integrate the example with the mechanism or give it a separate passage, according to the selected pattern. Do not repeat the same explanatory work solely to fill both labels.

## Qualification: establish the boundary

Job: show where the explanation changes, stops applying, or leaves uncertainty.

Use: [supported conditions, exceptions, and limits]

Develop: [the condition] → [how it changes the claim or its application]

Placement: keep qualifications with the claims they limit; add a separate limitations passage only when it serves a distinct purpose.

## Ending: develop the useful implication

Job: finish with the understanding the explanation has earned.

Use: [the contribution now established]

Write: [a supported implication or the final explanatory step that completes the reader's question]

Realization: the final substantive paragraph may already perform this job.

## Review assignments

- Mechanical: check the resolved length requirements, links, exact protected terms, and any declared structure constraints using the appropriate counter and parser.
- Editorial: recover the mechanism from the actual prose; examine missing connections, unnecessary repetition, and the usefulness of the example.
- Evidence: check support, attribution, qualifications, and the distinction between observation and illustration.
- Author: judge whether the explanation develops the intended insight and retains the author's expression.
